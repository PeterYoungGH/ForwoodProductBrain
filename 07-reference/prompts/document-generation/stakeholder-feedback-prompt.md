# Stakeholder Feedback Generation Prompt

## Use Case

Use this prompt when generating product team viewpoints for stakeholders on specific topics or issues. This prompt synthesizes research, frameworks, strategic context, and product knowledge to form a coherent, evidence-based viewpoint.

## Context

This prompt guides the creation of stakeholder feedback documents that present the product team's understanding and recommendations. The document should be:
- Evidence-based (grounded in research and insights)
- Strategically aligned (consistent with vision and focus areas)
- Framework-informed (uses established methodologies)
- Actionable (provides clear recommendations)

## Prompt Text

Generate a stakeholder feedback document following this structure:

### 1. Document Header

Include:
- **Topic**: The specific topic or issue being addressed
- **Date**: Date of document creation (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Audience**: Target audience (e.g., "Executive Team", "Stakeholders", "Leadership")
- **Prepared By**: Product Team

### 2. Executive Summary

Provide:
- **Purpose**: Why this feedback is being provided
- **Key Points**: 2-3 bullet points summarizing the main understanding and recommendations
- **Context**: Brief context about why this topic matters now

### 3. Product Team's Understanding of the Problem

This section should synthesize research, insights, and context to present a clear understanding:

#### Problem Statement
- **Clear problem statement**: What is the core problem or issue?
- **Why it matters**: Why is this important to address?
- **Current state**: What is the current situation?

#### Evidence and Research
- **Research findings**: What does our research tell us about this problem?
- **Customer insights**: What have we learned from customers?
- **Market context**: What is the market situation?
- **Data and metrics**: What quantitative evidence supports this understanding?
- **Link to sources**: Reference specific research documents, insights, and data sources

#### Context and Background
- **Historical context**: How did we get here?
- **Related issues**: What other problems or opportunities relate to this?
- **Stakeholder perspectives**: What are different perspectives on this issue?

#### Strategic Alignment
- **Strategic focus**: How does this relate to our strategic focus areas?
- **Vision alignment**: How does this connect to our vision?
- **Business outcomes**: What business outcomes are affected?
- **Product outcomes**: What product outcomes are affected?

### 4. Product Team's Recommendations

This section should provide clear, actionable recommendations:

#### Recommended Approach
- **Primary recommendation**: What is the product team's primary recommendation?
- **Alternative approaches**: What alternatives were considered?
- **Rationale**: Why is this the recommended approach?

#### Strategic Rationale
- **Strategic alignment**: How does this recommendation align with strategic focus?
- **Framework application**: What frameworks or methodologies inform this recommendation?
- **Risk assessment**: What risks are considered and how are they addressed?
- **Expected outcomes**: What outcomes do we expect from this recommendation?

#### Implementation Considerations
- **Feasibility**: Is this recommendation feasible?
- **Resources required**: What resources would be needed?
- **Timeline**: What is a realistic timeline?
- **Dependencies**: What dependencies exist?
- **Success criteria**: How will we measure success?

#### Related Opportunities and Solutions
- **Opportunities**: Link to related opportunities this recommendation addresses
- **Solutions**: Link to related solutions or initiatives
- **Experiments**: Link to related experiments or validation approaches

### 5. Supporting Evidence

Provide:
- **Research references**: Links to relevant research documents
- **Insight references**: Links to relevant insights
- **Framework references**: Links to relevant methodologies and frameworks
- **Data sources**: References to data and metrics
- **Customer feedback**: References to customer input

### 6. Strategic Context

Include:
- **Strategic focus areas**: How this relates to current strategic focus
- **Vision connection**: How this connects to our vision
- **Roadmap alignment**: How this fits with current roadmap
- **Business outcomes**: Related business outcomes
- **Product outcomes**: Related product outcomes

### 7. Related Documents

Link to:
- Related research documents
- Related insights
- Related opportunities and solutions
- Related initiatives
- Related frameworks and methodologies
- Use relative paths for internal links

### 8. Next Steps

List:
- **Immediate actions**: What should happen next?
- **Decisions needed**: What decisions are required?
- **Follow-up**: What follow-up is needed?

## Analysis Guidelines

When generating the document, follow these guidelines:

### Synthesizing Information

1. **Start with Research**: Ground the understanding in research and insights
2. **Apply Frameworks**: Use relevant frameworks to structure thinking
3. **Align with Strategy**: Ensure recommendations align with strategic focus
4. **Consider Outcomes**: Connect to business and product outcomes
5. **Be Evidence-Based**: Support all claims with evidence

### Problem Understanding

1. **Be Specific**: Clearly define the problem
2. **Provide Context**: Explain why it matters
3. **Use Evidence**: Support with research and data
4. **Show Alignment**: Connect to strategic context
5. **Acknowledge Complexity**: Recognize multiple perspectives

### Recommendations

1. **Be Clear**: Provide specific, actionable recommendations
2. **Explain Rationale**: Explain why this is the recommended approach
3. **Consider Alternatives**: Acknowledge other options
4. **Address Risks**: Consider and address risks
5. **Link to Outcomes**: Connect recommendations to expected outcomes

### Language and Tone

1. **Stakeholder-Friendly**: Use clear, accessible language
2. **Professional**: Maintain professional tone
3. **Confident but Humble**: Express confidence while acknowledging uncertainty
4. **Action-Oriented**: Focus on actionable insights
5. **Evidence-Based**: Ground statements in evidence

## Parameters

- `topic`: The specific topic or issue to address
- `audience`: Target audience for the feedback
- `context`: Additional context or constraints
- `research_sources`: Relevant research documents and insights
- `strategic_context`: Strategic focus areas and vision
- `frameworks`: Relevant frameworks and methodologies
- `outcomes`: Related business and product outcomes

## Expected Output

A complete stakeholder feedback document that:
- Presents a clear understanding of the problem (evidence-based)
- Provides actionable recommendations (strategically aligned)
- Links to supporting research and frameworks
- Uses stakeholder-friendly language
- Follows the template structure
- Includes proper markdown formatting and links
