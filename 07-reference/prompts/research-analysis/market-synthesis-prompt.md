# Market Synthesis Prompt

## Use Case

Use this prompt when synthesizing market research, competitive analysis, industry reports, or multiple market intelligence sources.

## Context

This prompt guides the synthesis of market intelligence from multiple sources to create a comprehensive understanding of market trends, competitive positioning, and opportunities.

## Prompt Text

Synthesize market intelligence following this structure:

### 1. Market Overview

Synthesize:
- **Market size and growth**: Current market size, growth rate (CAGR), projected size
- **Market drivers**: What is driving market growth or change?
- **Market segments**: Key segments, verticals, or customer types
- **Geographic trends**: Regional variations or opportunities

### 2. Competitive Landscape

Analyze:
- **Key competitors**: Who are the main players and how are they positioned?
- **Competitive strengths**: What do competitors do well?
- **Competitive gaps**: Where are competitors weak or missing capabilities?
- **Market positioning**: How do competitors position themselves?
- **Differentiation opportunities**: Where can we differentiate?

### 3. Customer Needs and Pain Points

Identify:
- **Primary customer needs**: What are customers trying to achieve?
- **Pain points**: What problems are customers experiencing?
- **Buying triggers**: What causes customers to seek solutions?
- **Evaluation criteria**: What do customers value when selecting solutions?

### 4. Technology and Innovation Trends

Synthesize:
- **Technology trends**: What technologies are emerging or becoming important?
- **Innovation patterns**: How is the market innovating?
- **Integration expectations**: What integrations or capabilities are expected?
- **Future outlook**: Where is the market heading in 3-5 years?

### 5. Market Opportunities

Identify:
- **Market gaps**: Where are there unmet needs or underserved segments?
- **Growth opportunities**: Where can we grow market share?
- **Regional opportunities**: Geographic opportunities
- **Adjacent markets**: Related markets we could enter

### 6. Implications for Product Strategy

Synthesize:
- **Product implications**: What does this mean for product features or capabilities?
- **Strategic implications**: What does this mean for product strategy or positioning?
- **Go-to-market implications**: What does this mean for sales, marketing, or partnerships?

## Parameters

- `source_materials`: List of market research documents, competitive analyses, or industry reports
- `market_focus`: Specific market or industry focus (optional)
- `geographic_scope`: Geographic scope of analysis (optional)

## Expected Output

A comprehensive market research document that includes:
- Market overview with size, growth, and drivers
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Customer needs and pain points
- Technology and innovation trends
- Market opportunities
- Strategic implications and recommendations
- Source references and citations
