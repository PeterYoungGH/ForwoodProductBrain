# Research Analysis Prompts

Prompts for analyzing research, extracting insights, and synthesizing market intelligence.

## Purpose

These prompts guide the analysis of research materials, customer feedback, market data, and competitive intelligence to extract actionable insights.

## Available Prompts

### Insight Extraction Prompt
**File**: `insight-extraction-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Extracting key insights from research documents, customer feedback, or market data
**Related Commands**: `extract-insights`, `process-inbox-todo`

Extracts insights including:
- Key learnings and findings
- Patterns and trends
- Implications for product strategy
- Action items and recommendations

### Market Synthesis Prompt
**File**: `market-synthesis-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Synthesizing market research, competitive analysis, or industry reports
**Related Commands**: `synthesize-research`, `process-inbox-todo`

Synthesizes market intelligence:
- Market trends and drivers
- Competitive positioning
- Customer needs and pain points
- Market opportunities

### Customer Feedback Analysis Prompt
**File**: `customer-feedback-analysis-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Analyzing customer interviews, surveys, or feedback
**Related Commands**: `extract-insights`, `process-inbox-todo`

Analyzes customer feedback to identify:
- Jobs to be done
- Pain points and friction
- Feature requests and needs
- Satisfaction drivers

### JIRA Acceptance Criteria Extraction Prompt
**File**: `jira-acceptance-criteria-extraction-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Extracting acceptance criteria from JIRA issues and their children for QA/UAT testing
**Related Commands**: `extract-jira-acceptance-criteria`

Extracts acceptance criteria including:
- All acceptance criteria from parent issue
- Recursive extraction from all child issues (any depth)
- Organized by issue hierarchy and categories
- Testing information fields (Tester, Test Date)

## Usage Pattern

1. Read research source material
2. Apply appropriate analysis prompt (insight extraction, market synthesis, or customer feedback)
3. Generate structured insights document
4. Link insights to related opportunities or research documents
