# Decision Framework Prompts

Prompts for applying decision frameworks and methodologies to product decisions.

## Purpose

These prompts guide the application of decision frameworks (OST, risk assessment, etc.) to product strategy and development decisions.

## Available Prompts

### OST Application Prompt
**File**: `ost-application-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Applying the Opportunity Solution Tree framework to structure product work
**Related Commands**: `apply-decision-framework`, `identify-opportunities`

Applies OST framework by:
- Mapping business outcomes to product outcomes
- Identifying opportunities from outcomes
- Structuring solutions under opportunities
- Organizing experiments under solutions

### Risk Assessment Prompt
**File**: `risk-assessment-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Assessing risks using frameworks like Marty Cagan's Four Risks
**Related Commands**: `apply-decision-framework`

Assesses risks by:
- Evaluating value risk (will customers buy it?)
- Evaluating usability risk (can users figure it out?)
- Evaluating feasibility risk (can we build it?)
- Evaluating business viability risk (does it work for our business?)

## Usage Pattern

1. Identify decision or question to address
2. Select appropriate framework (OST, risk assessment, etc.)
3. Apply framework prompt
4. Generate structured analysis
5. Document findings and recommendations
