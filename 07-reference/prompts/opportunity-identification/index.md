# Opportunity Identification Prompts

Prompts for identifying, framing, and structuring opportunities within the Opportunity Solution Tree framework.

## Purpose

These prompts guide the identification and structuring of opportunities from insights, research, and customer needs.

## Available Prompts

### Opportunity Framing Prompt
**File**: `opportunity-framing-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Identifying and framing new opportunities from insights or research
**Related Commands**: `identify-opportunities`, `process-inbox-todo`

Frames opportunities by:
- Identifying customer needs and pain points
- Mapping to business and product outcomes
- Defining opportunity statements
- Linking to related opportunities

### Solution Mapping Prompt
**File**: `solution-mapping-prompt.md`
**Use When**: Mapping solutions to opportunities within the OST framework
**Related Commands**: `identify-opportunities`

Maps solutions by:
- Identifying potential solutions for opportunities
- Evaluating solution feasibility
- Linking solutions to opportunities
- Structuring solution documents

## Usage Pattern

1. Review insights or research findings
2. Apply opportunity framing prompt to identify opportunities
3. Map opportunities to business/product outcomes
4. Apply solution mapping prompt to identify potential solutions
5. Create opportunity and solution documents
