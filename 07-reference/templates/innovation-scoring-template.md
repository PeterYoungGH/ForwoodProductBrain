# Innovation Scoring Template

**Purpose:** Score innovation opportunities using the Innovation Decision Framework to inform roadmap prioritization and initiative creation.

## Scoring Framework

Score each innovation opportunity on four weighted dimensions:
1. **Revenue Potential** (1-5)
2. **Reach & Impact** (1-5)
3. **Buy-In & Feasibility** (1-5)
4. **Execution Difficulty** (1-5)

## Usage Notes

1. **Score Honestly:** Be realistic about scores - inflated scores lead to poor decisions
2. **Use Evidence:** Base scores on evidence, not assumptions
3. **Consider Context:** Scores are one factor - consider strategic alignment and portfolio balance
4. **Update Regularly:** Reassess scores as new information becomes available
5. **Moonshot vs Small Bet:** Classify appropriately - moonshots need clear validation pathway
