# Opportunity Scoring Template

**Purpose:** Calculate opportunity scores using the Jobs to Be Done (JTBD) framework to inform prioritization decisions.

## Scoring Formula

```
Opportunity Score = Importance × (10 − Satisfaction)
```

Where:
- **Importance**: How important is this outcome to customers? (1-10 scale)
- **Satisfaction**: How satisfied are customers currently? (1-10 scale)

**High Score (50+):**
- High importance, low satisfaction
- Strong opportunity to address
- Consider for Active status

**Medium Score (25-49):**
- Moderate importance or moderate satisfaction
- Good opportunity, but not urgent
- Consider for Backlog or Active depending on context

**Low Score (<25):**
- Low importance or high satisfaction
- Weak opportunity
- Consider for Backlog or Archive

## Usage Notes

1. **Gather Evidence First:** Before scoring, ensure you have evidence for both importance and satisfaction
2. **Use Customer Research:** Base scores on actual customer research, not assumptions
3. **Update Regularly:** Reassess scores as new evidence becomes available
4. **Consider Context:** Scores are one factor in prioritization - consider strategic alignment and resources
