# Innovation Decision Framework

A comprehensive, repeatable framework for ideating, reviewing, ranking, and prioritising innovation projects across AI, data, hardware, product, and operational innovation streams.

---

## 1. Purpose & Operating Approach

### Purpose

Create clear alignment across the leadership trio on:

- High‑impact moonshots that can redefine Forwood's competitive advantage.
- Smaller bets that support continuous improvement and learning.
- A consistent decision-making framework for assessing and prioritising innovation opportunities.

### Operating Approach

- Shift from foundational build-out → future-focused innovation planning.
- Begin with divergent ideation, exploring opportunities across all innovation domains.
- Progress to structured convergence, using a shared prioritisation model.
- Align as a trio before extending discussions to broader teams or executives.

---

## 2. Innovation Domains & Strategic Alignment

Innovation initiatives should align with the core mission and reinforce Forwood's strategic pillars.

### Mission

Become the global gold standard in fatality prevention, across mining, construction, utilities, and industrial sectors.

### Innovation Domains

- **AI & ML Innovation** – risk detection, predictive analytics, coaching, content generation.
- **Data Innovation** – benchmarking, safety insights, aggregation, quality and trust layers.
- **Hardware Innovation** – sensors, wearables, edge devices, field-based automation.
- **Platform & Product Innovation** – CRM+, Safety+, integrations, workflows, UI/UX.
- **Process & Industry Model Innovation** – pricing, usage, data commercialisation, partner ecosystems.

### Strategic Pillars

Each innovation initiative must reinforce at least one pillar:

- Ease of Use
- SIF Prevention
- Adoption & Engagement
- Data & Insights

## 3. The Innovation Decision Framework

### Evaluation Criteria (Core Scoring Model)

Every idea is assessed on four weighted dimensions:

1. **Revenue Potential** – Can this materially contribute to ARR, retention, or expansion?
2. **Reach & Impact** – Number of customers affected + magnitude of SIF prevention impact.
3. **Buy‑In & Feasibility** – Cross-functional appetite, technical viability, risk profile.
4. **Execution Difficulty** – Time, effort, skills, and dependencies required.

Each dimension can be scored on a 1–5 scale, producing an overall prioritisation score.

### Supporting Factors

Optional qualitative factors that influence sequencing:

- Differentiation advantage
- Time-to-learn (how quickly it produces insights or validation)
- Regulatory or ESG alignment
- Strategic brand impact
- Customer pull vs Forwood push

---

## 4. Portfolio Structure: Moonshots vs Small Bets

### Moonshots (Big Swings)

- Transformational opportunities
- Require longer timelines, new capabilities, or new business models
- Must have a clear validation pathway (POC → pilot → MVP → scale)
- Must demonstrate potential for step-change improvements in fatality prevention or growth

### Small Bets (Incremental Innovation)

- Fast-learning, low-cost experiments
- Improve customer value, automation, or operational efficiency
- Should have a rapid validation cycle and measurable outcomes

All innovation streams feed into one portfolio, ensuring balance across long-term bets and short-term wins.

---

## 5. AI & Data Innovation Guardrails

### Principles

- **Protect customers:** All data must remain aggregated, anonymised, and permissioned.
- **Do no harm:** Innovations must reduce risk, not introduce new forms of risk.
- **Deliver measurable value:** AI must materially improve safety, productivity, or insight.
- **Ensure explainability:** AI outputs must be auditable and trustworthy.

### High-Value Opportunity Spaces

- AI-driven risk identification & prediction
- Control verification & assurance automation
- Behavioural coaching
- Cross-site benchmarking & industry insights
- Safety content generation and contextualisation
- Data quality improvement loops

---

## 6. Innovation Review & Prioritisation Process

### 1. Ideation (Divergent)

Monthly or quarterly brainstorming across AI, data, hardware, product, and workflow domains. Capture ideas in a consolidated repository.

### 2. Framing (Clarification)

Each idea must have a clear problem statement, target user, value proposition, and success measure.

### 3. Scoring (Structured Evaluation)

Apply the 4‑criteria scoring model + optional qualitative factors.

### 4. Portfolio Mapping (Visualisation)

Plot ideas on:

- Impact vs Effort Matrix
- Time-to-Value Map
- Moonshot vs Small Bet categories

### 5. Prioritisation (Convergence)

Rank initiatives by:

- Total score
- Strategic significance
- Portfolio balance

### 6. Alignment & Resourcing

Confirm ownership, timelines, required skills, and cross-functional dependencies.

### 7. Governance & Review

- Quarterly review of the full innovation portfolio
- Monthly review of in-flight pilots and experiments
- Fast feedback loops; kill or pivot underperforming initiatives

---

## 7. 12-Month Innovation Roadmap Structure

A standardised structure for planning forward:

- OKRs tied to innovation goals
- Strategic narratives for major bets
- Clear milestone progression (POC → MVP → Migration → Enterprise Scale)
- Cross-functional resource plan
- Quarter-by-quarter sequencing

- Which growth and GTM models unlock the next stage of scaling?
- Where should AI and data automation create the most customer value first?
- What hardware and on-site innovations meaningfully accelerate control verification and risk detection?

## 9. Innovation Opportunities Catalog

This section catalogs specific innovation opportunities across all domains. These opportunities should be evaluated using the framework above and categorized as Moonshots or Small Bets.

### AI & ML Innovation Opportunities

**AI Opportunities:**
- AI Risk ID – Automated risk identification using AI/ML
- AI Control Verification – Automated control verification and assurance
- AI Coaching – AI-powered behavioral coaching and guidance
- AI Data Insights – AI-driven analytics and insights generation
- Content generation (e.g., TBCCC content) – AI-generated safety content

**Core Considerations:**
- Accuracy – AI outputs must be accurate and reliable
- Trust – Users must trust AI recommendations
- Bias – Must avoid bias in AI models
- Safety implications – AI must enhance safety, not introduce new risks

**Growth Loop Ideas Enabled by AI:**
- Free content input → paid insights
- Public fatality-prevention GPT
- Forwood app in the GPT Store

### Data Innovation Opportunities

**Data-Driven Opportunities:**
- Maturity model & benchmarking – Self-assessment tool on website, full assessment in person, publish benchmarking data
- Safety insights & aggregation via user action loops
- Marketplace for content/data templates
- Recognition & award programs
- Insurance-linked data insights / incentives
- Owner company ↔ contracting company alignment via shared CRM data

**Data-Powered Growth Loops:**
- Feature usage limits / credits to drive expansion
- If 90% workforce adopts by X months → reduce fees (behavioural loop)
- Freemium or insights-based free tiers
- Demo accounts with dummy data

### Hardware Innovation Opportunities

**AR / VR / Hardware Use Cases:**
- Training (VR/AR simulation)
- Risk ID (e.g., vision-assisted tools, AR overlays)
- Control Verification (field capture using edge devices / wearables)

### Platform & Product Innovation Opportunities

**Product-Led Growth Motions:**
- Product-led growth philosophy (product = marketing channel)
- Free trial / Freemium / self-onboarding paths
- Demo accounts with dummy data
- Marketplace for apps, plugins, content, templates
- Templates discoverable on Google (e.g., TBCCC)
- Gamification with real-world rewards
- Tie usage & adoption to premium features and upsell paths
- Integration-driven expansion loops (CRM+, Safety+ ecosystem growth)

### Distribution & Growth Opportunities

**Commercial & Industry Motions:**
- Pricing tied to adoption targets (e.g., 90% workforce → discount)
- Data commercialisation options (paid insights, benchmarks)
- Partner & consultant channels with revenue share
- Certifications and courses to drive ecosystem uptake
- Insurance premium incentives linked to risk maturity
- Loop between owner operators ↔ contractors to drive CRM adoption

**Distribution & Growth Loops (General):**
- Interactive Demos
- Create a proper upsell path via feature-set
- Product = marketing channel
- Prefer loops over funnels
- Word of mouth / virality
- Template discoverability (Canva / SafetyCulture style)
- Data loop (Dropbox-style)
- Free Gift → Create Attention
- Product for Prospects → Create Trust
- Distribution Growth Loop: Core Product Offer → Create Transformation → Upsell Pathway → Referral System

## Usage Notes

**This framework is used for:**
- Triage of new ideas that might be "out there" or hovering in our brains
- Evaluating innovation opportunities that don't fit standard roadmap prioritisation
- Assessing moonshot vs incremental innovation opportunities

**This framework is NOT used for:**
- Scoring the entire roadmap
- Standard product feature prioritisation
- Routine product development decisions
