### Concentric Circles Release Framework

I was also thinking about a video I saw yesterday by Peter Yang, who's a very experienced product manager at Roblox. One of the things he suggested was shipping in concentric circles, which he described as launching internally first, then to beta customers, and then eventually going to general availability with new products and features. This is something I feel very aligned with.

I think I'd like to think about formalising this somehow—specifically defining who is in each of those circles for us. I'm thinking about creating a framework in the Product Brain file that outlines this, and then pushing that into Confluence as a documented process or procedure. I think the first thing that's important is just the general principle of concentric releases: shipping to small groups first, and then progressively larger groups as you move toward general availability.

I think this idea is reasonably understood across the organisation, but what isn't well understood is who is actually in those groups and why they're in those groups—who we are actually shipping to at each stage. Straight off the bat, I think the inner group should definitely include Sales and the Pre-Sales team. I also think anyone in the product team who isn't intimately involved in building the product should be in that group as well. Beyond that, I think we want to identify, probably on a case-by-case basis, representatives from other departments who would be affected by the release.

### Checklist to Form Workflow Insight

One other observation I had yesterday was when Xantos was trying to find his checklist. He had built a checklist and then was looking to preview it, and what he actually did was go into the module itself where the forms live. I think that opens up an insight for us. I think we need a workflow that, when you publish a checklist, allows you to either add it to a new form or add it to an existing form directly from the checklist library. If you choose one of those options, the navigation should then take you straight to that form. I think that would be a really helpful way to lay out the process more clearly for people.
