### Thread Discussion with Warwick, Ashley, and Product Team

**Participants:** @warwick.mcintosh, @Ashley Walker, @Sean, @lori.watt, @peter.young

#### Initial Message from Peter (5:53 PM)

Following discussion after UAT session, capturing where we landed and agreed approach.

**Key Points:**
- Technical release work still ongoing, having material impact on product team capacity
- Product team assisting with continued QA and triaging UAT sessions through various challenges
- Engineering assessment shows significant portion of release has not met technical release completion criteria
- Level of regressions and quality issues higher than expected
- Reference: [V1 Engineering Verification Status](https://forwood.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EAP/pages/3393912835/V1+Engineering+Verification+Status)

**UAT Status:**
- Three sessions completed to date
- First two sessions impacted - had to be stopped or left incomplete due to major platform issues
- Third session completed but continues to surface substantial usability and quality gaps
- UAT taking more time and effort than originally planned due to platform and technical release issues

**Impact on V2:**
- Product team heavily focused on:
  - Supporting UAT
  - Identifying and prioritising issues
  - Working closely with engineering on remediation of regressions and significant usability issues
- V2 detailed scoping work (planned to run in parallel with UAT) cannot proceed as scheduled
- **Agreed: V2 detailed scoping deferred until at least 19 January, potentially later**
- This will push back start of V2 development
- Will have downstream impacts on V2 delivery timelines and commitments to EDP

**Context:**
- Engineering team put in significant effort under challenging circumstances
- Volume and nature of issues was not anticipated
- Gap between 23 December technical release completion signal and actual readiness has had real downstream effects:
  - UAT effectiveness
  - Product team capacity

**Next Steps:**
- Remain committed to V1 launch to sales on 19 January
- Product capacity prioritised toward V1 quality and launch readiness
- Cannot progress V2 scoping in parallel
- Should make broader Upgrade planning group aware of situation to reset expectations
- Once V1 stable, reassess V2 start dates, revise delivery timelines, communicate updated commitments to EDP
- **Request: Prioritise messaging this with migration team tomorrow**

**Important Note:**
"it is important we provide the team the agreed time to do the detailed V2 scoping rather than expecting them to deal with the delays in V1 and simultaneously complete the detailed scope for V2 features."

#### Response from Warwick (7:43 AM)

**Key Points:**
- Acknowledges regressions have had real impact on schedule
- Agrees immediate focus should be on resolving issues to support sales from 19 January

**Future Cadence Expectation:**
- Work packages and PRDs should be prepared ahead of time and aligned to technical release cycles
- Even allowing for regression impact, should aim for smooth flow of work from one version to next
- Should avoid periods where engineering has limited forward work during UAT
- Encourages working closely with @Ashley Walker and Meagan to align on next steps and sequencing

#### Follow-up from Peter (8:01 AM)

**Question:** Who communicates timeline impact to migration group?

**Key Points to Communicate:**
1. **Technical Release Status:**
   - Technical Release is not complete and ongoing
   - Significant quality and regression issues

2. **Product Team Capacity:**
   - Product team capacity utilised assisting engineering team
   - Identifying issues through QA and continual feedback of bugs/issues
   - Significant investment of time impacting preparation of detailed V2 scope

3. **Recommendation:**
   - Given delays and technical issues, product team will need until **23rd Jan** to deliver initial detailed feature scopes/designs/PRDs for V2
   - Engineering should factor this into their delivery dates
   - Update migration planning group

4. **Important Note:**
   - Product team using capacity to ensure still on track for Sales onboarding after 19th Jan
   - Ensuring as many serious quality issues that would affect this process are remediated

**Re: DoR for Product Discovery:**
- This is exactly the plan
- Impact of regressions and general platform issues on capacity is the core issue being flagged
- To keep to process and ensure packages, specs, PRDs prepared ahead of time, product team needs to use capacity on those activities
- **Not** on QA and bug hunting work supporting engineering team
- Team has pitched in and done a lot of testing to help technical release
- **This has come at the cost of focus on V2 detailed scope**

**Core Issue:**
"We are getting stuck reporting obvious and quite serious issues with the product and platform that really should be picked up by the engineering team beforehand. We have done this to help our ultimate goals, but the cost of this is that our capacity of our team has been pulled away from the preparatory activities."
