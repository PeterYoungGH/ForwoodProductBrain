## Key Messages

### Mission Zero
Our Mission Zero is to discover and eliminate serious injury and fatality risks (SIF) in high risk industries. Like you, we believe that life is fragile and precious. The loss of just one worker diminishes us all.

### Success Metrics
- **99.6% Success Rate** after full implementation of Forwood CRM
- **1000+ Sites** across the world trust Forwood CRM
- **ICMM Case Study (2012-2020):** Companies without Forwood CRM: 791 fatalities | Companies with Forwood CRM: 0 fatalities

### Customer Success Stories

**Rio Tinto:**
- Six consecutive years fatality free globally
- 2019 marked their first fatality-free year in 147 years

**Newcrest:**
- Seven consecutive years fatality free to deployed sites

**HEDNO (Greece):**
- Full implementation led to fewer disruptions, zero fatalities, and a stronger safety-first culture
- A nationwide cultural and safety transformation

### Four Reasons Why Forwood CRM Is Uniquely Effective

1. **CRM+ AI-enhanced software**
2. **Patent-protected Triple Cross Check methodology**
3. **100 years of content and fatality research**
4. **Over 200 million verified data points**

### CRM+ Platform (December 2025 Release)

The First Fatality Prevention Platform Created by AI:
- One connected ecosystem
- Exceptional UX
- Multilingual support
- Configurable workflows
- AI-powered intelligence
- Seamless transitions from legacy systems

### The Accident Learning System (ALS)

A patented system that updates checklists in real time after a fatality or serious incident.

**Kinross Example:** A worker died in a fuel vapour explosion inside a shipping container. The ALS identified a missing gas-test question and updated the Hot Work checklist globally and instantly.

**ALS Workflow:**
1. Incident occurs
2. Investigation
3. Learning review
4. If a question is missing or unclear, CRM updates immediately

### Global Workplace Fatality Problem

- 330,000 die annually from workplace accidents
- 2.6 million die from work-related causes
- ILO 2023: Nearly three million workers die annually from work-related accidents and diseases
- A system like CRM is required to prevent SIF incidents

### Future Potential

Forwood is expanding beyond mining into:
- Oil & Gas
- Chemicals
- Construction
- Forestry
- Utilities

Any high-risk industry, any country, any language.
