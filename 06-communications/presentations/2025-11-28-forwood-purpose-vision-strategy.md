## Our Purpose

**Why we exist:**  
We partner with organizations to sustainably eradicate workplace fatalities and serious injuries.

### Purpose Explained

At Forwood, we are united by a single, unwavering focus: to eliminate workplace fatalities and serious injuries.

Through our industry-leading SaaS platform, we empower organizations to take control of critical risks and ensure every worker returns home safely. We believe safety isn't a priority—it's a non-negotiable commitment.

Our solutions combine cutting-edge technology, deep industry expertise, and purpose-built tools designed specifically for fatality prevention. We don't just support safety; we help make zero harm a reality.

Our goal is simple and powerful: **Zero fatalities. Zero serious injuries. Because every life matters.**

---

## Our Vision

To be the global leader in preventing Serious Injuries and Fatalities (SIFs) in high-risk industries—setting the global standard for Critical Risk Management (CRM).

---

## Our Strategy

Our strategy is purpose-built to achieve our vision and is anchored in clear, focused priorities across each of our key result areas.

### Commercial Strategy

Reach $100M in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) in Phase One by executing a high-growth commercial approach, strengthening customer retention, and delivering exceptional value with operational simplicity.

### Business Strategy

We will dominate the mining sector as the leading name in Critical Risk Management. Once established, we will expand into utilities and construction, followed by a strategic move into other high-risk industries where our impact is needed most.

### Investor Strategy

Our Risk+ pricing model offers a compelling, low-cost entry point that accelerates contract acquisition while creating a seamless upsell pathway to full CRM adoption. We will start with Macquarie Asset Managers (MAM) and replicate this strategy across other Asset Management Companies around the world to drive value and growth.

### Partner Strategy

We will work closely with global consultancies like Deloitte, Accenture, and EY, as well as targeted niche partners, to scale our impact and deliver exceptional customer outcomes.

- **Short-term goal (FY2025):** Partners generate 30% of total revenue
- **Long-term goal (2025–2027):** Partners drive 50% of total revenue

### Customer Strategy

We deliver an intuitive, integrated Ecosystem of Solutions that deliver demonstrable value to customers. Our products are designed to integrate effortlessly into existing systems and reporting tools, enabling seamless adoption and real-time risk control.

### Revenue Recognition Strategy

All contracts are structured with full ARR paid on day one of service delivery. This ensures immediate revenue recognition, avoids delayed implementations, and aligns ARR closely with actual cash flow and Annual Contract Value (ACV).

### Product Strategy

We provide a single, unified platform with multiple supporting solutions—all purpose-built, all unique, all adding tremendous value to customers—all focused on the elimination of Serious Injuries and Fatalities in high-risk industries.

Our flagship CRM solution, supported by a growing Ecosystem of complementary tools, is intuitive, scalable, and easy to implement across all levels of an organization. We are committed to ongoing innovation to ensure Forwood remains the most effective and widely adopted CRM platform in high-risk industries.

### Finance Strategy

We will maintain financial discipline while scaling sustainably. By aligning investments to strategic priorities, optimizing pricing models, and enforcing proactive revenue recognition, we will ensure strong cash flow and predictable growth. Our approach will drive investor confidence, maximize profitability, and support global expansion—all while maintaining operational excellence.

### People Strategy

Our mission-driven culture will attract, develop, and retain top talent who share our passion for eliminating workplace harm. We are building a high-performance environment rooted in agility, accountability, and continuous learning. We will invest in leadership development, optimize our workforce, and foster deep engagement across all levels. By embedding our purpose into everything we do, we create a workplace where people feel empowered, inspired, and proud to make a difference.

## A Final Word

Our purpose, vision, and strategy are more than words—they are our blueprint for impact. Together, we are creating a safer world, one solution, one partnership, and one life at a time.

**Forwood leads the growing global movement to eliminate workplace fatalities.**
