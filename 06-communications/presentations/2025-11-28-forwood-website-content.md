## Mission and Vision

Forwood's vision is encapsulated in **"Mission Zero"** — eliminating fatalities, serious injuries, and catastrophic events from the workplace.

For over **15 years**, the company has partnered with organizations worldwide to make this mission a reality, with implementations across **1,000+ operating sites in 60 countries**.

## Core Solutions

### CRM+ (Critical Risk Management)

**Purpose:** Eliminate serious injuries and fatalities.

**Features:**
- Proven fatality elimination capability
- Field-tested critical control verifications (10+ years)
- Patented 3-tier methodology integrating operator, supervisor, and manager levels

### RISK+

**Comprehensive risk management solution.**

**Features:**
- Enterprise Risk Register
- Bow-tie visualization for cause-and-control mapping
- Links between risks, controls, and operational data

### Additional Platforms

- **Tailings+** — Accelerates tailings facility audits to meet global compliance standards
- **Safety+** — Manages critical control verifications, incident reporting, and safety conversations
- **Compliance+** — Tracks multi-standard audit compliance and simplifies governance reporting
- **Vision+** — AI-powered behavioral monitoring tool that identifies unsafe acts and links them to critical controls
- **ESG+** — Monitors adherence to sustainability and ESG frameworks globally
