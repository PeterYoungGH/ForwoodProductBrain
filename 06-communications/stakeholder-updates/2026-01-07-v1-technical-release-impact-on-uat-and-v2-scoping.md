# Message to Warwick: V1 Technical Release Impact on UAT and V2 Scoping

**Date:** 2026-01-07  
**To:** Warwick  
**From:** Product Team

---

Hi Warwick,

I wanted to flag something with you that we need to share with the broader Upgrade/Migration Planning team. The ongoing technical release work is impacting our product team capacity in ways that will delay our V2 detailed scoping work, and this will have downstream impacts on V2 delivery timelines and our commitments to EDP.

## The Situation

As we discussed today, the technical release work is still ongoing. The engineering team completed their assessment, and it shows that 9 features haven't met technical release completion criteria, with only 4-5 features meeting the criteria. That's about a 30-35% completion rate against release standards. The engineering team is working hard to address these gaps, but the volume of regressions and quality issues has been greater than anyone anticipated.

On the UAT side, we've completed three sessions so far. The first two had to be stopped early due to major platform issues, and while the third completed, we're still finding significant usability and quality issues. We're continuing UAT with Angelina and Steve, but it's identifying a lot more issues than we expected.

## The Impact

This is consuming a lot of product team resources. We're spending time supporting UAT, helping identify and prioritize quality gaps, and working with engineering on remediation. The two-week UAT process we planned is being extended because of the volume of issues we're finding.

The problem is that we had planned to do detailed V2 feature scoping during these two weeks in parallel with UAT. That's not happening now. We can't start the V2 detailed scoping until at least January 19, and it might be later depending on how things go with the technical release stabilization and UAT completion.

This delay will push back when we can start V2 feature development, which will impact our V2 delivery timelines. And if that impacts engineering's ability to start V2 work, that will impact the commitments we've made to EDP.

## Important Context

I want to be clear—this isn't about blaming anyone. The engineering team has done really good work pulling this together under challenging circumstances. They didn't anticipate the volume of regressions and issues that have emerged, and they're working hard to address them. But the reality is that when engineering communicated technical release was complete on December 23, there was still significant work outstanding. That timing mismatch has had real downstream consequences—delayed UAT effectiveness, inability to run clean QA on other areas, and now product team resources being consumed by remediation work.

## What This Means

We're still committed to the V1 launch to sales, targeting January 19 as planned. But that commitment means we're deploying product team resources to V1 quality and launch readiness that were originally allocated to V2 scoping work. We can't do both at the same time.

So the bottom line is:
- V2 detailed scoping can't start until at least January 19, possibly later
- This will impact V2 delivery timelines
- This will impact our commitments to EDP
- We need to flag this with the broader Upgrade/Migration Planning team

## What We Need

I think we need to share this situation with the broader team so everyone has visibility. The engineering team will need to formally carry forward and own the impact assessment and recovery planning for the technical release delays. Once V1 launch is stable, we'll reassess when we can actually start V2 scoping and revise our delivery timelines accordingly. We'll need to communicate those revised timelines to EDP and other stakeholders.

I know you might be resistant to sharing this broadly, but I think it's important that the timing mismatch between stated release completion and actual readiness is clearly documented, along with its downstream consequences. We need everyone to understand the reality of the situation so we can align on revised expectations.

Happy to discuss this further if you want to talk through how we frame it or who needs to be informed.

Thanks,  
Product Team
