# Email to Ashley and Meagan: V2 Product DoR Date Change Request

**Date:** 2026-01-08  
**To:** Ashley Walker, Meagan  
**From:** Product Team  
**Subject:** V2 Product DoR Date Change: 19 January → 23 January

---

Hi Ashley and Meagan,

I wanted to reach out to request a change to the Product Definition of Ready (DoR) date for the first few V2 features. We need to move the date from 19 January to 23 January to deliver the initial detailed feature scopes, designs, and PRDs.

The features we aim to deliver detailed scope for on 23 January are:
- https://forwood.atlassian.net/browse/FPM-527
- https://forwood.atlassian.net/browse/FPM-881
- https://forwood.atlassian.net/browse/FPM-889
- https://forwood.atlassian.net/browse/FPM-884

This delay is directly related to the ongoing V1 technical release work and its impact on product team capacity. Engineering should factor this into their delivery dates and we'll need to update the migration planning group accordingly.

The V1 technical release work is still ongoing. Engineering's assessment shows that a significant portion of the V1 release hasn't yet met technical release completion criteria. We're seeing about a 30-35% completion rate against release standards. The volume of regressions and quality issues has been significantly higher than anticipated. You can see the full engineering assessment here: https://forwood.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EAP/pages/3393912835/V1+Engineering+Verification+Status

We've completed five UAT sessions to date. Four out of five sessions have had to stop or be significantly impacted due to major platform issues from the V1 technical release, and we've had to finish sessions before our scripts were fully complete. The UAT process is taking significantly more time and effort than originally planned.

To support the V1 technical release and ensure quality for the upcoming sales onboarding, the product team has been working closely with engineering during UAT, helping identify and prioritize issues as they arise, doing extensive QA and testing work that would normally be handled by engineering's QA processes, and collaborating on remediation. This collaboration has been essential to progress, but it has meant dedicating substantial product team capacity to work that goes beyond our normal scope. We believe this is the right trade-off to achieve our shared goals, but the cost is that our team's capacity has been pulled away from V2 scoping work.

We remain committed to the V1 launch to sales on 19 January, and the product team is using its capacity to ensure we're still on track for the sales onboarding. This is the right priority, but it means we can't progress V2 scoping in parallel. Given the delays in the V1 technical release, the product team will need until 23 January to deliver the initial detailed feature scopes, designs, and PRDs for V2.

This means engineering should factor this date change into their delivery dates for V2 features. We'll need to update the migration planning group with this revised timeline, which will have downstream impacts on V2 delivery timelines and our commitments to EDP.

I know this isn't ideal, and I want to acknowledge that the engineering team has put in significant effort under challenging circumstances. The volume and nature of the issues wasn't anticipated by anyone. We need to be realistic about what's possible given the current situation, and we need to provide the product team the agreed time to do the detailed V2 scoping properly.

I'm happy to discuss this further if you have questions about the timeline or approach.

Thanks,  
Peter
