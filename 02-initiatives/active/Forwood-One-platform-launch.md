## Goals and Success Metrics

### Primary Goals
- Launch Forwood One platform and win 5 new deals (new logos)
- Build and execute upgrade plans for Safety+, CRM+, and Risk+ customers
- Achieve product excellence through design system, usability, and customer experience
- Establish AI-driven product discovery and design workflow

### Success Metrics
- **New Customer Deals:** 5 new logos (target)
- **Forwood One NPS Score:** 20 from initial customers (target)
- **Usability Testing Success Rate:** ≥90% task success rate across critical user flows
- **Customer Migration:** All Safety+ customers transitioned to Forwood One in FY26
- **Sales Pipeline:** >15 deals in Forwood One pipeline by December 2025

## Scope

### In Scope
- Forwood One platform development and launch
- Customer migration from Safety+, CRM+, and Risk+ platforms
- Design system delivery
- Usability improvements
- Customer onboarding and support experience
- AI-driven product discovery workflow
- Strategic integration across CRM, Risk, and Compliance pillars

### Out of Scope
- Legacy platform maintenance (beyond migration support)
- Features not required for launch or migration

## Risks and Mitigation

- **Risk 1:** Balancing rapid customer acquisition with complex migration - Mitigation: Careful planning, phased approach, strong collaboration
- **Risk 2:** Team capacity and capability gaps - Mitigation: Focus on team capability development, identify and resource key gaps
- **Risk 3:** Customer migration challenges - Mitigation: Build comprehensive upgrade plans, ensure positive migration experience
- **Risk 4:** Delivery velocity and quality - Mitigation: Focus on prioritization, advocate for delivery velocity and quality

## Customer Feedback & Validation

### Early Adopter Engagement (December 2025)

**Rossing Meeting (2025-12-16):**
- **Customer:** Rossing (early adopter of SafetyPlus, long-term partner)
- **Outcome:** Strong enthusiasm for platform consolidation, improved integration, and simplified IT management
- **Key Feedback:**
  - Platform consolidation aligns with internal IT strategy (highlighted as major benefit)
  - Migration from previously integrated systems makes this evolution particularly valuable
  - Positive reception of migration approach (minimal client involvement, short downtime)
- **Customer Questions Identified:** 16 specific questions across platform structure, AI security, migration process, and commercial terms
  - **Action Required:** Create comprehensive customer-facing documentation addressing all questions
  - **Priority:** One-page platform overview, FAQ document, migration process guide
- **Timeline Milestones from Customer Engagement:**
  - **Early–Mid January 2026:** Customer-facing presentation to be shared (overview of full product suite, roadmap, migration options)
  - **Late January 2026:** Roadmap and migration planning discussions (target week of 19 January, proposed date: 23 January)
  - **Target Migration Window:** Around Q3 (calendar year), subject to planning and readiness

## Notes

Bringing Forwood One to market within 12 months will require balancing rapid customer acquisition with the complex migration of existing clients. We must accelerate discovery and delivery quality while empowering the team to achieve outsized impact through collaboration, culture, upskilling and AI-enabled tools. Embedding a culture of product discovery focused on deeply understanding industry problems, not just requested solutions, will be key to success.

**Customer Communication Priority:** Early adopter feedback (Rossing) indicates strong need for comprehensive customer-facing documentation, particularly:
- One-page platform overview showing all products and modules
- Detailed FAQ addressing platform structure, AI security, migration process, and commercial terms
- Migration process guide with step-by-step expectations
- Clear AI security and data protection messaging
