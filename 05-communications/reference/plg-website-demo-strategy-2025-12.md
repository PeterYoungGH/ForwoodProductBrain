# PLG Website Demo Strategy

**Date:** 2025-12-17  
**Type:** Marketing Strategy / Communication Framework  
**Status:** Active

## Overview

Strategy document for implementing product-led growth (PLG) through an interactive website demo of Forwood One, enabling lead generation and reducing customer acquisition costs.

## Strategic Context

### Product-Led Growth Philosophy

**Core Principle:** Product = marketing channel

The product itself becomes a marketing channel, enabling prospects to experience value before committing to a sales conversation. This reduces friction, improves lead quality, and creates scalable lead generation.

### Why This Matters

- Reduces customer acquisition costs
- Generates qualified leads through self-service exploration
- Creates scalable, always-on lead generation channel
- Enables prospects to experience value before sales engagement
- Supports Forwood One platform launch and new customer acquisition goals

## Strategy Components

### 1. Interactive Demo Experience

**Goal:** Enable prospects to explore Forwood One's capabilities without commitment

**Components:**
- Live, interactive demo environment (not just video or screenshots)
- Pre-populated with realistic dummy data for safety-critical workflows
- Accessible directly from website without registration barriers
- Guided tour or self-exploration options
- Lead capture mechanism integrated into demo experience

### 2. Lead Generation Mechanism

**Goal:** Convert engaged prospects into qualified leads

**Components:**
- Lead capture forms at key interaction points
- Progressive profiling (collect information as engagement deepens)
- CRM integration for lead management
- Qualification criteria based on demo engagement depth
- Follow-up automation for engaged prospects

### 3. Analytics and Optimization

**Goal:** Measure effectiveness and optimize conversion

**Components:**
- Track demo engagement metrics (time spent, features explored, interactions)
- Measure conversion rates (demo → lead → sales conversation)
- Analyze prospect behavior patterns
- A/B test different demo experiences
- Optimize based on data insights

## Implementation Approach

### Phase 1: Demo Environment Setup
- Create demo-specific Forwood One instance
- Populate with realistic dummy data
- Configure demo workflows and use cases
- Ensure demo environment stability and performance

### Phase 2: Website Integration
- Integrate demo into website
- Design user experience for demo access
- Implement lead capture mechanisms
- Set up analytics tracking

### Phase 3: Launch and Iterate
- Launch demo on website
- Monitor engagement and conversion metrics
- Gather feedback from prospects and sales team
- Iterate based on data and feedback

## Success Metrics

### Lead Generation Metrics
- Number of prospects engaging with demo
- Demo engagement depth (time, features explored)
- Lead conversion rate (demo → lead)
- Qualified lead rate (lead → sales conversation)
- Sales conversion rate (qualified lead → customer)

### Business Impact Metrics
- Customer acquisition cost reduction
- Lead quality improvement
- Sales cycle time reduction
- New customer acquisition from demo leads

## Messaging Framework

### For Website Visitors
- "Experience Forwood One - Try it yourself"
- "Explore our platform with a live demo"
- "See how Forwood One can transform your safety operations"
- "No commitment required - explore at your own pace"

### For Sales Team
- Demo engagement data provides qualification signals
- Engaged prospects are more qualified leads
- Demo reduces need for initial discovery calls
- Demo enables sales team to focus on closing, not educating
