# Strategic Themes Recommendations: VoC Alignment

**Date:** 2026-01-07  
**Purpose:** Recommendations for updating strategic themes to align with Voice of Customer research and industry trends  
**Source:** Voice of Customer Research, Strategic Roadmap Analysis

---

## Executive Summary

The current strategic themes are **solution/technology-focused** (AI, Integration, UX, Data, Platform), while the Voice of Customer research reveals **industry issues and trends** that should drive our strategic vision. This document provides two recommendation sets:

1. **Radical Update** - Complete restructuring of strategic themes around industry issues and customer problems
2. **Minor Update** - Incremental changes to existing themes, incorporating VoC insights

---

## Current Strategic Themes Analysis

### Current Themes (Technology/Solution-Focused)

**A. AI & Predictive Safety** - Technology capability  
**B. Integration & Interoperability** - Technology capability  
**C. Modern User Experience** - Technology capability  
**D. Data, Benchmarking & Insights** - Technology capability  
**E. Scalable, Future-Proof Platform** - Technology capability

### Gap Analysis

**What's Missing:**
- **Industry problem focus** - Themes don't address the core industry issues customers face
- **Customer pain points** - No explicit connection to VoC themes (accountability without authority, safety theatre, credibility erosion, etc.)
- **Strategic outcomes** - Focus on capabilities rather than customer transformation
- **Market differentiation** - Doesn't leverage Forwood's unique position in solving industry problems

**What's Good:**
- Clear technology investments
- Scalable platform foundation
- Modern UX focus aligns with adoption needs
- AI/Data capabilities support predictive safety

## Recommendation 1: Radical Update

### New Strategic Themes (Industry Issue-Focused)

Restructure themes around **industry problems and customer outcomes** rather than technology capabilities.

#### Theme 1: Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance
**Vision:** Make fatal risk as visible and actionable as production metrics, enabling executives to govern SIF prevention with the same clarity they manage business performance.

**Why This Matters (VoC Evidence):**
- **Executive Theme:** "Traditional Safety Metrics Mask Fatal Risk Exposure" - Green dashboards, red reality
- **Executive Theme:** "SIF Risk Has Become Board-Owned Governance Obligation" - Delegation is not a defence
- **Primary Persona Pain:** "Accountability Without Authority" - Need tools that make fatal risk visible to those who have authority
- **Customer Theme:** "Fatality Prevention is the Organising Principle" - Not zero harm, but fatality prevention

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Executive dashboards that surface fatal risk signals (not just lagging indicators)
- Real-time SIF risk visibility across operations, sites, and contractors
- Governance tools that enable board-level oversight of critical controls
- Predictive insights that highlight rising SIF potential before incidents occur
- Integration with business systems to make safety risk visible alongside production metrics

**Desired Outcome:** Executives can see and govern fatal risk with the same clarity as production metrics  
**Business Impact:** Reduced SIF exposure, improved executive confidence, competitive differentiation  
**Why Now:** Regulatory pressure (industrial manslaughter laws), board-level accountability, customer demand for governance tools

---

#### Theme 2: From Data Collection to Action
**Vision:** Transform safety from data collection and reporting into systems that drive visible action and prevent harm.

**Why This Matters (VoC Evidence):**
- **Primary Persona Pain:** "Drowning in Data, Starving for Action" - Overloaded with performative reporting
- **Market Theme:** "Reporting Goes Into a Black Hole" - Reports disappear with no visible follow-through
- **Customer Theme:** "Data becomes useful" - "getting that data in one place and getting it out and making it useful, is the advantage"
- **Primary Persona Pain:** "Safety Theatre vs Real Risk" - Systems that look good but don't prevent harm

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- AI-powered action recommendation engine (not just analytics)
- Automated control gap detection and closure workflows
- Visible action tracking and follow-through (not just dashboards)
- Data that connects directly to operational decisions
- Systems that celebrate issues and drive learning (not hide problems)

**Desired Outcome:** Every data point drives visible action; reporting becomes a trust mechanism, not a black hole  
**Business Impact:** Increased credibility, better safety outcomes, reduced EHS burnout  
**Why Now:** Market fatigue with performative dashboards, customer demand for action-oriented systems

---

#### Theme 3: Credibility & Trust Through Systems
**Vision:** Position safety professionals as enabling partners, not compliance cops, through systems that build trust and credibility.

**Why This Matters (VoC Evidence):**
- **Primary Persona Pain:** "Credibility Erosion - Seen as Police, Not Partners" - Workers don't trust them
- **Customer Theme:** "Credibility Depends on Visible, Consistent Leadership" - Walking the talk
- **Customer Theme:** "Non-Blame Culture Enables Truth" - People want to contribute when safe to do so
- **Executive Theme:** "Leadership Credibility Directly Affects Risk Exposure" - Signals matter more than statements

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Non-blame reporting and learning systems
- Tools that position safety professionals as enablers (not enforcers)
- Consistent language and frameworks across organizations
- Leadership engagement tools that support visible, consistent behavior
- Systems that reward speaking up and celebrating issues

**Desired Outcome:** Safety professionals regain credibility; workers trust and engage with safety systems  
**Business Impact:** Increased reporting, better safety culture, reduced professional burnout  
**Why Now:** Market distrust of safety systems, customer success with non-blame approaches

---

#### Theme 4: Controls That Actually Work
**Vision:** Enable organizations to implement controls that prevent harm in real conditions, not just satisfy audits.

**Why This Matters (VoC Evidence):**
- **Primary Persona Pain:** "Controls That Satisfy Audits But Don't Prevent Harm" - Moral injury from superficial controls
- **Customer Theme:** "Front-End Planning is Highest-Leverage Investment" - Design out hazards
- **Customer Theme:** "Systems Not People - Human Error is Inevitable" - System design over individual blame
- **Market Theme:** "Safety Systems Exist to Protect the Company, Not Workers" - Need to prove different intent

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Control effectiveness verification (not just documentation)
- Engineering and design-focused risk elimination tools
- Control verification that works in real field conditions
- Systems that help identify and advocate for effective controls
- Evidence-based control selection and verification

**Desired Outcome:** Controls that actually prevent harm, not just satisfy audits  
**Business Impact:** Reduced incidents, improved safety outcomes, reduced moral injury  
**Why Now:** Regulatory focus on "reasonably practicable" and control effectiveness, customer demand for real-world solutions

---

#### Theme 5: Standardization Without Rigidity
**Vision:** Enable consistent, shared understanding of critical risks across sites, contractors, and operations without creating rigid bureaucracy.

**Why This Matters (VoC Evidence):**
- **Market Theme:** "Non-standardisation Inconsistency Creates Real Risk" - Inconsistency is dangerous
- **Customer Theme:** "Consistent language across org" - "same language, behaviour and engagement across the different levels"
- **Market Reality:** Safety becomes constant context-switching and site-specific dialects
- **Customer Success:** Cognitive standardization - people looking for the same things

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Shared critical risk frameworks across sites and contractors
- Consistent language and control expectations
- Tools that reduce context-switching for mobile workers
- Standardization of what matters (fatal risks) without rigid procedures
- Integration capabilities that enable shared systems across operations

**Desired Outcome:** Consistent understanding of critical risks without bureaucratic rigidity  
**Business Impact:** Reduced incidents from inconsistency, improved contractor safety, operational efficiency  
**Why Now:** Market frustration with inconsistency, customer success with shared frameworks

---

### Radical Update: What Changes

**Removed/De-emphasized:**
- **AI & Predictive Safety** → Becomes an enabler within themes (predictive insights in Theme 1, AI action recommendations in Theme 2)
- **Integration & Interoperability** → Becomes an enabler within themes (governance integration in Theme 1, standardization tools in Theme 5)
- **Modern User Experience** → Becomes foundational capability across all themes
- **Data, Benchmarking & Insights** → Becomes enabler within Theme 2 (From Data to Action)
- **Scalable, Future-Proof Platform** → Remains as foundational infrastructure (not a strategic theme)

**Added:**
- **Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance** - New theme addressing executive and board-level needs
- **From Data Collection to Action** - New theme addressing data overload and action gap
- **Credibility & Trust Through Systems** - New theme addressing credibility erosion
- **Controls That Actually Work** - New theme addressing audit vs. effectiveness gap
- **Standardization Without Rigidity** - New theme addressing inconsistency problem

**Strategic Pillars Alignment:**
- **Ease of Use** → Embedded in all themes (foundational)
- **SIF Prevention** → Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility) + Theme 4 (Controls That Work)
- **Adoption & Engagement** → Theme 3 (Credibility & Trust)
- **Data Insights** → Theme 2 (From Data to Action)

## Recommendation 2: Minor Update

### Incremental Changes to Existing Themes

Keep current structure but **reframe and enhance** themes to incorporate VoC insights and industry trends.

#### Theme A: AI & Predictive Safety → **Fatal Risk Intelligence & Prevention**
**Current:** Transform safety management from reactive to predictive through AI-powered insights and automation.

**Updated Vision:** Use AI and predictive capabilities to make fatal risk visible early enough to prevent incidents, giving executives and safety professionals the signals they need to govern SIF prevention effectively.

**Enhanced Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- AI Safety Coach to guide users, analyse patterns, and prompt proactive actions
- **AI-powered fatal risk identification** - Surface rising SIF potential before incidents occur (addresses "green dashboards, red reality")
- **Executive fatal risk dashboards** - Make SIF risk as visible as production metrics (addresses accountability without authority)
- **Predictive control gap detection** - Identify weakening controls before failure (addresses controls that satisfy audits)
- Automated translation and intelligent coaching to support consistent global standards

**Desired Outcome:** Fatal risk becomes visible and actionable before incidents occur  
**Why Now:** Executive need for SIF governance, regulatory pressure, customer demand for predictive safety  
**Business Impact:** Reduced SIF exposure, improved executive confidence, competitive differentiation

---

#### Theme B: Integration & Interoperability → **Integration & Interoperability** (Enhanced)
**Current:** Create a seamless ecosystem where Forwood integrates with all enterprise systems and workflows.

**Updated Vision:** Enable seamless integration that makes safety risk visible alongside business metrics, supports consistent frameworks across sites and contractors, and connects data to operational decisions.

**Enhanced Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- CRM data connects seamlessly with Risk, Safety, and Compliance modules under Forwood One
- **Integration with business systems** - Make safety risk visible alongside production metrics (addresses executive visibility needs)
- **Shared frameworks across sites and contractors** - Enable consistent critical risk understanding (addresses non-standardization)
- APIs and integration services for enterprise systems (HR, asset management, construction planning)
- Real-time data synchronization across platforms
- Third-party marketplace for integrations

**Desired Outcome:** Safety becomes part of operational decision-making, not separate from it  
**Why Now:** Customer migration requires seamless integration; need for consistent frameworks across operations  
**Business Impact:** Reduced implementation time, increased customer stickiness, improved safety outcomes

---

#### Theme C: Modern User Experience → **Credibility-Building User Experience**
**Current:** Deliver the most intuitive, engaging safety platform in the industry.

**Updated Vision:** Design experiences that position safety professionals as enabling partners, build trust with workers, and make safety systems feel helpful rather than bureaucratic.

**Enhanced Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Complete redesign of desktop and mobile experiences
- Best-in-class UI and UX design system
- **Non-blame reporting interfaces** - Design for trust and truth-telling (addresses credibility erosion)
- **Role-specific experiences** - Position safety professionals as enablers, not cops (addresses police vs. partner problem)
- Enhanced offline capability for field workers
- Role-specific dashboards and quick actions
- Streamlined, decluttered workflows designed for simplicity

**Desired Outcome:** Safety systems build credibility and trust, not erode it  
**Why Now:** Market distrust of safety systems, customer success with trust-building approaches  
**Business Impact:** Higher user adoption, improved NPS scores, increased reporting, reduced training time

---

#### Theme D: Data, Benchmarking & Insights → **From Data to Action**

**Updated Vision:** Transform safety from data collection into systems that drive visible action, prevent harm, and connect insights directly to operational decisions.

**Enhanced Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**

- **Visible action tracking** - Show that data leads to action (addresses black hole problem)

- C-suite reporting linking safety outcomes to business performance

**Desired Outcome:** Every data point drives visible action; reporting becomes a trust mechanism  
**Why Now:** Market fatigue with performative dashboards, customer demand for action-oriented systems  
**Business Impact:** Data-driven decision making, competitive differentiation, customer retention, increased credibility

#### Theme E: Scalable, Future-Proof Platform → **Scalable, Future-Proof Platform** (Unchanged)
**Vision:** Build a platform that scales globally and adapts to emerging technologies.

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**

- Global scalability and multi-region deployment
- Platform APIs for ecosystem development

**Desired Outcome:** Reduced time-to-market, lower cost per customer, innovation velocity  
**Why Now:** Current platform limitations constrain growth; need foundation for scale  
**Business Impact:** Reduced time-to-market, lower cost per customer, innovation velocity

## Recommendations Summary

### Recommendation 1: Radical Update (Preferred for Long-Term)

**When to Use:**
- If you want strategic themes to directly address industry problems
- If you want clear differentiation from capability-focused competitors
- If you're willing to invest in change management and reframing
- If you want themes that guide both product and go-to-market strategy

**Benefits:**
- Themes directly address VoC pain points and industry trends
- Clear problem → solution narrative
- Strong market differentiation
- Themes guide both product development and messaging

**Risks:**
- Requires significant reframing of existing work
- May require change management with team
- Less familiar structure (problem-focused vs. capability-focused)

---

### Recommendation 2: Minor Update (Preferred for Incremental Change)

**When to Use:**
- If you want to maintain current structure while incorporating VoC insights
- If you prefer incremental change over radical restructuring
- If you want easier team adoption
- If you want to test VoC alignment before full restructuring

**Benefits:**
- Maintains familiar structure
- Easier team adoption
- Lower implementation risk
- Still incorporates VoC insights

**Risks:**
- Themes remain technology/capability-focused
- Less clear differentiation from competitors
- Requires translation from capabilities to customer problems in messaging
