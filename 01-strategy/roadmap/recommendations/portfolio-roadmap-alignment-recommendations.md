## Recommendation 2: New Q3+ Initiatives Aligned with Strategic Themes

### Proposed Initiatives for Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)

Extend portfolio roadmap to Q3 FY26 (January-March 2027) with new theme-aligned initiatives.

#### Initiative: Executive Fatal Risk Dashboards
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 1 - Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance  
**Timeline:** Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)  
**Status:** Proposed

**Problem Statement:** Executives lack visibility into fatal risk exposure. Traditional safety metrics (TRIFR, LTIFR) mask SIF risk, creating "green dashboards, red reality" where metrics improve while fatal risk exposure remains unchanged.

**Opportunity Summary:** Deliver executive dashboards that surface fatal risk signals (not just lagging indicators), making SIF risk as visible and actionable as production metrics.

**Expected Outcome:** Executives can see and govern fatal risk with the same clarity as production metrics; reduced SIF exposure; improved executive confidence.

**Key Metrics:**
- Executive dashboard adoption rate
- Fatal risk signal quality (vs. noise)
- SIF exposure reduction
- Executive confidence scores

**Key Deliverables:**
- Executive fatal risk dashboard
- Real-time SIF risk visibility across operations
- Governance tools for board-level oversight
- Integration with business systems (safety risk alongside production metrics)

**Strategic Alignment:**
- Addresses VoC: "Traditional Safety Metrics Mask Fatal Risk Exposure"
- Addresses VoC: "SIF Risk Has Become Board-Owned Governance Obligation"
- Addresses VoC: "Accountability Without Authority" (makes fatal risk visible to those with authority)

---

#### Initiative: Action-Oriented Data Systems
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 2 - From Data Collection to Action  
**Timeline:** Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)  
**Status:** Proposed

**Problem Statement:** Safety professionals are drowning in data but starving for action. Reports go into a black hole with no visible follow-through. Dashboards are performative but don't drive decisions.

**Opportunity Summary:** Transform safety from data collection into systems that drive visible action. Every data point should connect to action workflows with visible tracking and follow-through.

**Expected Outcome:** Every data point drives visible action; reporting becomes a trust mechanism, not a black hole; increased credibility; reduced EHS burnout.

**Key Metrics:**
- Action completion rate (from data insights)
- Reporting-to-action time
- Visible follow-through rate
- Reporting rates (trust indicator)

**Key Deliverables:**
- AI-powered action recommendation engine
- Automated control gap detection and closure workflows
- Visible action tracking and follow-through
- Data-to-action connection in dashboards
- Systems that celebrate issues and drive learning

**Strategic Alignment:**
- Addresses VoC: "Drowning in Data, Starving for Action"
- Addresses VoC: "Reporting Goes Into a Black Hole"
- Addresses VoC: "Data becomes useful" (customer success)

---

#### Initiative: Non-Blame Reporting & Learning Systems
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 3 - Credibility & Trust Through Systems  
**Timeline:** Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)  
**Status:** Proposed

**Problem Statement:** Safety professionals have lost credibility - seen as police, not partners. Workers don't trust them. Blame-focused systems suppress reporting and prevent truth-telling.

**Opportunity Summary:** Design systems that position safety professionals as enabling partners, build trust with workers, and enable non-blame learning. Systems that reward speaking up and celebrating issues.

**Expected Outcome:** Safety professionals regain credibility; workers trust and engage with safety systems; increased reporting; better safety culture; reduced professional burnout.

**Key Metrics:**
- Reporting rates (trust indicator)
- User trust scores
- Safety professional credibility metrics
- Non-blame culture indicators

**Key Deliverables:**
- Non-blame reporting interfaces
- Learning systems that celebrate issues
- Tools that position safety professionals as enablers
- Leadership engagement tools for visible, consistent behavior
- Systems that reward speaking up

**Strategic Alignment:**
- Addresses VoC: "Credibility Erosion - Seen as Police, Not Partners"
- Addresses VoC: "Non-Blame Culture Enables Truth"
- Addresses VoC: "Credibility Depends on Visible, Consistent Leadership"

---

#### Initiative: Control Effectiveness Verification
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 4 - Controls That Actually Work  
**Timeline:** Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)  
**Status:** Proposed

**Problem Statement:** Organizations implement controls that satisfy audits but don't prevent harm in real conditions. This creates moral injury for EHS professionals who know controls don't work but are accountable for preventing fatalities.

**Opportunity Summary:** Enable control effectiveness verification (not just documentation). Tools that verify controls work in real field conditions and help identify and advocate for effective controls.

**Expected Outcome:** Controls that actually prevent harm, not just satisfy audits; reduced incidents; improved safety outcomes; reduced moral injury.

**Key Metrics:**
- Control effectiveness verification rate
- Incident reduction (vs. control implementation)
- Audit vs. real-world alignment
- Control failure detection rate

**Key Deliverables:**
- Control effectiveness verification tools
- Engineering and design-focused risk elimination tools
- Control verification in real field conditions
- Systems that help identify and advocate for effective controls
- Evidence-based control selection and verification
- Front-end planning and design-out tools

**Strategic Alignment:**
- Addresses VoC: "Controls That Satisfy Audits But Don't Prevent Harm"
- Addresses VoC: "Front-End Planning is Highest-Leverage Investment"
- Addresses VoC: "Systems Not People - Human Error is Inevitable"

---

#### Initiative: Shared Critical Risk Frameworks
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 5 - Standardization Without Rigidity  
**Timeline:** Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)  
**Status:** Proposed

**Problem Statement:** Non-standardization and inconsistency create real risk. Workers moving across sites experience safety as fragmented, site-specific, and mentally exhausting. Inconsistency is viewed as a direct contributor to incidents and fatalities.

**Opportunity Summary:** Enable consistent, shared understanding of critical risks across sites, contractors, and operations without creating rigid bureaucracy. Standardize what matters (fatal risks) without rigid procedures.

**Expected Outcome:** Consistent understanding of critical risks without bureaucratic rigidity; reduced incidents from inconsistency; improved contractor safety; operational efficiency.

**Key Metrics:**
- Cross-site consistency metrics
- Contractor integration success
- Context-switching reduction
- Standardization adoption rate

**Key Deliverables:**
- Shared critical risk frameworks across sites and contractors
- Consistent language and control expectations
- Tools that reduce context-switching for mobile workers
- Standardization of what matters (fatal risks) without rigid procedures
- Integration capabilities for shared systems across operations
- Automated translation for consistent global standards

**Strategic Alignment:**
- Addresses VoC: "Non-standardisation Inconsistency Creates Real Risk"
- Addresses VoC: "Consistent language across org" (customer success)
- Addresses VoC: Market reality of constant context-switching
