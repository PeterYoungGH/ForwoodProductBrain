# Roadmaps

This directory contains Forwood's roadmaps organized according to our [4-layer roadmapping framework](../../07-reference/methodology/roadmapping-framework.md).

## Roadmap Structure

### 1. Strategic Roadmap (2+ Year Vision + 6-12 Months)
**File:** [`01-strategic-roadmap.md`](./01-strategic-roadmap.md)  
**Update Frequency:** Quarterly (lightly), plus mid-quarter check-ins  
**Purpose:** Long-term vision (2+ years) and strategic themes/bets (6-12 months) - merged into single document

### 2. Portfolio Roadmap (3-6 Months)
**File:** [`02-portfolio-roadmap.md`](./02-portfolio-roadmap.md)  
**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Fortnightly  
**Purpose:** Show how initiatives ladder up to strategy, allocate team focus, define major launch moments

### 3. Delivery Roadmap (Weekly/Ongoing)
**File:** [`03-delivery-roadmap.md`](./03-delivery-roadmap.md)  
**Update Frequency:** Weekly or as releases are prepared  
**Purpose:** Move work into production through rapid, frequent releases

### 4. Public Roadmap (External)
**File:** [`04-public-roadmap.md`](./04-public-roadmap.md)  
**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Quarterly  
**Purpose:** Inform customers about investment areas without overcommitting

## How Roadmaps Connect

```
Strategic Roadmap (2+ years vision + 6-12 months themes/bets)
    ↓
Portfolio Roadmap (3-6 months)
    ↓
Delivery Roadmap (weekly releases)
    ↓
Public Roadmap (external communication)
```
