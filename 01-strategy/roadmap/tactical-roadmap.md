# Tactical Roadmap (12-Month)

**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09  
**Roadmap Period:** FY26 (July 2025 - June 2026)  
**Type:** Strategic Roadmap (6-12 Month Horizon) + Portfolio Roadmap (3-6 Month Horizon)  
**Framework Layers:** Strategic Roadmap + Portfolio Roadmap  
**Update Frequency:** Strategic elements quarterly; Portfolio elements monthly/fortnightly

## Overview

This tactical roadmap provides the detailed 12-month execution plan derived from our [Strategic Roadmap (2-Year Vision)](./strategic-roadmap.md). It includes specific quarterly milestones, deliverables, and tactical initiatives that will drive us toward our strategic goals.

**Note:** This roadmap combines elements of both the **Strategic Roadmap** (6-12 months) and **Portfolio Roadmap** (3-6 months) layers from Forwood's [4-layer roadmapping framework](../../07-reference/methodology/roadmapping-framework.md). As we evolve our roadmap structure, we may separate these into distinct roadmap documents.

## Strategic Alignment

This tactical roadmap supports the following strategic themes:
- **AI & Predictive Safety** - Launch AI features from innovation stream (AI Safety Coach, AI risk identification & control verification, predictive insights, auto translation & coaching)
- **Modern User Experience** - Deliver design system and achieve usability targets (redesigned desktop & mobile app, clean UI/UX, offline capability, role dashboards, quick actions, streamlined workflows)
- **Integration & Interoperability** - Enable customer migration and platform integration (CRM connects with Risk, Safety, Compliance; APIs for HR, asset management, construction planning)
- **Data, Benchmarking & Insights** - Launch analytics and maturity model (Maturity Model, real-time analytics & exec dashboards, benchmarking, C-suite reporting)
- **Scalable Platform** - Complete Forwood One platform launch and migration (cloud-native, modular, configurable; faster innovation; extensible to AI/IoT/vision systems)

## Risks & Mitigation

### Key Risks
- **Timeline pressure:** Bringing Forwood One to market within 12 months requires balancing rapid customer acquisition with complex migration
- **Quality vs. Speed:** Need to maintain delivery quality while accelerating
- **Customer readiness:** Migration depends on customer readiness and support

### Mitigation Strategies
- Accelerate discovery and delivery quality
- Empower team through collaboration, culture, upskilling and AI-enabled tools
- Embed culture of product discovery focused on deeply understanding industry problems
- Phased migration approach with strong customer support

## Notes

- Success depends on accelerating discovery and delivery quality while empowering the team through collaboration, culture, upskilling and AI-enabled tools
- Embedding a culture of product discovery focused on deeply understanding industry problems, not just requested solutions, will be key to success
- Balancing rapid customer acquisition with complex migration of existing clients requires careful planning and execution
