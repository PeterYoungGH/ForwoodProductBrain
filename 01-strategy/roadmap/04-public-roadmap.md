# Public Roadmap

**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09  
**Roadmap Period:** Ongoing  
**Type:** Public Roadmap (External Communication)  
**Framework Layer:** Public Roadmap  
**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Quarterly  
**Responsible:** Senior Product Manager, Chief Product Officer, Product Marketing Manager

## Overview

This public roadmap informs customers about investment areas without overcommitting. It focuses on user outcomes and themes rather than specific features, and only includes items that are stable enough to share publicly.

**Note:** This roadmap is the **Public Roadmap** layer in Forwood's [4-layer roadmapping framework](../../07-reference/methodology/roadmapping-framework.md). It selectively communicates elements of our journey that align with the long-term narrative and are safe to share.

## Themes (User Outcomes Framed)

### Improving Platform Stability and Performance
We're investing in making Forwood One more stable, reliable, and performant so you can focus on safety, not system issues.

### Enhancing User Experience
We're redesigning the platform to be more intuitive, easier to use, and accessible across all devices and roles.

### Enabling Seamless Integration
We're building APIs and integration capabilities so Forwood One works seamlessly with your existing systems and workflows.

### Delivering Predictive Safety Insights
We're developing AI-powered capabilities to help you identify and prevent risks before incidents occur.

### Supporting Mobile and Offline Work
We're enhancing mobile capabilities and offline functionality so field workers can complete critical work regardless of connectivity.
