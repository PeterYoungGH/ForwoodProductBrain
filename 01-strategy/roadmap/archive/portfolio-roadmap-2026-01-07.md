# Portfolio Roadmap (3-6 Months)

**Last Updated:** 2025-12-16  
**Roadmap Period:** Next 3-6 Months (Q1-Q2 FY26)  
**Type:** Portfolio / Product-Area Roadmap (3-6 Month Horizon)  
**Framework Layer:** Portfolio Roadmap  
**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Fortnightly  
**Responsible:** Senior Product Manager in consultation with Engineering Leads

## Overview

This portfolio roadmap shows how initiatives ladder up to our [Strategic Roadmap](./01-strategic-roadmap.md), allocates team focus, and defines major launch moments. It breaks strategic themes into concrete initiatives with problem statements, expected outcomes, and key metrics.

**Note:** This roadmap is the **Portfolio Roadmap** layer in Forwood's [4-layer roadmapping framework](../../07-reference/methodology/roadmapping-framework.md). It translates strategic bets into executable initiatives.

## Now / Next / Later

### Now (Next 3 Months)

**Focus:** Foundation and MVP Launch

#### Initiative: Forwood One Platform Launch
- **Problem Statement:** Need to launch Forwood One platform and achieve commercial traction with new customers while migrating existing Safety+ customers
- **Opportunity Summary:** Unify CRM+, Risk+, Safety+, and Compliance+ into one intelligent system enabling faster, AI-assisted delivery
- **Expected Outcome:** 5 new customer deals, successful Safety+ migrations, platform stability and adoption
- **Key Metrics:** 
  - New customer deals: 5 new logos
  - Sales pipeline: >15 deals by December 2025
  - Migration completion rate
  - Platform NPS score: 20+ from initial customers
- **Status:** Building
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Define and embed agentic product processes
- Launch Forwood One MVP
- Complete UAT and train wider team
- Establish product discovery and delivery cadence

**Milestones:**
- ✅ Forwood One MVP launched
- ✅ Team trained on new platform
- ✅ Product processes embedded

**Customer-Facing Milestones (from early adopter engagement):**
- **Early–Mid January 2026:** Customer-facing presentation to be shared (overview of full product suite under Forwood One, roadmap, migration options)
- **Late January 2026:** Roadmap and migration planning discussions (target week of 19 January, proposed date: 23 January)
- **Q3 2026 (Target):** Customer migration window (subject to planning and readiness)

#### Initiative: Design System and UX Foundation
- **Problem Statement:** Current platform lacks modern, consistent design system limiting user adoption and experience quality
- **Opportunity Summary:** Deliver best-in-class UI/UX design system enabling intuitive, engaging safety platform
- **Expected Outcome:** Improved usability (≥90% task success rate), higher NPS, reduced training time
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Usability testing success rate: ≥90% across critical user flows
  - Design system adoption across platform
  - User satisfaction scores
- **Status:** Building
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Onboard and embed Product Designer
- Launch Forwood One V1 with design system
- Launch Forwood One V2 with enhanced UX
- Implement branding and new platform name

### Next (3-6 Months)

**Focus:** Platform Enhancement and Customer Migration

#### Initiative: Customer Migration Program
- **Problem Statement:** Need to migrate existing Safety+, CRM+, and Risk+ customers to Forwood One without disruption
- **Opportunity Summary:** Enable seamless migration pathway ensuring customer success and retention
- **Expected Outcome:** Successful customer migrations, high retention rate, positive migration experience
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Migration completion rate
  - Customer retention post-migration
  - Migration satisfaction scores
- **Status:** Validating
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Begin migration of Safety+ clients to Forwood One
- Launch Forwood One V3 (ready for CRM client migration)
- Validate Risk+ iframe in Forwood One
- Develop migration playbooks and support processes

#### Initiative: Native App Launch
- **Problem Statement:** Field workers need mobile-first experience optimized for offline use and field conditions
- **Opportunity Summary:** Launch MVP Forwood One Native App enabling field workers to complete verifications and access critical information offline
- **Expected Outcome:** Increased field worker adoption, improved offline capability, enhanced mobile experience
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Native app adoption rate
  - Offline usage patterns
  - Mobile task completion rates
- **Status:** Exploring
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Launch MVP Forwood One Native App
- Enhanced offline capability for field workers
- Mobile-optimized workflows

### Later (6+ Months)

**Focus:** Scale and Innovation

#### Initiative: AI Features from Innovation Stream
- **Problem Statement:** Customers need predictive safety capabilities and AI-assisted workflows to prevent incidents before they occur
- **Opportunity Summary:** Launch AI features including AI Safety Coach, AI risk identification, predictive insights, and automated translation
- **Expected Outcome:** Increased proactive risk management, improved safety outcomes, competitive differentiation
- **Key Metrics:**
  - AI feature adoption rates
  - Predictive accuracy
  - Safety outcome improvements
- **Status:** Exploring
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Launch AI Safety Coach
- AI Risk Identification and Control Verification
- Predictive insights dashboard
- Automated translation and intelligent coaching

## Dependencies & Constraints

### Dependencies
- Product Designer onboarding (Q2 2025) - Required for design system
- Sales pipeline development (Q2-Q3) - Required for customer acquisition
- Customer readiness for migration (Q3) - Required for migration program
- Technical infrastructure for scale - Required for platform stability

### Constraints
- Timeline pressure: Bringing Forwood One to market within 12 months
- Quality vs. Speed: Need to maintain delivery quality while accelerating
- Customer readiness: Migration depends on customer readiness and support
- Team capacity: Balancing rapid customer acquisition with complex migration

## Resourcing Overview

### Team Allocation
- **Product:** Senior Product Manager, Product Designer (Q2+)
- **Engineering:** Engineering teams focused on platform launch, migration, and native app
- **Commercial:** Sales and customer success teams for pipeline and migration support
- **Operations:** Solutions and support teams for migration execution

### Capacity Considerations
- Onboard Product Designer in Q2
- Embed agentic product processes in Q1
- Build team capability throughout the year
- Balance new customer acquisition with migration support
