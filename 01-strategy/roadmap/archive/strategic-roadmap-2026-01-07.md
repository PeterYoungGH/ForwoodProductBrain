# Strategic Roadmap

**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09  
**Roadmap Period:** Jan 2026 - Dec 2027 + Next 6-12 Months  
**Type:** Long-Term Vision & Strategy (2+ Year Horizon) + Strategic Roadmap (6-12 Month Horizon)  
**Framework Layers:** Long-Term Vision (anchors all roadmap layers) + Strategic Roadmap  
**Update Frequency:** Quarterly (lightly), plus mid-quarter check-ins  
**Responsible:** Chief Product Officer (CPO), Senior Product Manager, Product Marketing Manager

## Overview

This strategic roadmap outlines the long-term vision, themes, and major initiatives for Forwood over the next two years, and translates that vision into actionable themes, bets, and areas of focused investment for the next 6-12 months. It provides the high-level direction that guides our tactical planning and execution.

**Note:** This roadmap serves as both the **Long-Term Vision & Strategy** (2+ years) and **Strategic Roadmap** (6-12 months) layers in Forwood's [4-layer roadmapping framework](../../07-reference/methodology/roadmapping-framework.md). It anchors the Portfolio Roadmap (3-6 months) and Delivery Roadmap (weekly releases).

## Strategic Vision

Our mission is to eliminate workplace fatalities and serious injuries. Over the next two years, we will establish Forwood One as the global leader in Critical Risk Management (CRM), setting the standard for SIF prevention in high-risk industries.

## Strategic Themes

### A. AI & Predictive Safety
**Vision:** Transform safety management from reactive to predictive through AI-powered insights and automation.

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- AI Safety Coach to guide users, analyse patterns, and prompt proactive actions
- AI Risk Identification and Control Verification — automated detection of high-risk scenarios
- Predictive insights to highlight rising SIF potential before incidents occur
- Automated translation and intelligent coaching to support consistent global standards

**Desired Outcome:** Increased adoption, engagement, and proactive risk management  
**Why Now:** AI capabilities are maturing and customer demand for predictive safety is growing  
**Business Impact:** Increased adoption, engagement, and proactive risk management

### B. Integration & Interoperability
**Vision:** Create a seamless ecosystem where Forwood integrates with all enterprise systems and workflows.

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- CRM data connects seamlessly with Risk, Safety, and Compliance modules under Forwood One
- APIs and integration services for enterprise systems (HR, asset management, construction planning)
- Real-time data synchronization across platforms
- Third-party marketplace for integrations

**Desired Outcome:** Reduced implementation time, increased customer stickiness, expanded market reach  
**Why Now:** Customer migration requires seamless integration; enterprise customers demand interoperability  
**Business Impact:** Reduced implementation time, increased customer stickiness, expanded market reach

### C. Modern User Experience
**Vision:** Deliver the most intuitive, engaging safety platform in the industry.

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Complete redesign of desktop and mobile experiences
- Best-in-class UI and UX design system
- Enhanced offline capability for field workers
- Role-specific dashboards and quick actions
- Streamlined, decluttered workflows designed for simplicity

**Desired Outcome:** Higher user adoption, improved NPS scores, reduced training time  
**Why Now:** User experience is a key differentiator; current platform needs modernization for scale  
**Business Impact:** Higher user adoption, improved NPS scores, reduced training time

### D. Data, Benchmarking & Insights
**Vision:** Turn data into actionable intelligence that drives safety maturity and business performance.

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Forwood Maturity Model — measure CRM maturity and understand what to do next
- Real-time analytics and executive dashboards
- Benchmarking across projects, business units, and peer networks
- C-suite reporting linking safety outcomes to business performance
- Industry-wide safety intelligence and trends

**Desired Outcome:** Data-driven decision making, competitive differentiation, customer retention  
**Why Now:** Customers need better insights to justify investment; data is a competitive moat  
**Business Impact:** Data-driven decision making, competitive differentiation, customer retention

### E. Scalable, Future-Proof Platform
**Vision:** Build a platform that scales globally and adapts to emerging technologies.

**Strategic Initiatives / Bets:**
- Cloud-native, modular, and configurable architecture
- Faster innovation cycles and shared value across all clients
- Architecture built for extensibility into AI, IoT, and vision-based safety systems
- Global scalability and multi-region deployment
- Platform APIs for ecosystem development

**Desired Outcome:** Reduced time-to-market, lower cost per customer, innovation velocity  
**Why Now:** Current platform limitations constrain growth; need foundation for scale  
**Business Impact:** Reduced time-to-market, lower cost per customer, innovation velocity

## Strategic Pillars

Based on customer feedback and market analysis, our strategy is anchored in four key pillars:

### 1. Ease of Use
Deliver an intuitive, decluttered experience that makes Forwood effortless for every user at every level — operators, supervisors, and managers.

### 2. SIF Prevention
Double down on CRM+; make our methodology and tools even more effective, useful, and powerful; sharpen our tools, workflows, and insights to directly reduce Serious Injuries and Fatalities.

### 3. Adoption & Engagement
Accelerate user uptake through role-based design, gamification, smart onboarding, and in-product coaching.

### 4. Data Insights
Embed intelligence and analytics to turn data into proactive action, predict incidents before they happen, and measure and benchmark your maturity model.

## Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Mining Sector Domination (Year 1-2)
- Establish Forwood One as the leading CRM platform in mining
- Achieve market leadership through product excellence and customer success
- Build reference customers and case studies

### Phase 2: Expansion to Utilities & Construction (Year 2)
- Adapt platform for utilities and construction industries
- Leverage mining success to enter adjacent markets
- Build industry-specific capabilities and partnerships

### Phase 3: Global Expansion (Year 2+)
- Expand into other high-risk industries
- International market entry and localization
- Strategic partnerships with global consultancies

## Commercial Strategy

### Revenue Goals
- **Year 1:** Establish commercial traction with new customer acquisition
- **Year 2:** Scale revenue through expansion and partnerships
- **Long-term:** $100M ARR target

### Customer Strategy
- Migrate existing customers from legacy platforms (Safety+, CRM+, Risk+) to Forwood One
- Acquire new customers through direct sales and partnerships
- Build customer success programs to drive retention and expansion

### Partner Strategy
- Work with global consultancies (Deloitte, Accenture, EY) and niche partners
- **Year 1:** Partners generate 30% of total revenue
- **Year 2:** Partners drive 50% of total revenue

## Success Measures

### Strategic Success Measures (6-12 Months)
**Activation:** User adoption rates, feature usage, platform engagement  
**Retention:** Customer retention rate, expansion revenue, churn reduction  
**ARR:** New customer acquisition, upsell/cross-sell, partner revenue  
**Efficiency:** Time-to-market, cost per customer, delivery velocity  
**Market Expansion:** New industry entry, geographic expansion, partner growth

### Long-Term Success Metrics (2-Year Horizon)

#### Year 1 Metrics
- Number of new Forwood One customers
- Customer migration completion rate
- Platform NPS score
- User adoption and engagement rates
- Migration success criteria achievement (platform readiness, zero operational disruption, client satisfaction)

#### Year 2 Metrics
- ARR growth
- Market share in mining sector
- Partner-driven revenue percentage
- Customer retention and expansion rates
- Commercial migration milestones (80% of Safety+ revenue by Q2 2026, 80% of Risk+/Compliance+ revenue by Q2 2027, 80% of CRM+ revenue by Q4 2027)
- Post-migration performance (95% of client operational activity in Forwood One by end-2027)

## Strategic Risks & Mitigation

### Key Risks
- Market competition and differentiation
- Customer migration complexity
- Technology scalability challenges
- Talent acquisition and retention

### Mitigation Strategies
- Focus on product excellence and customer success
- Phased migration approach with strong support
- Cloud-native architecture and scalable design
- Investment in team capability and culture
