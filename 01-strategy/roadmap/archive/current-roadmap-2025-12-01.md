## High-Level Roadmap / What's Coming

Rather than chasing a rigid list of features, our roadmap represents the areas where we believe we can make the greatest impact in our mission to eliminate serious injuries and fatalities. It's designed as a living framework that evolves as technology and our customers' needs evolve — ensuring that what we build is not only innovative, but genuinely effective in the field.

### A. AI & Predictive Safety
- AI Safety Coach to guide users, analyse patterns, and prompt proactive actions
- Delivering greater adoption and engagement
- AI Risk Identification and Control Verification under development — automated detection of high-risk scenarios and confirmation of control effectiveness
- Predictive insights to highlight rising SIF potential before incidents occur
- Automated translation and intelligent coaching to support consistent global standards

### B. Integration & Interoperability
- CRM data connects seamlessly with Risk, Safety, and Compliance modules under Oculus
- APIs and integration services for enterprise systems (HR, asset management, construction planning)

### C. Modern User Experience
- Redesign of our desktop and mobile experiences
- Clean, best-in-class UI and UX
- Enhanced offline capability
- Role-specific dashboards and quick actions for supervisors, operators, and leaders
- Streamlined, decluttered workflows designed for simplicity and engagement

### D. Data, Benchmarking & Insights
- Launching the Forwood Maturity Model — measure your CRM maturity and understand what to do next
- Real-time analytics and executive dashboards for visibility and assurance
- Benchmarking across projects, business units, and peer networks
- C-suite reporting linking safety outcomes to business performance

### E. Scalable, Future-Proof Platform
- Cloud-native, modular, and configurable — no bespoke builds required
- Enables faster innovation cycles and shared value across all clients
- Architecture built for extensibility into AI, IoT, and vision-based safety systems
