# Portfolio Roadmap

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-07  
**Roadmap Period:** Q1 FY26 - Q3 FY26 (Jan 2026 - Mar 2027)  
**Type:** Portfolio / Product-Area Roadmap (3-12 Month Horizon)  
**Framework Layer:** Portfolio Roadmap  
**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Fortnightly  
**Responsible:** Senior Product Manager in consultation with Engineering Leads

## Overview

This portfolio roadmap shows how initiatives ladder up to our [Strategic Roadmap](./01-strategic-roadmap.md), allocates team focus, and defines major launch moments. It breaks strategic themes into concrete initiatives with problem statements, expected outcomes, and key metrics.

**Note:** This roadmap is the **Portfolio Roadmap** layer in Forwood's [4-layer roadmapping framework](../../07-reference/methodology/roadmapping-framework.md). It translates strategic bets into executable initiatives.

**Strategic Theme Alignment:** This roadmap has been updated to align with the new problem-focused strategic themes. Initiatives are mapped to themes that directly address industry problems and customer outcomes identified in Voice of Customer research.

## Strategic Theme Mapping

All initiatives are aligned with one or more strategic themes:

- **Theme 1: Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance** - Make fatal risk as visible as production metrics
- **Theme 2: From Data Collection to Action** - Transform reporting into visible action
- **Theme 3: Credibility & Trust Through Systems** - Position safety professionals as partners, not cops
- **Theme 4: Controls That Actually Work** - Controls that prevent harm, not just satisfy audits
- **Theme 5: Standardization Without Rigidity** - Consistent understanding without bureaucracy

### Later (6-9 Months) - Q3 FY26 (Oct-Dec 2026)

**Focus:** Strategic Theme Initiatives  
**Status:** ⚠️ **REFRAMED - AI Features initiative split into theme-aligned initiatives**

#### Initiative: Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance (Theme 1)
- **Problem Statement:** Executives lack visibility into fatal risk exposure. Traditional safety metrics (TRIFR, LTIFR) mask SIF risk, creating "green dashboards, red reality" where metrics improve while fatal risk exposure remains unchanged. EHS professionals have accountability without authority - they need tools that make fatal risk visible to those who have authority.
- **Opportunity Summary:** Deliver executive dashboards that surface fatal risk signals (not just lagging indicators), making SIF risk as visible and actionable as production metrics. Enable board-level governance of critical controls.
- **Strategic Theme Alignment:**
  - **Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance):** Direct alignment
  - Addresses VoC: "Traditional Safety Metrics Mask Fatal Risk Exposure"
  - Addresses VoC: "SIF Risk Has Become Board-Owned Governance Obligation"
  - Addresses VoC: "Accountability Without Authority"
- **Expected Outcome:** Executives can see and govern fatal risk with the same clarity as production metrics; reduced SIF exposure; improved executive confidence
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Executive dashboard adoption rate
  - Fatal risk signal quality (vs. noise)
  - SIF exposure reduction
  - Executive confidence scores
- **Status:** Exploring
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Executive fatal risk dashboard
- Real-time SIF risk visibility across operations, sites, and contractors
- Governance tools for board-level oversight of critical controls
- Predictive insights that highlight rising SIF potential before incidents occur
- Integration with business systems (safety risk alongside production metrics)

---

#### Initiative: From Data Collection to Action (Theme 2)
- **Problem Statement:** Safety professionals are drowning in data but starving for action. Reports go into a black hole with no visible follow-through. Dashboards are performative but don't drive decisions. This creates burnout and erodes credibility.
- **Opportunity Summary:** Transform safety from data collection into systems that drive visible action. Every data point should connect to action workflows with visible tracking and follow-through. Systems that celebrate issues and drive learning, not hide problems.
- **Strategic Theme Alignment:**
  - **Theme 2 (From Data Collection to Action):** Direct alignment
  - Addresses VoC: "Drowning in Data, Starving for Action"
  - Addresses VoC: "Reporting Goes Into a Black Hole"
  - Addresses VoC: "Data becomes useful" (customer success)
- **Expected Outcome:** Every data point drives visible action; reporting becomes a trust mechanism, not a black hole; increased credibility; reduced EHS burnout
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Action completion rate (from data insights)
  - Reporting-to-action time
  - Visible follow-through rate
  - Reporting rates (trust indicator)
- **Status:** Exploring
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- AI-powered action recommendation engine (not just analytics)
- Automated control gap detection and closure workflows
- Visible action tracking and follow-through (not just dashboards)
- Data-to-action connection in dashboards
- Systems that celebrate issues and drive learning (not hide problems)

---

#### Initiative: Credibility & Trust Through Systems (Theme 3)
- **Problem Statement:** Safety professionals have lost credibility - seen as police, not partners. Workers don't trust them. Blame-focused systems suppress reporting and prevent truth-telling. This erodes safety culture and increases risk exposure.
- **Opportunity Summary:** Design systems that position safety professionals as enabling partners, build trust with workers, and enable non-blame learning. Systems that reward speaking up and celebrating issues. Leadership engagement tools that support visible, consistent behavior.
- **Strategic Theme Alignment:**
  - **Theme 3 (Credibility & Trust Through Systems):** Direct alignment
  - Addresses VoC: "Credibility Erosion - Seen as Police, Not Partners"
  - Addresses VoC: "Non-Blame Culture Enables Truth"
  - Addresses VoC: "Credibility Depends on Visible, Consistent Leadership"
- **Expected Outcome:** Safety professionals regain credibility; workers trust and engage with safety systems; increased reporting; better safety culture; reduced professional burnout
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Reporting rates (trust indicator)
  - User trust scores
  - Safety professional credibility metrics
  - Non-blame culture indicators
- **Status:** Exploring
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Non-blame reporting interfaces
- Learning systems that celebrate issues
- Tools that position safety professionals as enablers (not enforcers)
- Leadership engagement tools for visible, consistent behavior
- Systems that reward speaking up
- Consistent language and frameworks across organizations

### Future (9-12 Months) - Q3 FY26 (Jan-Mar 2027)

**Focus:** Advanced Strategic Theme Initiatives  
**Status:** ✅ **NEW - Theme-aligned initiatives**

#### Initiative: Controls That Actually Work (Theme 4)
- **Problem Statement:** Organizations implement controls that satisfy audits but don't prevent harm in real conditions. This creates moral injury for EHS professionals who know controls don't work but are accountable for preventing fatalities. Controls are often superficial (administrative controls for fatal risks) when engineering solutions are needed.
- **Opportunity Summary:** Enable control effectiveness verification (not just documentation). Tools that verify controls work in real field conditions and help identify and advocate for effective controls. Support front-end planning and design-out approaches that eliminate risk at the source.
- **Strategic Theme Alignment:**
  - **Theme 4 (Controls That Actually Work):** Direct alignment
  - Addresses VoC: "Controls That Satisfy Audits But Don't Prevent Harm"
  - Addresses VoC: "Front-End Planning is Highest-Leverage Investment"
  - Addresses VoC: "Systems Not People - Human Error is Inevitable"
- **Expected Outcome:** Controls that actually prevent harm, not just satisfy audits; reduced incidents; improved safety outcomes; reduced moral injury
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Control effectiveness verification rate
  - Incident reduction (vs. control implementation)
  - Audit vs. real-world alignment
  - Control failure detection rate
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Control effectiveness verification tools
- Engineering and design-focused risk elimination tools
- Control verification in real field conditions
- Systems that help identify and advocate for effective controls
- Evidence-based control selection and verification
- Front-end planning and design-out tools

---

#### Initiative: Standardization Without Rigidity (Theme 5)
- **Problem Statement:** Non-standardization and inconsistency create real risk. Workers moving across sites experience safety as fragmented, site-specific, and mentally exhausting. Inconsistency is viewed as a direct contributor to incidents and fatalities. Safety becomes constant context-switching and site-specific dialects.
- **Opportunity Summary:** Enable consistent, shared understanding of critical risks across sites, contractors, and operations without creating rigid bureaucracy. Standardize what matters (fatal risks) without rigid procedures. Tools that reduce context-switching for mobile workers.
- **Strategic Theme Alignment:**
  - **Theme 5 (Standardization Without Rigidity):** Direct alignment
  - Addresses VoC: "Non-standardisation Inconsistency Creates Real Risk"
  - Addresses VoC: "Consistent language across org" (customer success)
  - Addresses VoC: Market reality of constant context-switching
- **Expected Outcome:** Consistent understanding of critical risks without bureaucratic rigidity; reduced incidents from inconsistency; improved contractor safety; operational efficiency
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Cross-site consistency metrics
  - Contractor integration success
  - Context-switching reduction
  - Standardization adoption rate
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](../../02-initiatives/active/Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

**Key Deliverables:**
- Shared critical risk frameworks across sites and contractors
- Consistent language and control expectations
- Tools that reduce context-switching for mobile workers
- Standardization of what matters (fatal risks) without rigid procedures
- Integration capabilities for shared systems across operations
- Automated translation for consistent global standards

## Dependencies & Constraints

### Dependencies
- Product Designer onboarding (Q2 2025) - Required for design system
- Sales pipeline development (Q2-Q3) - Required for customer acquisition
- Customer readiness for migration (Q3) - Required for migration program
- Technical infrastructure for scale - Required for platform stability
- Platform foundation (Q1-Q2) - Required for Q3+ theme-aligned initiatives

### Constraints
- Timeline pressure: Bringing Forwood One to market within 12 months
- Quality vs. Speed: Need to maintain delivery quality while accelerating
- Customer readiness: Migration depends on customer readiness and support
- Team capacity: Balancing rapid customer acquisition with complex migration
- Strategic theme alignment: Q3+ initiatives must align with problem-focused themes
