## Slide 5: Theme 1 - Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance
**Headline:** Theme 1: Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance

**Vision Statement:**
Make fatal risk as visible and actionable as production metrics

**Key Initiatives:**
- Executive dashboards with fatal risk signals
- Real-time SIF risk visibility
- Governance tools for board-level oversight
- Predictive insights before incidents occur
- AI-powered risk identification

**Why Now:**
- Regulatory pressure (industrial manslaughter laws)
- Board-level accountability
- Customer demand for governance tools

---

- Market fatigue with performative dashboards

## Design Recommendations

**Color Scheme:**
- Primary: Professional blue/green (safety/trust)
- Accent: Orange/red (for risk/urgency where appropriate)
- Neutral: Gray scale for text and backgrounds

**Visual Style:**
- Clean, modern, professional
- Use icons and graphics to break up text
- Consistent typography hierarchy
- White space for readability

**Slide Layout:**
- Title slides: Large, bold headlines
- Content slides: Headline + 3-5 bullet points max
- Use visuals (icons, charts, timelines) where possible
- Keep text concise and scannable

**Presentation Flow:**
- Start with mission and vision
- Present themes (the core of the strategy)
- Show timeline and execution
- End with outcomes and path forward
