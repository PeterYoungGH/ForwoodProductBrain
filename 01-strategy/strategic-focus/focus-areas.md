## Current Strategic Focus

High-level strategic priorities and focus areas for FY25-26.

### Focus
- **Description:** Drive prioritisation that aligns effort to key results and governs scope, requirements, and commercial agreements. Advocate for the importance of delivery velocity, quality and impact of agreed outcomes.
- **Rationale:** Ensures we maintain focus on what matters most and deliver with velocity and quality
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)
- **Business Outcomes:** [Forwood One Platform Launch and New Customer Acquisition](../../04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/oculus-platform-launch-new-deals.md)

### Team Capability
- **Description:** Develop depth of skill and product principles within the team while identifying and resourcing key gaps to ensure sustainable delivery and innovation capacity.
- **Rationale:** Building team capability is essential for sustainable delivery and innovation
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)
- **Business Outcomes:** [Forwood One Platform Launch and New Customer Acquisition](../../04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/oculus-platform-launch-new-deals.md)

### Customer Connection
- **Description:** Build close, insight-driven relationships with clients to inform product direction, create growth loops, and ensure every release drives measurable value.
- **Rationale:** Strong customer relationships drive product direction and ensure we deliver value
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)
- **Business Outcomes:** [Forwood One Platform Launch and New Customer Acquisition](../../04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/oculus-platform-launch-new-deals.md)

### Training and Enablement
- **Description:** Strengthen internal capability confidence through structured training, communication, and enablement programs.
- **Rationale:** Effective training and enablement build confidence and capability across the organization
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)
- **Business Outcomes:** [Forwood One Platform Launch and New Customer Acquisition](../../04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/oculus-platform-launch-new-deals.md)

### Innovation and Experimentation
- **Description:** Nurture innovation streams that test emerging technologies, such as AI and data-driven experiments, to inform our 12–24 month horizon and maintain an outcome-focused mindset.
- **Rationale:** Innovation and experimentation ensure we stay ahead and maintain an outcome-focused approach
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)
- **Business Outcomes:** [Forwood One Platform Launch and New Customer Acquisition](../../04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/oculus-platform-launch-new-deals.md)

## Strategic Pillars for the Next Two Years

Based on customer feedback, we are focusing on four key areas in our roadmap for the next two years:

### Ease of Use
- **Description:** Intuitive, decluttered, effortless for all roles.
- **Rationale:** Usability is fundamental to adoption and engagement across all user levels
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

### SIF Prevention
- **Description:** Double-down on CRM+, sharpen tools & workflows, reduce SIFs.
- **Rationale:** Our core mission is to eliminate SIFs, and this pillar ensures we continuously improve our effectiveness
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

### Adoption & Engagement
- **Description:** Role-based design, smart onboarding & coaching, gamification.
- **Rationale:** High adoption and engagement are essential for delivering value and achieving safety outcomes
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

### Data & Insights
- **Description:** Proactive analytics, predict incidents, benchmark maturity.
- **Rationale:** Data-driven insights enable proactive safety management and continuous improvement
- **Key Initiatives:** [Forwood One Platform Launch](Forwood-One-platform-launch.md)

## Decision Criteria

How do we decide what to focus on?
- Alignment to key results and OKRs
- Impact on commercial traction and customer migration
- Delivery velocity, quality and impact of agreed outcomes
- Customer value and measurable outcomes

## Notes

These strategic pillars guide our approach to delivering the Forwood One platform and achieving our FY25-26 objectives. They emphasize the importance of focus, capability, customer connection, enablement, and innovation.
