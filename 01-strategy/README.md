# Strategy

High-level strategic focus, roadmaps, and product vision.

## Purpose

This section contains strategic planning documents that guide product direction:
- **Roadmaps**: Strategic (2-year) and tactical (12-month) product roadmaps
- **Strategic Focus**: Key focus areas and priorities
- **Vision**: Product vision statements and north star metrics

## Structure

- `roadmap/` - Roadmap documents (4-layer framework)
  - `01-strategic-roadmap.md` - Strategic roadmap (2+ year vision + 6-12 month themes/bets)
  - `02-portfolio-roadmap.md` - Portfolio roadmap (3-6 months: initiatives and outcomes)
  - `03-delivery-roadmap.md` - Delivery roadmap (weekly: release planning)
  - `04-public-roadmap.md` - Public roadmap (external: customer communication)
  - `README.md` - Roadmap navigation guide
  - `archive/` - Historical roadmaps organized by time period
- `strategic-focus/` - Strategic focus areas
  - `focus-areas.md` - Current strategic focus
  - `archive/` - Historical focus documents
- `vision/` - Product vision and north star
  - `vision-statements.md` - Vision documentation

## Navigation

- Start with `roadmap/01-strategic-roadmap.md` for the 2-year vision and 6-12 month strategic themes/bets
- Check `roadmap/02-portfolio-roadmap.md` for active initiatives (3-6 months)
- See `roadmap/03-delivery-roadmap.md` for release planning (weekly)

## Roadmap Structure

Forwood uses a 4-layer roadmapping framework. See [`roadmap/README.md`](roadmap/README.md) for complete details.

### Strategic Roadmap (2+ Year Vision + 6-12 Months)
- Long-term vision and strategic themes (2+ years)
- Strategic themes and bets (6-12 months)
- Problem/opportunity areas
- Desired outcomes and metric shifts
- High-level Now/Next/Later timeline
- Market positioning and competitive strategy
- Key business outcomes and success metrics
- Strategic partnerships and expansion plans

**Update Frequency:** Quarterly (lightly), plus mid-quarter check-ins

### Portfolio Roadmap (3-6 Months)
- Initiatives with problem statements
- Expected outcomes and key metrics
- Now/Next/Later structure
- Launch moments and dependencies

**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Fortnightly

### Delivery Roadmap (Weekly)
- Release planning and execution
- Release candidate items
- Readiness status and risks
- Launch alignment

**Update Frequency:** Weekly or as releases are prepared

### Public Roadmap (External)
- Customer-facing themes and outcomes
- Planned / In Progress / Recently Released
- Safe-to-share direction

**Update Frequency:** Monthly / Quarterly

## Best Practices

- Strategic roadmap should align with vision and long-term business goals
- Tactical roadmap should be derived from and support the strategic roadmap
- Update tactical roadmap quarterly to reflect changing priorities and learnings
- Archive old roadmaps to maintain historical record
- Link roadmaps to initiatives in `02-initiatives/`
- Connect strategic focus to business outcomes in `04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/`
