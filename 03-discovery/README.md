# Discovery

Research, experiments, and product discovery practices.

## Purpose

This section houses all research and discovery activities that inform product decisions:
- **Research**: User research, market research, and data analysis
- **Experiments**: Product experiments and tests
- **Insights**: Synthesized insights from research

## Structure

- `research/` - Research activities
  - `user-research/` - User interviews, surveys, usability studies
  - `market-research/` - Market analysis, competitive research, industry trends
  - `data-analysis/` - Analytics, metrics analysis, quantitative insights
- `experiments/` - Product experiments and tests
- `insights/` - Synthesized insights from research

## Creating Research Documents

1. Use the template from `06-reference/templates/research-template.md`
2. Store in the appropriate research subfolder
3. Link research findings to:
   - Opportunities in `04-opportunities/03-opportunities/`
   - Solutions in `04-opportunities/04-solutions/`
   - Business outcomes in `04-opportunities/01-business-outcomes/`

## Navigation

- Browse `research/` by type (user, market, data)
- Check `experiments/` for active tests
- Review `insights/` for synthesized learnings

## Best Practices

- Document research methodology and participants
- Link research findings to opportunities they inform
- Synthesize insights regularly to identify patterns
- Archive completed experiments with results documented
- Keep research organized by date or topic for easy retrieval
