# Research: Rossing Forwood One Platform Meeting

**Type:** User Research / Customer Meeting  
**Date:** 2025-12-16  
**Researcher(s):** Serina Sands  
**Status:** Completed  
**Source:** [Inbox Item: Rossing Forwood One Platform Meeting](../../../00-inbox/raw/2025-12-16-rossing-forwood-one-platform-meeting-[TO-DO].md)

## Research Question

How do early adopter customers (specifically Rossing) perceive the Forwood One platform strategy, migration approach, and what are their key concerns and questions about the platform transition?

## Objectives

- Understand customer sentiment about Forwood One platform strategy
- Document customer questions and concerns about platform structure, migration, and features
- Capture timeline expectations and feedback on migration approach
- Identify areas where customer communication and documentation need improvement
- Understand customer enthusiasm and concerns about platform benefits

## Methodology

### Research Method
Customer Meeting / Stakeholder Discussion

### Participants/Data Sources
- **Customer:** Rossing (early adopter of SafetyPlus, long-term partner)
- **Forwood Participants:** Serina Sands, David Chiapoco, aja.stuart
- **Meeting Type:** Strategic partnership discussion and platform strategy sharing

### Timeline
- **Start:** 2025-12-16
- **End:** 2025-12-16

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Strong Customer Enthusiasm for Platform Consolidation
Rossing expressed strong enthusiasm for:
- Platform consolidation (unified system instead of multiple disconnected solutions)
- Improved integration and analytics capabilities
- Simplified IT and data management
- Alignment with internal IT strategy (highlighted as a major benefit)
- Migration from previously integrated systems makes this evolution particularly valuable

**Implication:** Platform consolidation is a strong value proposition that resonates with customers' IT strategy goals.

### Finding 2: Customer Questions Reveal Communication Gaps
Rossing has specific questions across multiple categories that indicate areas where communication and documentation need improvement:

**Platform & Product Structure:**
- Is Forwood One a replacement for SafetyPlus, or a parent platform that includes SafetyPlus, RiskPlus, CompliancePlus, and other modules?
- How do existing solutions (e.g. RiskPlus, CompliancePlus, CRN/BestSafe) fit into the new Forwood One structure?
- Is there a one-page overview that shows all products and modules available under the Forwood One umbrella?

**AI, Data Security & Privacy:**
- How will AI be implemented — is it an internal AI agent or an external service?
- How is confidential safety data protected when AI features are enabled?
- Will client data be isolated to their own environment?

**Integration & IT Strategy:**
- Will Forwood One reduce the number of platforms IT needs to manage?
- How does this platform align with the organisation's broader IT strategy and system centralisation?

**Migration & Change Management:**
- What is involved in migrating from SafetyPlus to Forwood One?
- Will the migration be handled remotely, or is site involvement required?
- Will there be system downtime during migration, and how long might it be?
- How will the existing SafetyPlus mobile app function during and after migration?

**Timeline & Rollout:**
- When will customers first see the Forwood One platform?
- What is the expected migration timeframe?
- When will roadmap and migration planning sessions occur?

**Commercials:**
- Will there be any changes to licensing or costs as part of the upgrade?

**Implication:** Need to create comprehensive customer-facing documentation addressing these questions, particularly a one-page overview of the Forwood One platform structure.

### Finding 3: Positive Reception of Migration Approach
Rossing responded positively to:
- Migration being largely handled by Forwood with minimal client involvement
- Very short, planned downtime window
- Mobile app remaining offline-capable
- Gradual integration approach with flexibility in timing

**Implication:** The migration approach is well-received and should be emphasized in customer communications.

### Finding 4: Timeline Expectations Set
Key timeline milestones communicated:
- **Early–Mid January 2026:** Customer-facing presentation to be shared (overview of full product suite, roadmap, migration options)
- **Late January 2026:** Roadmap and migration planning discussions
- **Target Migration Window:** Around Q3 (calendar year), subject to planning and readiness

**Implication:** These are concrete customer-facing milestones that should be tracked and communicated consistently.

### Finding 5: Partnership Positioning Successful
The meeting was positioned as an early "heads up" and partnership milestone. Rossing said this was an "early Christmas surprise" and expressed mutual appreciation for the long-term partnership. Agreement to reconvene in January.

**Implication:** Early engagement with key customers is valuable and strengthens partnership relationships.

## Insights

Synthesized insights from the research:

1. **Platform consolidation is a strong value proposition** - Customers see significant value in unified systems that simplify IT management and enable better data integration.

2. **Customer communication needs improvement** - Multiple questions indicate gaps in customer-facing documentation, particularly around platform structure, AI security, and migration details.

3. **One-page overview is needed** - Customers want a clear visual/overview showing all products and modules under Forwood One umbrella.

4. **AI security concerns are top of mind** - Customers need clear communication about how AI is implemented, data protection, and isolation.

5. **Migration approach is well-received** - The low-touch, Forwood-handled migration approach resonates with customers.

6. **Timeline transparency is important** - Customers want clear visibility into when they'll see the platform and when migration will occur.
