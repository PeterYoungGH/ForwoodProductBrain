## Research Question

Who are the primary users of Forwood's platform, what are their functional, emotional, and social jobs, and how should the product be designed to serve their needs?

## Objectives

- Define comprehensive user personas for all platform roles
- Identify functional, emotional, and social jobs for each persona
- Document frustrations, pain points, and design implications
- Provide foundation for product and design decisions

## Methodology

### Research Method
Persona development with Jobs-to-Be-Done framework overlay

### Participants/Data Sources
- User research and observation
- Product team analysis
- Jobs-to-Be-Done framework application
- Working draft for validation

### Timeline
- **Start:** 2025-11-28
- **End:** 2025-11-28

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Operator (Field Worker) Persona

**Profile:** Performs physical work on site and completes field-level verifications, audits, and safety observations using the Forwood mobile app.

**Functional Jobs:**
- Complete verifications, inspections, and safety forms quickly and accurately
- Report hazards, incidents, or near misses using mobile tools
- Stay compliant while performing physical work in variable field conditions

**Emotional Jobs:**
- Feel confident that their work is captured correctly and valued
- Avoid frustration from slow or confusing mobile experiences
- Feel safe and supported while working in challenging environments

**Social Jobs:**
- Be seen as reliable and safety-conscious by peers and supervisors
- Demonstrate responsibility and professionalism in their reporting

**Frustrations:**
- Limited or unreliable connectivity in field conditions
- Small screen usability issues, glare, or difficulty reading content
- Too many steps or unclear workflows for simple tasks

**Design Implications:**
- Must function reliably offline with auto-sync on reconnect
- Use large touch targets, minimal typing, and clear visual feedback
- Consider environmental conditions (sunlight, gloves, noise)
- Support short, guided workflows and local language options

### Finding 2: Supervisor (Frontline Operations Leader) Persona

**Profile:** Supervises daily site activities, ensuring work is performed safely and that operators engage with verification and reporting processes.

**Functional Jobs:**
- Oversee operator activities and ensure verification completion
- Coach teams and reinforce correct safety behaviors
- Track and report on daily performance and outstanding actions

**Emotional Jobs:**
- Feel in control of team safety outcomes
- Gain satisfaction from smooth, compliant operations
- Avoid stress caused by poor visibility or delays in data

**Social Jobs:**
- Be perceived as a capable, proactive leader who ensures team compliance
- Build trust with both operators and management

**Frustrations:**
- Time pressure and competing operational demands
- Poor usability in mobile workflows or slow app performance on site
- Difficulty tracking completion or follow-up on actions

**Design Implications:**
- Needs fast, intuitive mobile workflows and clear offline handling
- Clear progress tracking and visual indicators for outstanding tasks
- Quick access to history, reports, and team progress

### Finding 3: HSE Manager (Safety and Risk Manager) Persona

**Profile:** Responsible for overseeing the company's safety programs, reporting on performance, and ensuring continuous improvement in line with corporate and regulatory expectations.

**Functional Jobs:**
- Conduct and review verifications and incident reports
- Analyze trends and identify systemic risks
- Generate reports for executives and teams

**Emotional Jobs:**
- Feel confident that data is accurate and complete
- Experience trust in the system's ability to surface meaningful insights
- Feel effective in driving organizational safety improvement

**Social Jobs:**
- Be seen as a data-driven, strategic contributor to safety outcomes
- Demonstrate leadership in improving safety culture

**Frustrations:**
- Difficult or time-consuming reporting workflows
- Data inconsistency between sites
- Limited visibility into action completion or overdue verifications

**Design Implications:**
- Flexible reporting tools, trend dashboards, and automated alerts
- Intuitive filters and visual insights for communicating results
- Needs workflows that enable easy escalation and delegation of actions

### Finding 4: System Administrator (Corporate-Level ICT Admin) Persona

**Profile:** A technically proficient user responsible for maintaining the integrity and performance of the Forwood Safety+ platform across the organization.

**Functional Jobs:**
- Manage users, permissions, and integrations
- Maintain stable system performance and clean data
- Oversee subscription plans, billing, and license allocation

**Emotional Jobs:**
- Feel confident that the system is reliable and under control
- Avoid stress from outages, sync errors, or billing confusion
- Take pride in maintaining smooth system operations

**Social Jobs:**
- Be viewed as the technical expert who keeps things running efficiently
- Be trusted by leadership to manage system compliance and cost

**Frustrations:**
- Integration failures or sync errors between systems
- Inconsistent user role management and access requests
- Lack of visibility into backend data or billing issues

**Design Implications:**
- Provide health dashboards, clear billing overviews, and automation options
- Offer precise audit trails and role management tools
- Include clear UI for managing hierarchies, permissions, and data syncs

### Finding 5: Corporate Executive (CSO/VP HSE) Persona

**Profile:** Senior leaders who oversee enterprise-wide safety performance, governance, and investment in the Forwood Safety+ platform.

**Functional Jobs:**
- Monitor organization-wide safety performance and assurance coverage
- Receive alerts for critical events and overdue verifications
- Evaluate ROI, adoption, and subscription utilization

**Emotional Jobs:**
- Feel confident that risk is managed and governance is sound
- Experience assurance that their investment is delivering results
- Avoid anxiety from missing or delayed high-priority information

**Social Jobs:**
- Be recognized as a strategic leader driving zero-harm culture
- Communicate safety excellence to boards and investors

**Frustrations:**
- Limited transparency into assurance activity completion or coverage
- Too much granular data without clear insights or summaries
- Delay in receiving critical alerts or performance updates

**Design Implications:**
- Executive dashboard with configurable KPIs and filters (site, business unit, risk type)
- Smart notifications for key incidents or overdue assurance activities
- ROI and usage analytics to support budgeting and performance reviews

### Finding 6-11: Additional Personas

The research also identifies:
- **Incident Reporter** - Any user reporting incidents or near misses
- **Responder** - Immediate response role for emergencies
- **Investigator** - Post-incident analysis role
- **Action Reporter** - Creates corrective/preventive actions
- **Responsible Person** - Completes assigned actions
- **Reviewer** - Verifies completed actions meet standards

Each persona includes functional, emotional, and social jobs, frustrations, and design implications.

## Insights

Synthesized insights from the research:
- **Multi-role platform:** Forwood serves diverse roles from field workers to executives, each with distinct needs
- **Mobile-first for field:** Operators and supervisors need reliable offline mobile experiences
- **Desktop for analysis:** HSE managers and executives need powerful desktop reporting and analytics
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done overlay:** Understanding functional, emotional, and social jobs enables better product design
- **Environmental constraints:** Field users face unique challenges (connectivity, sunlight, gloves, noise)
- **Workflow integration:** Actions, verifications, and incidents must be seamlessly connected

## Implications

What do these findings mean for product decisions?

- **Platform must support both mobile and desktop** with appropriate feature sets for each
- **Offline functionality is critical** for field workers in remote/harsh environments
- **Reporting and analytics** must serve both operational (supervisors) and strategic (executives) needs
- **Workflow integration** is essential - actions, verifications, and incidents must connect seamlessly
- **Role-based experiences** should be tailored to each persona's primary jobs and contexts
- **Data accuracy and trust** are critical for HSE managers and executives
