## Research Question

What are the best practices for navigating uncertainty and managing risks in high-risk industries, and how can organizations build effective risk management programs?

## Objectives

- Document best-practice risk management process for high-risk industries
- Provide guidance on identifying, assessing, mitigating, monitoring, and communicating risks
- Support Mission Zero goal (zero deaths or serious injuries)
- Create reference material for risk management programs

## Methodology

### Research Method

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Global Context - Understanding Risk Management

**Current Business Environment:**
- Change is the only constant
- Issues like climate change, cybersecurity, and consumer demand for sustainable practices are keeping companies on their toes
- Need for greater accountability across every aspect of business
- Understanding and effectively managing risks is essential for achieving sustainable growth and success

**Systematic Approach:**
- Risk management protocols can feel overwhelming without the right tools and processes
- Following a systematic approach that captures threats and opportunities can significantly reduce vulnerability and strengthen opportunity

### Finding 2: Identifying Risks

**Importance:**
- Effective risk management begins with identifying potential threats that could impact organization's objectives
- Includes risks across safety, financial, operational, reputational, legal, and other important aspects

**Techniques for Identifying Risks:**
- Brainstorming Sessions
- Risk Registers
- SWOT Analysis
- Scenario Planning
- Expert Consultation
- Data Analysis
- Checklists and Templates

### Finding 3: Assessing Risks

**Key Outcomes:**
1. **Evaluating Likelihood and Impact:** Organizations must assess and rank the likelihood and potential impact of each risk
2. **Prioritizing Critical Risks:** Focus efforts and resources on mitigating the most critical risks

**Prioritization Criteria:**
- Severity
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Strategic importance

### Finding 4: Mitigating Risks

**Core Purpose:**
- Reduce the likelihood or impact of identified threats

**Strategies May Include:**
- Risk transfer through insurance
- Implementing control measures
- Diversifying operations
- Establishing contingency plans

**Protection Focus:**
- Protect people and organizational assets from harm or loss
- Includes both tangible assets (property, equipment, inventory) and intangible assets (reputation, brand value, intellectual property)
- **Clear focus on worker safety remains imperative**

### Finding 5: Monitoring Risks

**Continuous Process:**
- Risk management is an ongoing process requiring continuous monitoring
- Ensure control measures remain effective

**Monitoring Includes:**
- Reviewing risk registers
- Performance indicators
- Control effectiveness tests
- Feedback mechanisms

**Proactive Adaptation:**
- Stay informed about emerging risks and external trends
- Proactively adapt risk management strategies to address evolving challenges

### Finding 6: Communicating Risks

**Importance:**
- Effectively communicating risks and mitigation strategies is essential
- Ensures all stakeholders are informed and engaged

**Communication Strategies:**
- Regular risk reporting
- Stakeholder meetings
- Workshops and training sessions
- Tailoring communication methods to suit stakeholder needs

## Insights

Synthesized insights from the research:
- **Systematic approach is critical:** Following a structured process (identify → assess → mitigate → monitor → communicate) is essential
- **Mission Zero focus:** Clear focus on preventing fatalities and serious injuries
- **Continuous monitoring:** Risk management is ongoing, not one-time
- **Stakeholder engagement:** Effective communication is key to successful risk management
- **Adaptability:** Organizations must proactively adapt to evolving risk landscape

## Implications

What do these findings mean for product decisions?

- **Product must support systematic risk management:** Features should align with identify → assess → mitigate → monitor → communicate flow
- **Mission Zero alignment:** All features should support the goal of zero deaths or serious injuries
- **Continuous monitoring capabilities:** Product must enable ongoing risk monitoring and control effectiveness testing
- **Communication tools:** Product should facilitate stakeholder communication and reporting
- **Adaptability features:** Product should support proactive adaptation to evolving risks
