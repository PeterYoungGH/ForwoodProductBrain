# Research: EDP Pain Points and Requests

**Type:** Customer Research  
**Date:** 2025-12-08  
**Researcher(s):** EDP (Customer)  
**Status:** Completed

## Research Question

What are the outstanding pain points, unresolved issues, and service requests from EDP that need to be addressed to ensure the tools provided by Forwood (particularly Safety+ and QuickSight) are effective within EDP's company?

## Objectives

- Document all outstanding issues from the contract that EDP believes remain unfulfilled
- Capture stability and operational issues affecting daily use
- Identify corrective and evolutionary service requests needed for effective adoption
- Understand EDP's expectations for resolution timeframes and prioritization

## Methodology

### Research Method
Formal customer communication (email) from EDP to Forwood account manager

### Participants/Data Sources

- **Start:** 2025-12-08
- **End:** 2025-12-08

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Outstanding Contractual Requirements (EDP Perspective)

EDP believes the following items from the contract remain unfulfilled (should have been closed by end of October):

1. **Enhanced Self-Service and Configuration Capability**: Ability to include or modify questions or questionnaires
2. **Application Loading on Other Devices**: Loading issues on certain devices (various Android versions and terminal models)
3. **Viewable User Comments**: Comments from risk checks cannot be viewed or exported together; each check must be accessed individually
4. **Configurable Action Plans and Evidence**: No direct warning when 'No' is answered in checks, which could lead to critical risks materializing as accidents
5. **Safety+App Dashboard and Predictive Analytics**: Dashboard is overly simple; the main differentiating element (predictive capacity for accidents) has not been shown in any model or example. Dashboards should be more action-oriented, easier to interpret, and enable immediate action based on results
6. **Import/Export Data Functionality**: Ability to import/export data between the app and other EDP systems has not been demonstrated. EDP does not currently have the capacity to import/export data in an agile and manageable way, nor verify information included in the application
7. **Date Filtering in Map View**: Risk assessments cannot be filtered by completion date on the map view. This is partially done but not available for all users (as it should be)
8. **Capturing Work Description in Verifications**: It is unclear what work each verification refers to

**Note**: Forwood does not believe these are contractual requirements, but EDP does.

### Finding 2: Critical Stability and Operational Issues

- **Stability problems** reported by end users, discouraging use and risking job performance
- **Ticket management issues**:
  - Difficulty replicating reported problems
  - Slow resolution of operational incidents
  - False closure of unresolved tickets
  - Need for minimum information requirements when opening tickets
- **Localization**: Questions and questionnaires appear in English; should be in Spanish
- **Data quality**: Need to remove Forwood's test verifications and EDP test data
- **UI/UX issues**: Open/pending tickets showing as "white page with black text"
- **AI content accuracy**: AI-generated content may be incorrect

### Finding 3: Key Concern - Regression After Updates

EDP is particularly concerned that after each new update, issues previously resolved or functioning correctly begin to fail again. This creates setbacks and conveys an image of unreliability to end users.

### Finding 4: Corrective and Evolutionary Service Requests

EDP has identified multiple service requests for improvement:

**High Priority:**
- Improve usability based on suggestions
- Propose methods to verify accuracy of user-entered information (including photographic evidence). The accuracy of information entered is the greatest risk of the tool
- Provide greater traceability and status updates for tickets, including expected resolution dates
- Make dashboards more action-oriented, easier to interpret, and enable immediate action (applies to all three dashboard types)

**Documentation:**
- Prepare comprehensive user manuals and quick reference guides for interpreting reports and suggested actions
- Create simple operational manual for bulk loading companies and contracts

**Administrative:**
- Enable admin role to delete records (e.g., tests)
- Grant contractors admin roles to download verifications from Safety Plus (in manageable file format like Excel) and create DACs for their personnel

**Configuration:**
- Adjust questionnaire options:
  - Action Type: Only "Implemented in field" or "Stop work"
  - Action Plan Type: Only "Procedure" and "Installation/equipment/weather"
- Adapt organizational structure and visibility to EDP requirements: Contractors should view verifications of their subcontractors; EDP contract managers should view verifications within their contracts

**Feature Requests:**
- Include audio recording for pre-task talks during questionnaire completion
- Create pop-up prompt for users when new version is available from Play Store or Apple Store

### Finding 5: Strategic Note on Service Expectations

EDP notes that they are contributing to tool development at no cost, adding value for the entire user community. Therefore, they expect fast, efficient, "premium" service in most cases at no additional cost to them, given the added value they provide.

## Insights

Synthesized insights from the research:
- **Stability is critical**: Stability problems are discouraging use and risking job performance, making this a blocker to adoption
- **Data accuracy is the greatest risk**: EDP identifies information accuracy verification as the greatest risk of the tool
- **Predictive analytics value not visible**: The main differentiating feature (predictive capacity) is not visible to users
- **Support process friction**: Ticket management issues create frustration and slow problem resolution
- **Update regression concern**: EDP is concerned about issues re-emerging after updates, creating reliability concerns

## Implications

What do these findings mean for product decisions?

- **Immediate action required**: Stability issues must be addressed immediately as they are blocking adoption
- **Data integrity focus**: Mechanisms to verify accuracy of user-entered information are critical
- **Value demonstration**: Need to make predictive analytics visible and actionable in dashboards
- **Support process improvement**: Ticket management processes need better traceability, status updates, and resolution workflows
- **Contract alignment**: Need to clarify and align on what is truly contractual vs. enhancement requests
- **Quality assurance**: Need to prevent regression of previously resolved issues in new updates
