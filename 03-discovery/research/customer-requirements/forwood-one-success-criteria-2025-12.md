# Research: Forwood One Success Criteria

**Type:** Customer Requirements  
**Date:** 2025-12-09  
**Researcher(s):** Meagan (Head of Solutions)  
**Status:** Completed

## Research Question

What are the success criteria for Forwood One platform migrations and how should we measure migration success?

## Objectives

- Document comprehensive success criteria for Forwood One migrations
- Establish measurable targets across platform readiness, migration execution, support, and satisfaction
- Define commercial milestones for phased migration approach
- Create reference framework for migration planning and execution

## Methodology

### Research Method
Internal requirements gathering and success criteria definition

### Participants/Data Sources
- Meagan (Head of Solutions) - provided success criteria document
- Input from Solutions team on migration requirements
- Success criteria treated as input, not 100% locked in plans

### Timeline
- **Start:** 2025-12-09
- **End:** 2025-12-09

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Platform Readiness Requirements
Success criteria require:
- V1, V2, V3 releases on committed dates
- Zero P1 defects at client cut-over
- Maximum three P2 defects (each with documented workaround, committed fix date, and communication line)
- Critical workflow UAT pass rate above 95%
- Average load time under 2 seconds for safety-critical workflows

### Finding 2: Migration Execution Standards
Migration success requires:
- Zero operational disruption during client migrations
- All Safety+ and Safety+ legacy clients migrated in agreed sequence without rollback
- Ninety percent of users able to complete core workflows on day one

### Finding 3: Post-Migration Support Targets
Support performance targets include:
- Fifty percent reduction in P1–P3 tickets within 60 days post-migration
- Eighty percent of tickets resolved by CS or support without engineering by month two
- No bespoke development delivered outside approved change control

### Finding 4: Client Satisfaction Goals
Client satisfaction targets:
- Client satisfaction score of 4.2/5 or higher within 60 days
- Zero executive-level escalations relating to performance or delivery
- Three or more clients provide testimonials supporting the public launch

### Finding 5: Commercial Phased Migration
Commercial milestones phased by platform:
- Safety+ phase: Eighty percent of Safety+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2026
- CRM+ phase: Eighty percent of CRM+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q4 2027
- Risk+ and Compliance+ phase: Eighty percent of Risk+ and Compliance+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2027
- Forwood One becomes the default platform for all new sales by mid-2026
- Zero churn of Safety+ and CRM+ clients due to performance or migration issues

## Insights

Synthesized insights from the research:
- Success criteria cover technical, operational, commercial, and satisfaction dimensions
- Criteria emphasize controlled, production-ready migrations with strong client experience
- Phased commercial approach allows for platform-specific migration timelines
- Governance and change control are critical for maintaining platform stability
- Internal readiness (team education, playbooks) is essential for migration success

## Implications

What do these findings mean for product decisions?
- Platform development must prioritize release delivery commitments and defect management
- Migration planning must focus on zero operational disruption and day-one user capability
- Support processes must be designed to reduce ticket volume and resolve issues efficiently
- Client satisfaction measurement and escalation prevention are critical success factors
- Commercial milestones require careful planning and execution across platform phases
