# Research: Rossing Forwood One Platform Meeting

**Type:** Customer Research  
**Date:** 2025-12-16  
**Researcher(s):** Serina Sands (Forwood), Rossing team  
**Status:** Completed  
**Created:** 2026-01-03

## Research Question

What are Rossing's questions and concerns about Forwood One platform strategy, and how should Forwood communicate the platform direction to early adopter customers?

## Objectives

- Share platform strategy before broader market release
- Acknowledge Rossing's partnership as early adopter
- Set expectations on roadmap, migration, and benefits
- Capture customer questions and concerns about Forwood One

## Methodology

### Research Method
Customer meeting to share platform strategy and gather feedback

### Participants/Data Sources
- Serina Sands (Forwood - Global Lead Strategic Key Accounts)
- Rossing team (customer)
- Meeting notes shared with David Chiapoco and Aja Stuart

### Timeline
- **Start:** 2025-12-16
- **End:** 2025-12-16

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Platform Overview - Forwood One

**Platform Strategy:**
- Forwood One is the evolution and upgrade of SafetyPlus
- It becomes the baseline platform for all Forwood products
- Existing products (e.g., SafetyPlus, RiskPlus, CompliancePlus) will be:
  - Unified into one single platform
  - Delivered through a single user interface
  - Existing SafetyPlus functionality will continue to work within the new platform

### Finding 2: Key Platform Benefits

**1. Unified System:**
- One platform instead of multiple disconnected solutions
- Simplifies IT management, licensing, hosting, and support
- Enables better data integration across:
  - Incidents
  - Audits & inspections
  - Risk
  - Compliance

**2. Increased Configuration & Customisation:**
- Clients can:
  - Modify forms, checklists, and workflows
  - Configure platform behaviour without needing support tickets
  - Greater client ownership and flexibility

**3. AI-Enabled Future:**
- Forwood One is built as an AI-ready platform
- AI features will be introduced progressively
- Enables:
  - Advanced insights
  - Smarter trend analysis
  - Future automation capabilities

### Finding 3: AI, Security & Data Privacy

**AI Approach:**
- AI will be:
  - Secure
  - Isolated to the client environment
  - No direct use of public AI tools (e.g., OpenAI endpoints)
- Platform being developed in alignment with ISO 42001 (AI Management Systems)
- Architectural and security details will be shared as AI features roll out

### Finding 4: Commercials & Licensing

**Pricing:**
- No change to existing licensing fees
- Customers receive:
  - Additional functionality
  - Platform upgrade
  - Improved performance and stability
  - Enhanced configuration capabilities

### Finding 5: Customer Questions

**Platform & Product Structure:**
- Is Forwood One a replacement for SafetyPlus, or a parent platform that includes SafetyPlus, RiskPlus, CompliancePlus, and other modules?
- How do existing solutions (e.g. RiskPlus, CompliancePlus, CRN/BestSafe) fit into the new Forwood One structure?
- Is there a one-page overview that shows all products and modules available under the Forwood One umbrella?

**AI, Data Security & Privacy:**
- How will AI be implemented — is it an internal AI agent or an external service?
- How is confidential safety data protected when AI features are enabled?
- Will client data be isolated to their own environment?

**Integration & IT Strategy:**
- Will Forwood One reduce the number of platforms IT needs to manage?
- How does this platform align with the organisation's broader IT strategy and system centralisation?

**Migration & Change Management:**
- What is involved in migrating from SafetyPlus to Forwood One?
- Will the migration be handled remotely, or is site involvement required?
- Will there be system downtime during migration, and how long might it be?
- How will the existing SafetyPlus mobile app function during and after migration?

**Timeline & Rollout:**
- When will customers first see the Forwood One platform?
- What is the expected migration timeframe?
- When will roadmap and migration planning sessions occur?

**Commercials:**
- Will there be any changes to licensing or costs as part of the upgrade?

### Finding 6: Migration Approach

**Forwood One is an upgrade, not a replacement:**
- Migration will be largely handled by Forwood
- Require minimal client involvement
- Expected impact:
  - Very short, planned downtime window
  - Mostly affects administrators
  - Mobile app remains offline-capable

**Change management approach includes:**
- Gradual integration of the existing SafetyPlus mobile app
- Flexibility in timing of feature transitions

### Finding 7: Timeline (Indicative)

**Early–Mid January 2026:**
- Customer-facing presentation to be shared
- Overview of full product suite under ForwoodOne, roadmap, migration options

**Late January 2026:**
- Roadmap and migration planning discussions
- Target week of 19 January, proposed date: 23 January

**Target Migration Window:**
- Around Q3 (calendar year), subject to planning and readiness

### Finding 8: Hosting & Infrastructure

- Hosting remains unchanged: AWS
- Existing accreditations retained
- Change is at the application stack level only

### Finding 9: Client Feedback

**Strong enthusiasm for:**
- Platform consolidation
- Improved integration and analytics
- Simplified IT and data management
- Alignment with internal IT strategy highlighted as a major benefit
- Migration from previously integrated systems makes this evolution particularly valuable

**Customer Response:**
- Rossing said this was an "early Christmas surprise"
- Mutual appreciation expressed for the long-term partnership

## Insights

Synthesized insights from the research:
- **Early adopter engagement:** Forwood is proactively sharing platform strategy with early adopters before broader market release
- **Unified platform value:** Single platform simplifies IT management and enables better data integration
- **Configuration is key:** Increased configuration capabilities are a major selling point
- **AI readiness:** Platform is designed for AI but details will be shared progressively
- **No pricing change:** Existing customers receive upgrade benefits without fee increases

## Implications

What do these findings mean for product decisions?

- **Platform strategy communication:** Need clear messaging about unified platform benefits
- **Configuration capabilities:** Must deliver on promise of client self-service configuration
- **AI transparency:** Need to balance AI readiness messaging with security/privacy concerns
- **Migration messaging:** Early adopters need clear migration path and timeline
- **Value demonstration:** Must show how unified platform delivers better integration and insights
