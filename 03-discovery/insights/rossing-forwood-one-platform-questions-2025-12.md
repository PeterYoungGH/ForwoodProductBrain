# Rossing Forwood One Platform Questions - Customer Insights

**Topic:** Customer Questions and Concerns About Forwood One Platform Structure, Migration, and Features  
**Date:** 2025-12-16  
**Source Research:** [Rossing Forwood One Platform Meeting](../../03-discovery/research/user-research/rossing-forwood-one-platform-meeting-2025-12.md)  
**Source Inbox:** [Inbox Item: Rossing Forwood One Platform Meeting](../../00-inbox/raw/2025-12-16-rossing-forwood-one-platform-meeting-[TO-DO].md)

## Insight Statement

Rossing, an early adopter and long-term partner, has raised specific questions about Forwood One platform that reveal critical communication gaps and customer concerns. These questions span platform structure, AI security, migration process, and commercial terms, indicating areas where product communication and documentation need significant improvement to support successful customer migration and adoption.

---

## Evidence

### Platform & Product Structure Questions

1. **Platform Relationship Clarity:**
   - "Is Forwood One a replacement for SafetyPlus, or a parent platform that includes SafetyPlus, RiskPlus, CompliancePlus, and other modules?"
   - **Implication:** Customers are unclear about the relationship between Forwood One and existing products

2. **Product Integration:**
   - "How do existing solutions (e.g. RiskPlus, CompliancePlus, CRN/BestSafe) fit into the new Forwood One structure?"
   - **Implication:** Need clear visual/structural diagram showing product relationships

3. **Overview Documentation:**
   - "Is there a one-page overview that shows all products and modules available under the Forwood One umbrella?"
   - **Implication:** Customers want a single, clear visual overview document

### AI, Data Security & Privacy Questions

4. **AI Implementation:**
   - "How will AI be implemented — is it an internal AI agent or an external service?"
   - **Implication:** Customers need clarity on AI architecture and implementation approach

5. **Data Protection:**
   - "How is confidential safety data protected when AI features are enabled?"
   - **Implication:** Security and privacy concerns are top of mind, especially for safety-critical data

6. **Data Isolation:**
   - "Will client data be isolated to their own environment?"
   - **Implication:** Customers need assurance about data isolation and multi-tenancy security

### Integration & IT Strategy Questions

7. **Platform Consolidation:**
   - "Will Forwood One reduce the number of platforms IT needs to manage?"
   - **Implication:** IT simplification is a key value proposition that needs clear confirmation

8. **IT Strategy Alignment:**
   - "How does this platform align with the organisation's broader IT strategy and system centralisation?"
   - **Implication:** Customers want to understand strategic fit with their IT roadmap

### Migration & Change Management Questions

9. **Migration Process:**
   - "What is involved in migrating from SafetyPlus to Forwood One?"
   - **Implication:** Need detailed migration process documentation

10. **Migration Execution:**
    - "Will the migration be handled remotely, or is site involvement required?"
    - **Implication:** Customers want clarity on their involvement level

11. **Downtime:**
    - "Will there be system downtime during migration, and how long might it be?"
    - **Implication:** Business continuity concerns need clear, specific answers

12. **Mobile App Continuity:**
    - "How will the existing SafetyPlus mobile app function during and after migration?"
    - **Implication:** Mobile app continuity is critical for field operations

### Timeline & Rollout Questions

13. **Platform Visibility:**
    - "When will customers first see the Forwood One platform?"
    - **Implication:** Customers want visibility into rollout timeline

14. **Migration Timeline:**
    - "What is the expected migration timeframe?"
    - **Implication:** Need clear migration timeline communication

15. **Planning Sessions:**
    - "When will roadmap and migration planning sessions occur?"
    - **Implication:** Customers want scheduled engagement points

### Commercial Questions

16. **Licensing Changes:**
    - "Will there be any changes to licensing or costs as part of the upgrade?"
    - **Implication:** Commercial terms need clear communication (note: meeting notes indicate no change to licensing fees)

---

## Context

These questions come from Rossing, an early adopter of SafetyPlus and a long-term partner. The meeting was positioned as an early "heads up" and partnership milestone before broader market release. Despite strong enthusiasm for platform consolidation, the volume and specificity of questions indicate that:

1. **Customer-facing documentation is insufficient** - Many questions should be answerable through existing documentation
2. **Platform structure needs clearer communication** - The relationship between Forwood One and existing products is not clear
3. **Migration process needs detailed documentation** - Customers need step-by-step understanding of what to expect
4. **AI security messaging needs improvement** - Customers have legitimate concerns about data protection with AI features

The meeting notes indicate that Rossing expressed "strong enthusiasm" for platform consolidation and alignment with IT strategy, suggesting the questions are not blockers but rather indicate a need for better communication and documentation.

---

## Implications

### Product & Strategy

**High Priority:**
- **Create one-page platform overview** - Visual diagram showing all products and modules under Forwood One umbrella
- **Develop comprehensive FAQ document** - Address all questions systematically
- **Improve AI security messaging** - Clear communication about data protection, isolation, and implementation approach
- **Create migration process documentation** - Detailed step-by-step guide for customers

**Medium Priority:**
- **Platform structure visualization** - Clear diagram showing how SafetyPlus, RiskPlus, CompliancePlus fit into Forwood One
- **Timeline communication** - Clear, consistent messaging about rollout and migration timelines
- **Mobile app continuity documentation** - Specific information about app functionality during and after migration

**Lower Priority:**
- **IT strategy alignment messaging** - How Forwood One supports customer IT centralization goals
- **Commercial terms communication** - Clear documentation of licensing and cost structure

### Customer Success

- **Use questions to inform communication strategy** - These questions likely represent concerns from other customers
- **Create customer-facing materials** - FAQ, one-page overview, migration guide
- **Schedule follow-up sessions** - Ensure questions are answered in roadmap and migration planning sessions

### Market

- **Competitive positioning** - Clear platform structure communication helps differentiate Forwood One
- **Customer retention** - Addressing concerns proactively supports successful migration
- **Adoption barriers** - Unanswered questions could become barriers to adoption if not addressed
