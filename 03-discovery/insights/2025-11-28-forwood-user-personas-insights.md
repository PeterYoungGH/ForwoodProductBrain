## Insight Statement

Forwood serves 11 distinct user personas from field workers to executives, each with unique functional, emotional, and social jobs. The platform must support both mobile-first offline experiences for field workers and powerful desktop analytics for managers, with seamless workflow integration across all personas. Understanding these personas and their jobs-to-be-done is foundational for product design decisions that address real user needs across all organizational roles.

---

## Evidence

### Multi-Role Platform with Diverse Needs

**11 Distinct Personas Identified:**
1. **Operator (Field Worker)** - Mobile-first, offline-critical, environmental constraints
2. **Supervisor (Frontline Operations Leader)** - Both mobile and desktop, time pressure, team oversight
3. **HSE Manager (Safety and Risk Manager)** - Primarily desktop, data accuracy, reporting needs
4. **System Administrator (Corporate-Level ICT Admin)** - Desktop, system reliability, integration management
5. **Corporate Executive (CSO/VP HSE)** - Desktop dashboards, strategic insights, governance
6. **Incident Reporter (Any Role)** - Predominantly mobile, quick reporting, stress reduction
7. **Responder (Immediate Response Role)** - Mobile alerts, desktop coordination, real-time updates
8. **Investigator (Post-Incident Analysis Role)** - Desktop analysis, mobile evidence collection, collaboration
9. **Action Reporter (Actions Workflow Context)** - Mobile creation, desktop linking, visibility needs
10. **Responsible Person (Actions Workflow Context)** - Mobile execution, desktop planning, accountability
11. **Reviewer (Actions Workflow Context)** - Desktop review, mobile quick approvals, evidence verification

**Implication:** Platform must serve diverse roles with distinct needs, contexts, and primary interaction modes. One-size-fits-all approaches will fail.

### Mobile-First for Field Workers with Offline-Critical Requirements

**Operator and Supervisor Needs:**
- Must function reliably offline with auto-sync on reconnect
- Large touch targets, minimal typing, clear visual feedback
- Environmental considerations: sunlight, gloves, noise
- Short, guided workflows and local language options
- Fast, intuitive mobile workflows with clear offline handling

**Frustrations:**
- Limited or unreliable connectivity in field conditions
- Small screen usability issues, glare, or difficulty reading content
- Too many steps or unclear workflows for simple tasks
- Poor usability in mobile workflows or slow app performance on site

**Implication:** Offline functionality is critical for field workers in remote/harsh environments. Mobile experiences must be optimized for environmental constraints and reliability.

### Desktop-First for Managers and Executives with Powerful Analytics

**HSE Manager and Executive Needs:**
- Flexible reporting tools, trend dashboards, and automated alerts
- Intuitive filters and visual insights for communicating results
- Executive dashboard with configurable KPIs and filters
- Smart notifications for key incidents or overdue assurance activities
- ROI and usage analytics to support budgeting and performance reviews

**Frustrations:**
- Difficult or time-consuming reporting workflows
- Data inconsistency between sites
- Limited visibility into action completion or overdue verifications
- Too much granular data without clear insights or summaries
- Delay in receiving critical alerts or performance updates

**Implication:** Reporting and analytics must serve both operational (supervisors) and strategic (executives) needs. Desktop experiences must provide powerful analytics and clear insights.

### Jobs-to-Be-Done Framework Reveals Emotional and Social Needs

**Functional Jobs:** What tasks must they accomplish?
- Complete verifications, inspections, and safety forms quickly and accurately
- Report hazards, incidents, or near misses using mobile tools
- Analyze trends and identify systemic risks
- Monitor organization-wide safety performance

**Emotional Jobs:** How do they want to feel?
- Feel confident that their work is captured correctly and valued
- Avoid frustration from slow or confusing mobile experiences
- Feel safe and supported while working in challenging environments
- Feel confident that data is accurate and complete
- Experience trust in the system's ability to surface meaningful insights

**Social Jobs:** How do they want to be perceived?
- Be seen as reliable and safety-conscious by peers and supervisors
- Be perceived as a capable, proactive leader who ensures team compliance
- Be seen as a data-driven, strategic contributor to safety outcomes
- Be recognized as a strategic leader driving zero-harm culture

**Implication:** Product design must address not just functional needs but also emotional and social jobs. Features should reduce friction, create confidence, and support professional identity.

### Workflow Integration is Essential Across All Personas

**Integration Requirements:**
- Actions, verifications, and incidents must connect seamlessly
- Workflow integration across all personas is essential
- Data must flow between mobile and desktop experiences
- Role-based experiences should be tailored to each persona's primary jobs and contexts

**Implication:** Workflow integration is essential - actions, verifications, and incidents must connect seamlessly. The platform must support end-to-end workflows that span multiple personas and touchpoints.

### Data Accuracy and Trust are Critical

**Trust Requirements:**
- HSE managers need confidence that data is accurate and complete
- Executives need trust in the system's ability to surface meaningful insights
- System administrators need confidence that the system is reliable and under control
- All personas need to trust that their work is captured correctly and valued

**Implication:** Data accuracy and trust are critical for HSE managers and executives. The platform must ensure data integrity and provide transparency into data quality.

## Context

This research provides comprehensive user personas with Jobs-to-Be-Done overlay, identifying 11 distinct personas from field workers to executives. Each persona includes functional, emotional, and social jobs, frustrations, and design implications.

The personas serve as a foundation for product and design decisions, ensuring features address real user needs across all organizational roles and workflows. The Jobs-to-Be-Done framework reveals that users have not just functional needs but also emotional and social needs that must be addressed in product design.

## Implications

### Product

**High Priority:**
- **Platform must support both mobile and desktop** with appropriate feature sets for each
- **Offline functionality is critical** for field workers in remote/harsh environments
- **Reporting and analytics** must serve both operational (supervisors) and strategic (executives) needs
- **Workflow integration** is essential - actions, verifications, and incidents must connect seamlessly
- **Role-based experiences** should be tailored to each persona's primary jobs and contexts

**Medium Priority:**
- **Data accuracy and trust** are critical for HSE managers and executives
- **Environmental considerations** must be addressed in mobile design (sunlight, gloves, noise)
- **Localization** is important for field workers (language options)
- **Performance** is critical for mobile experiences (fast load times, smooth interactions)

### Strategy

**High Priority:**
- **Persona-driven design** - Use personas as foundation for all product design decisions
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done approach** - Validate features against persona jobs (functional, emotional, social)
- **Multi-role platform strategy** - Ensure platform serves all organizational roles effectively

**Medium Priority:**
- **User research validation** - Validate personas with user research and interviews
- **Feature specification** - Use personas in feature specification and design reviews
- **Testing** - Test with real users representing each persona

### Design

**High Priority:**
- **Mobile-first for field workers** - Design mobile experiences for field workers with offline-first approach
- **Desktop-first for managers** - Design desktop experiences for managers and executives with powerful analytics
- **Workflow integration** - Ensure seamless workflow integration across all personas
- **Role-based experiences** - Tailor experiences to each persona's primary jobs and contexts

**Medium Priority:**
- **Environmental design** - Consider environmental conditions (sunlight, gloves, noise) in mobile design
- **Accessibility** - Ensure designs are accessible for all personas
- **Localization** - Support local language options for field workers
