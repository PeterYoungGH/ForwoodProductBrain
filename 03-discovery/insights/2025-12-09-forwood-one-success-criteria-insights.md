## Insight Statement

Forwood One migration success requires comprehensive, measurable criteria across technical readiness, migration execution, post-migration support, client satisfaction, and commercial milestones. The success criteria emphasize controlled, production-ready migrations with zero operational disruption, strong client experience, and phased commercial approach aligned to platform-specific timelines.

---

## Evidence

### Platform Readiness Requirements

**Technical Quality Standards:**
- V1, V2, V3 releases must be delivered on committed dates
- Zero P1 defects at client cut-over (critical quality gate)
- Maximum three P2 defects allowed, each requiring:
  - Documented workaround
  - Committed fix date
  - Clear communication line
- Critical workflow UAT pass rate above 95%
- Average load time under 2 seconds for safety-critical workflows

**Implication:** Platform must be production-ready with rigorous quality gates before any client migration.

### Migration Execution Standards

**Operational Excellence Requirements:**
- Zero operational disruption during client migrations
- All Safety+ and Safety+ legacy clients migrated in agreed sequence without rollback
- Ninety percent of users able to complete core workflows on day one

**Implication:** Migration execution must be flawless, with strong user readiness and zero disruption to safety-critical operations.

### Post-Migration Support Targets

**Support Performance Metrics:**
- Fifty percent reduction in P1–P3 tickets within 60 days post-migration
- Eighty percent of tickets resolved by CS or support without engineering by month two
- No bespoke development delivered outside approved change control

**Implication:** Support processes must be designed for efficiency and self-service, reducing engineering burden and maintaining platform stability.

### Client Satisfaction Goals

**Satisfaction and Advocacy Targets:**
- Client satisfaction score of 4.2/5 or higher within 60 days
- Zero executive-level escalations relating to performance or delivery
- Three or more clients provide testimonials supporting the public launch

**Implication:** Client experience must be exceptional from day one, with strong satisfaction and advocacy outcomes.

### Commercial Phased Migration

**Revenue Milestones by Platform:**
- **Safety+ phase:** Eighty percent of Safety+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2026
- **CRM+ phase:** Eighty percent of CRM+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q4 2027
- **Risk+ and Compliance+ phase:** Eighty percent of Risk+ and Compliance+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2027
- Forwood One becomes the default platform for all new sales by mid-2026
- Zero churn of Safety+ and CRM+ clients due to performance or migration issues

**Implication:** Commercial success requires careful phasing across platforms, with Forwood One becoming the default for new sales while maintaining zero churn.

---

## Context

These success criteria were provided by Meagan (Head of Solutions) and represent comprehensive requirements for Forwood One platform migrations. The criteria cover:

1. **Technical readiness** - Platform quality and performance standards
2. **Migration execution** - Operational excellence during migration
3. **Post-migration support** - Support efficiency and ticket reduction
4. **Client satisfaction** - Satisfaction scores and advocacy
5. **Commercial milestones** - Revenue assignment and churn prevention

The criteria are treated as input, not 100% locked-in plans, allowing for adjustment based on learnings and feedback. The phased commercial approach recognizes that different platforms have different migration timelines and requirements.

## Implications

### Product

**High Priority:**
- **Release delivery commitments** must be met - V1, V2, V3 releases on committed dates
- **Defect management** is critical - Zero P1 defects at cut-over, maximum three P2 defects with workarounds
- **Performance standards** must be met - 2-second load time for safety-critical workflows, 95% UAT pass rate
- **Platform stability** is essential - Zero operational disruption, zero churn due to performance issues

**Medium Priority:**
- **User readiness** - Ninety percent of users able to complete core workflows on day one
- **Support efficiency** - Fifty percent ticket reduction, eighty percent resolution without engineering
- **Change control** - No bespoke development outside approved change control

### Strategy

**High Priority:**
- **Phased commercial approach** - Platform-specific migration timelines (Safety+ Q2 2026, CRM+ Q4 2027, Risk+/Compliance+ Q2 2027)
- **Forwood One as default** - Becomes default platform for all new sales by mid-2026
- **Zero churn** - Prevent churn of Safety+ and CRM+ clients due to performance or migration issues

**Medium Priority:**
- **Client satisfaction measurement** - 4.2/5 satisfaction score within 60 days
- **Advocacy** - Three or more client testimonials for public launch
- **Escalation prevention** - Zero executive-level escalations

### Operations

**High Priority:**
- **Migration planning** - Must align with success criteria across all dimensions
- **Measurement processes** - Establish measurement and reporting for all success criteria
- **Migration playbooks** - Create playbooks aligned with success criteria
- **Support process design** - Design support processes for efficiency and self-service
