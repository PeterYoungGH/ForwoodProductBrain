# Experiment: AI-Driven Workflow Efficiency and Quality Impact

**Status:** Planned  
**Created:** 2026-01-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-03

## Overview

Test whether AI-driven product discovery and design workflow improves efficiency and quality without compromising outcomes, validating that AI tools effectively support discovery processes and enable the team to focus on understanding industry problems.

## Related Solution

Link to the solution this experiment tests:
- [AI-Driven Product Discovery Workflow](../04-solutions/active/ai-driven-product-discovery.md)

## Hypothesis

We believe that implementing an AI-driven product discovery and design workflow will result in improved efficiency and quality in discovery processes. We will know this is true when:
- Discovery processes are accelerated (≥20% time reduction)
- Team reports improved efficiency without quality compromise (≥80% agree)
- Team adoption rate is high (≥70% of team members actively using AI tools)
- Quality metrics remain stable or improve (no decrease in discovery quality)

## Methodology

### Experiment Type
Pilot Program with Before/After Comparison

### Approach

1. **Baseline Measurement (Week 1)**
   - Measure current discovery process time and quality metrics
   - Document current workflow and pain points
   - Establish baseline efficiency and quality metrics
   - Survey team on current discovery process challenges

2. **Pilot Implementation (Week 2-6)**
   - Implement AI-driven workflow for 2-3 discovery projects
   - Provide team training on AI tools and workflows
   - Track tool usage and adoption rates
   - Monitor process time and quality metrics
   - Collect qualitative feedback on workflow experience

3. **Comparison Analysis (Week 7-8)**
   - Compare efficiency metrics (time to discovery completion)
   - Compare quality metrics (insight quality, opportunity quality)
   - Analyze team adoption and usage patterns
   - Survey team on efficiency and quality impact
   - Document learnings and best practices

### Participants/Data
- **Team Members:** Product team members involved in discovery (5-10 people)
- **Discovery Projects:** 2-3 active discovery projects
- **Selection Criteria:**
  - Projects that represent typical discovery work
  - Team members willing to adopt new workflow

## Success Criteria

### Primary Success Criteria
- **Efficiency Improvement:** ≥20% reduction in discovery process time
- **Quality Maintenance:** Quality metrics remain stable or improve (no decrease)
- **Team Adoption:** ≥70% of team members actively using AI tools
- **Team Satisfaction:** ≥80% of team members report improved efficiency without quality compromise

### Secondary Success Criteria
- Team reports improved focus on understanding problems vs. solutions
- AI tools effectively support discovery processes
- Workflow integrates smoothly with existing processes
