# Experiment: URL Schema Customer Feedback and Acceptance

**Status:** Planned  
**Created:** 2026-01-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-03

## Overview

Test customer acceptance and feedback on the simplified `{client}.forwood.one` URL structure before full implementation, validating that new customers will accept the simplified URL structure and that the clean URLs positively impact client satisfaction and platform perception.

## Related Solution

Link to the solution this experiment tests:
- [Simplified URL Schema for Forwood One Platform](../04-solutions/active/url-schema-forwood-one.md)

## Hypothesis

We believe that implementing a simplified `{client}.forwood.one` URL structure will result in improved client satisfaction and platform professionalism. We will know this is true when:
- New customers express positive feedback on the clean URL structure (≥80% positive feedback)
- Customers report improved platform perception and professionalism (≥70% agree)
- No significant concerns raised about data residency or regional URL requirements
- Sales team reports that clean URLs support professional platform positioning

## Methodology

### Experiment Type
Customer Feedback Survey and Sales Team Interview

### Approach

1. **Design Phase (Week 1)**
   - Create visual mockups showing the new URL structure (`{client}.forwood.one`)
   - Prepare survey questions for customer feedback
   - Prepare interview guide for sales team

2. **Customer Feedback Collection (Week 2-3)**
   - Present URL structure mockups to 5-10 new customer prospects
   - Survey questions:
     - How do you perceive the `{client}.forwood.one` URL structure?
     - Does this align with your expectations for a modern SaaS platform?
     - Are you concerned about data residency without regional URLs?
     - How does this compare to your current platform URLs?
   - Collect qualitative feedback on URL structure perception

3. **Sales Team Interview (Week 2-3)**
   - Interview 3-5 sales team members
   - Questions:
     - How do clean URLs support your sales conversations?
     - Do prospects express concerns about URL structure?
     - Does URL simplicity help differentiate Forwood One?
   - Collect feedback on sales enablement value

4. **Analysis Phase (Week 4)**
   - Analyze customer feedback for themes and concerns
   - Analyze sales team feedback for positioning value
   - Identify any blockers or concerns that need addressing

### Participants/Data
- **Customer Prospects:** 5-10 new customer prospects (not yet migrated)
- **Sales Team:** 3-5 sales team members
- **Selection Criteria:** 
  - Prospects: New customers considering Forwood One, diverse industries
  - Sales Team: Active sales team members with customer-facing experience

## Success Criteria

### Primary Success Criteria
- **Customer Feedback:** ≥80% of prospects express positive feedback on clean URL structure
- **Platform Perception:** ≥70% of prospects agree that clean URLs improve platform professionalism
- **Data Residency Concerns:** ≤20% of prospects express concerns about data residency without regional URLs
- **Sales Enablement:** ≥80% of sales team members report that clean URLs support professional positioning

### Secondary Success Criteria
- No significant blockers identified that would prevent implementation
- Customer feedback aligns with product direction (URL simplicity as strategic goal)
- Sales team reports improved confidence in platform positioning
