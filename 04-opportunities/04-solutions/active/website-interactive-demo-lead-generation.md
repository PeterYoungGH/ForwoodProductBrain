## Overview

## Solution Description

This solution involves creating an interactive, live demo of Forwood One that is embedded directly on the Forwood website. The demo allows prospects to:

- Explore Forwood One's core capabilities without requiring a sales call
- Experience the platform with realistic dummy data
- Understand product value through hands-on interaction
- Self-qualify by exploring features relevant to their needs
- Convert to leads by requesting more information or a sales conversation

**Key Features:**
- Live, interactive demo environment (not just video or screenshots)
- Pre-populated with realistic dummy data for safety-critical workflows
- Accessible directly from website without registration barriers
- Guided tour or self-exploration options
- Lead capture mechanism integrated into demo experience
- Analytics to track engagement and conversion

**Technical Approach:**
- Embedded demo environment (could be sandbox instance or demo-specific deployment)
- Website integration for seamless access
- Dummy data that represents realistic use cases
- Lead capture forms integrated at key interaction points
- Analytics tracking for engagement and conversion metrics

This solution addresses the need for product-led growth and lead generation by:

- **Reducing Friction:** Prospects can explore product value without committing to a sales call
