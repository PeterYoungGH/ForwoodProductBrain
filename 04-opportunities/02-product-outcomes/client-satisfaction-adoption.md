# Product Outcome: Client Satisfaction and Adoption

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-12-09  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09

## Overview

Achieve high client satisfaction scores, zero executive escalations, and strong client advocacy through successful migrations and platform adoption.

## Description

This product outcome measures client satisfaction and adoption following migrations to Forwood One. It focuses on achieving high satisfaction scores, preventing executive escalations, and generating client testimonials that support public launch. This outcome directly supports the business outcome of successful migrations by ensuring that clients are satisfied with the migration experience and platform capabilities.

The outcome covers satisfaction measurement, escalation prevention, and client advocacy. Success in this outcome ensures that migrations build client confidence and generate positive outcomes that support future sales and expansion.

## Target Metrics

- **Client Satisfaction Score:** 4.2/5 or higher within 60 days
- **Executive Escalations:** Zero executive-level escalations relating to performance or delivery
- **Client Testimonials:** Three or more clients provide testimonials supporting the public launch

## Current State

- **Satisfaction Measurement:** Client satisfaction survey process being defined
- **Escalation Management:** Executive escalation prevention processes being developed
- **Testimonial Program:** Client testimonial collection process being planned
- **Public Launch:** Public launch planning in progress
