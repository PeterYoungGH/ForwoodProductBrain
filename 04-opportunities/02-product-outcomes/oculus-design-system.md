# Product Outcome: Forwood One Design System

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-01-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-27

## Overview

Deliver a new design system for Forwood One that enables a cohesive, scalable, and accessible user experience across all product interfaces.

## Description

A comprehensive design system is essential for creating a consistent, high-quality user experience across the Forwood One platform. This product outcome focuses on delivering a new design system that provides cohesive design patterns, components, and guidelines that enable teams to build interfaces that are scalable, accessible, and aligned with the Forwood One brand and user experience goals.

The design system will support the broader product development effort by providing reusable components and clear guidelines, reducing design and development time while ensuring consistency. This outcome directly supports usability, customer experience, and the overall quality of the Forwood One platform.
