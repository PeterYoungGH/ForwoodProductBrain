# Product Outcome: Forwood One NPS Score

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-01-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-27

## Overview

Achieve an Forwood One product NPS score of 20 from initial customers, demonstrating strong customer satisfaction and likelihood to recommend the platform.

## Description

Net Promoter Score (NPS) is a key product metric that measures customer satisfaction and likelihood to recommend the product. Achieving an NPS score of 20 from initial customers indicates strong product-market fit and positive customer experience. This product outcome directly supports the business outcome of launching Forwood One and acquiring new customers by ensuring that early adopters have a positive experience and are likely to recommend the platform to others.

This outcome will be measured through NPS surveys conducted with Forwood One customers, with the target of achieving a score of 20. The NPS survey will be conducted in Q4 2026, including all Forwood One clients.
