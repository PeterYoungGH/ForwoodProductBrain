# Product Outcome: Post-Migration Support Performance

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-12-09  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09

## Overview

Deliver exceptional post-migration support that reduces ticket volume, resolves issues efficiently, and maintains governance standards.

## Description

This product outcome measures the quality and efficiency of post-migration support. It focuses on reducing ticket volume through platform stability, resolving issues quickly through effective support processes, and maintaining governance by preventing unauthorized bespoke development. This outcome directly supports the business outcome of successful migrations by ensuring that post-migration support is effective and sustainable.

The outcome covers ticket reduction, support resolution efficiency, and governance compliance. Success in this outcome ensures that customers receive high-quality support after migration and that the platform remains stable and maintainable.

## Target Metrics

- **Ticket Reduction:** Fifty percent reduction in P1–P3 tickets within 60 days post-migration
- **Support Resolution:** Eighty percent of tickets resolved by CS or support without engineering by month two
- **Bespoke Development:** No bespoke development delivered outside approved change control

## Current State

- **Support Processes:** Post-migration support processes being defined
- **Ticket Management:** Ticket reduction targets being established
- **Governance Framework:** Change control and governance processes being developed
- **Support Capability:** CS and support team capability being built

## Notes

- Support resolution target applies to tickets that don't require engineering changes
- Bespoke development must go through approved change control framework
- Governance compliance is critical for platform maintainability
