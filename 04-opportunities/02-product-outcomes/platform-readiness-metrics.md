# Product Outcome: Platform Readiness Metrics

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-12-09  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09

## Overview

Ensure Forwood One platform meets all technical readiness criteria for client migrations, including release delivery, defect management, UAT pass rates, and performance standards.

## Description

This product outcome measures the technical readiness of the Forwood One platform to support client migrations. It focuses on ensuring that platform releases are delivered on time, defects are managed appropriately, critical workflows pass UAT at high rates, and performance meets safety-critical requirements. This outcome directly supports the business outcome of successful migrations by ensuring the platform is production-ready before cut-over.

The outcome covers release delivery commitments, defect severity management, workflow testing success, and performance benchmarks. Success in this outcome ensures that technical risks are minimized during migrations and that the platform can support safety-critical operations from day one.

## Target Metrics

- **Release Delivery:** V1, V2, and V3 released on committed dates
- **P1 Defects at Cut-Over:** Zero P1 defects
- **P2 Defects at Cut-Over:** Maximum three P2 defects (each with documented workaround, committed fix date, and communication line)
- **Critical Workflow UAT Pass Rate:** Above 95%
- **Average Load Time:** Under 2 seconds for safety-critical workflows

## Current State

- **Platform Status:** Forwood One in development
- **Release Planning:** V1, V2, V3 releases planned with committed dates
- **UAT Process:** Critical workflow UAT process being defined
- **Performance Testing:** Load time targets being established and tested
