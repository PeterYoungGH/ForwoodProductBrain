# Product Outcome: Forwood One Customer Experience

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-01-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-27

## Overview

Ensure a positive (via survey) implementation, onboarding, training, and support experience for Forwood One, creating a smooth and successful customer journey from initial engagement through ongoing use.

## Description

Customer experience across implementation, onboarding, training, and support is critical for customer success and satisfaction. This product outcome focuses on ensuring that every touchpoint in the customer journey - from initial implementation through ongoing support - is positive, effective, and creates value for customers.

A positive customer experience directly supports customer retention, reduces churn, increases product adoption, and creates advocates who recommend the platform to others. This outcome is measured through customer surveys and is essential for both new customer acquisition and successful customer migration from legacy platforms.

## Target Metrics

- **Implementation Experience:** Positive (via survey)
- **Onboarding Experience:** Positive (via survey)
- **Training Experience:** Positive (via survey)
- **Support Experience:** Positive (via survey)
- **Overall Customer Experience:** Positive across all touchpoints

## Current State

- **Current State:** Customer experience programs to be developed and implemented
- **Target State:** Positive experience across implementation, onboarding, training, and support
- **Status:** Planning phase
- **Measurement:** Customer surveys to measure experience
