# Business Outcome: Forwood One Platform Launch and New Customer Acquisition

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-01-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-27

## Overview

Launch the Forwood One platform and win 5 new customer deals (new logos) to achieve commercial traction and establish Forwood One as a viable platform in the market.

## Description

This business outcome focuses on successfully launching the Forwood One platform and acquiring new customers. Achieving 5 new deals represents the initial commercial traction needed to validate the platform in the market and establish a foundation for growth. This outcome is critical for demonstrating market demand and building momentum for the platform.

The launch requires coordination across product, engineering, commercial, and operations teams to ensure the platform is ready for customer acquisition and that sales processes are effective. Success in this outcome validates the strategic investment in Forwood One and creates a foundation for future growth.

## Target Metrics

- **New Customer Deals:** 5 new logos (target)
- **Sales Pipeline:** >15 deals in Forwood One pipeline by December 2025
- **First Deals Signed:** February/March 2026
- **New Client Onboarding:** April/May 2026

## Current State

- **Platform Status:** Forwood One in development, MVP launch planned for Q1 2025
- **Sales Pipeline:** Building pipeline, target of >15 deals by December 2025
- **Current Deals:** 0 (target: 5 new logos)
- **Launch Timeline:** Q1 2025 (MVP), with first deals expected Q3 2026
