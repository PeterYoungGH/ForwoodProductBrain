# Business Outcome: Strategic Integration Plan

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-01-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-27

## Overview

Build a strategic integration plan across product pillars of CRM, Risk, and Compliance to support unified product architecture and enable seamless customer experience across all product areas.

## Description

This business outcome focuses on creating a comprehensive strategic integration plan that unifies the CRM, Risk, and Compliance product pillars within the Forwood One platform. The integration plan is essential for achieving a unified product architecture that enables customers to work seamlessly across all product areas without fragmentation or silos.

The strategic integration plan will guide how these three product pillars work together within Forwood One, ensuring consistent user experience, shared infrastructure, and aligned functionality. This outcome supports the broader vision of Forwood One as a unified platform rather than separate products.

## Target Metrics

- **Integration Plan Completion:** Comprehensive strategic integration plan documented
- **Platform Unification:** CRM, Risk, and Compliance pillars integrated into Forwood One
- **Architecture Alignment:** Unified product architecture supporting all three pillars
- **Customer Experience:** Seamless experience across CRM, Risk, and Compliance within Forwood One

## Current State

- **Current State:** CRM, Risk, and Compliance exist as separate product pillars
- **Target State:** Unified integration within Forwood One platform
- **Integration Status:** Planning phase, strategic plan to be developed
- **Architecture Vision:** Unified product architecture supporting all pillars
