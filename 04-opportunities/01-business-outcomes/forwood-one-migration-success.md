# Business Outcome: Forwood One Migration Success

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-12-09  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-09

## Overview

Achieve successful migration of all Safety+, CRM+, Risk+, and Compliance+ clients to Forwood One platform with zero operational disruption, high client satisfaction, and strong commercial outcomes.

## Description

This business outcome focuses on delivering world-class migration experiences for all customers transitioning from legacy platforms to Forwood One. Success is measured across multiple dimensions: platform readiness, migration execution, support quality, client satisfaction, and commercial position. The outcome ensures that migrations are controlled, production-ready, and confidence-building for customers while achieving strategic commercial goals.

The migration success criteria cover platform technical readiness, migration execution quality, post-migration support, client satisfaction, and commercial milestones. This outcome is critical for maintaining customer relationships, reducing churn, and establishing Forwood One as the default platform for all new sales.

## Target Metrics

### Platform Readiness
- **V1, V2, V3 Releases:** Released on committed dates
- **P1 Defects at Cut-Over:** Zero P1 defects
- **P2 Defects at Cut-Over:** Maximum three P2 defects (each with documented workaround, committed fix date, and communication line)
- **Critical Workflow UAT Pass Rate:** Above 95%
- **Average Load Time:** Under 2 seconds for safety-critical workflows

### Migration Outcomes
- **Operational Disruption:** Zero operational disruption during client migrations
- **Migration Completion:** All Safety+ and Safety+ legacy clients migrated in agreed sequence without rollback
- **Day-One User Capability:** Ninety percent of users able to complete core workflows on day one

### Support and Stability
- **Ticket Reduction:** Fifty percent reduction in P1–P3 tickets within 60 days post-migration
- **Support Resolution:** Eighty percent of tickets resolved by CS or support without engineering by month two
- **Bespoke Development:** No bespoke development delivered outside approved change control

### Scope and Governance
- **Change Requests:** All change requests managed through the approved framework
- **Unauthorised Scope Deviations:** Fewer than five unauthorised scope deviations annually
- **Engineering Velocity:** Engineering velocity unaffected by more than ten percent from client demands

### Client Satisfaction
- **Client Satisfaction Score:** 4.2/5 or higher within 60 days
- **Executive Escalations:** Zero executive-level escalations relating to performance or delivery
- **Client Testimonials:** Three or more clients provide testimonials supporting the public launch

### Internal Readiness
- **Team Education:** Sales, CS, Support, and Academy fully educated before first migration
- **Onboarding Playbook:** Onboarding playbook used consistently for all clients
- **Tenant Creation:** Tenant creation and onboarding commenced within three business days for new clients

### Data and Reporting
- **Continuous Reporting:** Continuous reporting without history loss or data gaps
- **Reporting Platform:** QuickSight or embedded reporting operating from a unified data model
- **Manual Workarounds:** No manual reporting workarounds beyond two weeks of hypercare

### Commercial Position (Phased Migration)
- **Safety+ Phase:** Eighty percent of Safety+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2026
- **CRM+ Phase:** Eighty percent of CRM+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q4 2027
- **Risk+ and Compliance+ Phase:** Eighty percent of Risk+ and Compliance+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2027
- **New Sales Default:** Forwood One becomes the default platform for all new sales by mid-2026
- **Client Churn:** Zero churn of Safety+ and CRM+ clients due to performance or migration issues

### Organisational Alignment
- **Communication Streams:** All four communication streams delivered on time with consistent messaging
- **SC Gateway Approvals:** SC gateway approvals achieved without rework
- **Governance Adoption:** Governance and change control adopted across all delivery teams

### Post-Migration Performance
- **Client Operational Activity:** Ninety-five percent of client operational activity occurring in Forwood One by end-2027
- **Hypercare Exit:** Clients exit Hypercare within eight weeks and transition to BAU support

## Current State

- **Platform Status:** Forwood One in development, V1, V2, V3 releases planned
- **Migration Planning:** Migration frameworks and playbooks being developed
- **Client Readiness:** Internal teams preparing for first migrations
- **Current Migrations:** Planning phase for Safety+ and CRM+ migrations
