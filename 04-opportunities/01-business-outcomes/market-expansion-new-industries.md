# Business Outcome: Market Expansion into New Industries

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-11-28  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-28

## Overview

Expand Forwood's market presence beyond mining into new high-risk industries including Oil & Gas, Chemicals, Construction, Forestry, and Utilities to address the global workplace fatality problem and grow market share.

## Description

Forwood CRM has achieved exceptional success in the mining industry with a 99.6% success rate and deployment at 1000+ sites globally. The global workplace fatality problem is significant, with 330,000 annual workplace fatalities and 2.6 million work-related deaths. This business outcome focuses on expanding Forwood's reach into additional high-risk industries where CRM's fatality prevention capabilities can have transformative impact.

The expansion strategy leverages Forwood's proven track record, including customer success stories from mining (Rio Tinto, Newcrest) and utilities (HEDNO), to enter new markets. The goal is to make CRM available to any high-risk industry, any country, any language, addressing the ILO's finding that nearly three million workers die annually from work-related accidents and diseases.
