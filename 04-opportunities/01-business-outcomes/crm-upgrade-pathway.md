# Business Outcome: CRM Upgrade Pathway

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2024-11-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-28

## Overview

Create an upgrade pathway for CRM+ customers to migrate to Forwood One platform, supporting unified product architecture.

## Description

CRM+ is another product line that will be integrated into Forwood One. This business outcome focuses on creating a viable pathway for existing CRM+ customers to upgrade to the Forwood One platform, ensuring a smooth transition and customer retention. The upgrade plan supports the unified product architecture vision, with Forwood One V3 ready for CRM migration in Q4 2026.
