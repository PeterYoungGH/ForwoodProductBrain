# Business Outcome: Safety Plus Upgrade

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2024-11-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-27

## Overview

Create a viable upgrade pathway for five customers currently on our legacy Safety Plus platform to migrate them to Forwood One, transitioning all customers in FY26.

## Description

We have five customers currently using our legacy Safety Plus platform. This business outcome focuses on creating a clear, viable pathway for these customers to upgrade to Forwood One, ensuring we retain these customers and successfully transition them to our new platform. The migration will begin in Q3 2026 (January-March) with the goal of transitioning all Safety+ customers to Forwood One within FY26.

## Target Metrics

- **Number of customers migrated:** 5 customers
- **Migration completion rate:** 100% of Safety Plus customers
- **Customer retention:** Maintain all 5 customers through migration

## Current State

- **Legacy platform:** Safety Plus (5 customers)
- **Target platform:** Forwood One
- **Migration status:** Planning phase, migration to begin Q3 2026
