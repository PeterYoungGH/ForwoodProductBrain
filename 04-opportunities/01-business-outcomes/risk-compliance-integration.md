# Business Outcome: Risk and Compliance Platform Integration

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2024-11-27  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-04

## Overview

Integration of our Risk+ platform into Forwood One, supporting unified product architecture.

## Description

Integrate the existing Risk+ platform functionality into Forwood One, creating a unified platform that includes risk and compliance capabilities alongside other product features. The integration will be validated in Q2 2025 with UAT and possible migration, supporting the strategic integration plan across product pillars.

**Strategic Context:** Compliance is becoming one of the most powerful forces shaping enterprise purchasing decisions. Buyers are no longer asking "are you compliant?" but "what does your product enable us to comply with?" The most competitive SaaS vendors are treating compliance like a feature set, building capabilities that allow customers to meet regulatory requirements through the platform itself. Compliance features that are visible and user-facing become procurement enablers, shortening sales cycles, reducing legal friction, and increasing customer stickiness, especially in compliance-heavy sectors like mining, utilities, and construction.
