# Opportunity: Product-Led Growth via Interactive Website Demo

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2025-12-17  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-17  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

Prospects need a way to experience Forwood One's value before committing to a sales conversation, enabling product-led growth where the product itself becomes a marketing channel for lead generation.

## Customer Problem

Prospects and potential customers are experiencing:
- High friction in the sales process requiring commitment to a sales call before seeing product value
- Lack of self-service options to explore Forwood One capabilities
- Need to understand product value before engaging with sales team
- Desire for low-friction entry points to experience the platform

This problem is particularly relevant because:
- Product-led growth (PLG) strategies can reduce customer acquisition costs
- Interactive demos enable prospects to experience value before commitment
- Self-service exploration can generate qualified leads
- Website-based demos create scalable lead generation channel
