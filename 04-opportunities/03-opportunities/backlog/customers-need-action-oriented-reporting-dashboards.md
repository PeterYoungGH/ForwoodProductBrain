# Opportunity: Customers Need Action-Oriented Reporting and Dashboards

**Status:** Backlog  
**Created:** 2026-01-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-03  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

Customers need reporting and dashboards that are action-oriented, easier to interpret, and enable immediate action based on results. Current dashboards are overly simple, lack predictive capacity, and don't enable customers to take clear actions from data.

## Customer Problem

Customers struggle with reporting and dashboards that:
- Are overly simple and lack predictive capacity for accidents
- Don't enable immediate action based on results
- Are difficult to interpret and don't provide clear insights
- Don't show action-oriented outputs that enable decision-making
- Have inconsistencies between Safety+ and dashboards
- Don't enable production of "printable" verification records (PDF-like) with responses and signatures

**What Customers Need:**
- Action-oriented dashboards that enable immediate action based on results
- Easier to interpret dashboards with clear insights
- Predictive capacity for accidents (main differentiating element)
- Consistent data between Safety+ and dashboards
- Printable verification records (PDF-like) with responses and signatures
- Ability to derive clear actions from high data volumes
