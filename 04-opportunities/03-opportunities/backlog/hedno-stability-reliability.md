# Opportunity: Hedno Stability and Reliability

**Status:** Backlog  
**Created:** 2025-12-08  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-08  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

Hedno customers have deep sensitivity to breakages in Safety+; unstable releases have caused their workforce to reject the tool, creating significant adoption challenges.

## Customer Problem

Hedno customers are experiencing:
- Deep sensitivity to breakages in Safety+ platform
- Unstable releases that have caused their workforce to reject the tool
- Concerns about reliability and stability that impact platform adoption
- Need for reassurance that release processes have been tightened

This problem is particularly acute because:
- Their original purchase driver was Geomap, not CRM
- They have an extremely hierarchical organisational culture requiring precise language and framing
- They heavily use DSS-built content rather than Forwood content, requiring careful mapping

## Insights

Key insights that led to identifying this opportunity:
- Hedno has deep sensitivity to breakages due to past unstable releases
- Workforce rejection of the tool is a critical adoption challenge
- Release processes must emphasize stability and quality to rebuild trust
- Focus on stability, reliability, and ease of use (particularly for field users)

## Notes

- Hedno requires focus on stability, reliability, and ease of use
- Reassure that release processes have been tightened: regression testing, QA governance, and gated deployments
- Begin shifting mindset from bespoke requests to future configurability
- Acknowledge legacy issues in Safety+ without undermining trust
- Migration timeline: mid-2026 is the safest headline, but messaging needs flexibility
