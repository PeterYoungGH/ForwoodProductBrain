# Opportunity: Customers Need Domain-Specific AI That Reduces Work, Not Adds It

**Status:** Backlog  
**Created:** 2026-01-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-03  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

Customers need AI capabilities that reduce work and save time, not add complexity. They want domain-specific AI models that provide AI evidence mapping, AI action recommendation, AI duplicate action detection, and predictive fatality insights—capabilities that competitors lack but Forwood can deliver.

## Customer Problem

Customers are frustrated with AI that:
- Adds work rather than reducing it (more dashboards, more complexity)
- Lacks domain-specific intelligence relevant to their industry
- Doesn't provide actionable insights (analytics without recommendations)
- Requires significant setup or configuration effort
- Doesn't leverage Forwood's decade of fatality prevention data

**What Customers Want:**
- AI evidence mapping that saves time in investigations
- AI action recommendation that reduces decision-making burden
- AI duplicate action detection that prevents redundant work
- Predictive fatality insights from Forwood's decade of data
- Domain-specific models (mining, utilities, construction) that outperform generic AI
