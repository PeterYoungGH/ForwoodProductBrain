# Opportunity: Customers Need Mobile-First Offline Experiences for Field Workers

**Status:** Backlog  
**Created:** 2026-01-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-03  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

Field workers (operators, supervisors, incident reporters) need mobile-first experiences that function reliably offline in remote and harsh environments. They struggle with limited connectivity, environmental constraints (sunlight, gloves, noise), and need fast, intuitive workflows that work when connectivity fails.

## Customer Problem

Field workers experience frustration with mobile experiences that:
- Fail when connectivity is limited or unreliable in remote/harsh environments
- Are difficult to use in environmental conditions (sunlight glare, gloves, noise)
- Have too many steps or unclear workflows for simple tasks
- Perform slowly or have usability issues that cause adoption regression to paper

**Critical Requirements:**
- Must function reliably offline with auto-sync on reconnect
- Large touch targets, minimal typing, clear visual feedback
- Short, guided workflows optimized for field conditions
- Fast, intuitive mobile workflows with clear offline handling
- Local language options for diverse workforces
