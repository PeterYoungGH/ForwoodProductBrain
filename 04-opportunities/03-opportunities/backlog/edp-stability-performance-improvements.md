# Opportunity: EDP Stability and Performance Improvements

**Status:** Backlog  
**Created:** 2025-12-08  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-17  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

EDP customers experience frustration due to stability problems, performance issues, and regression of previously resolved issues after each update, creating setbacks and conveying an image of unreliability to end users.

## Customer Problem

EDP customers are experiencing:
- Stability problems reported by end users, discouraging use and risking job performance
- Issues previously resolved or functioning correctly begin to fail again after each new update
- Performance issues that impact user confidence and platform adoption
- Perception of unreliability that undermines trust in the platform

This problem is particularly acute given EDP's recent fatality and their perception of performance failure. The customer believes they already operate at very high safety maturity, so any messaging that implies otherwise must be avoided.
