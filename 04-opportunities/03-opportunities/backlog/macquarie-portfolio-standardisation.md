# Opportunity: Macquarie Portfolio Standardisation

**Status:** Backlog  
**Created:** 2025-12-08  
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-08  
**Priority:** Medium

## Overview

Both EDP and Hedno operate under the Macquarie portfolio, creating an opportunity to standardise across utilities and align messaging around best-practice CRM methods from mining into utilities.

## Customer Problem

Macquarie portfolio utilities (EDP and Hedno) need:
- Standardisation across utilities to reduce complexity and improve efficiency
- Alignment with best-practice CRM methods from mining industry
- Consistent platform experience across portfolio companies
- Clear narrative about standardisation objectives and Forwood One as the platform to achieve that

This opportunity supports strategic positioning by:
- Framing standardisation as a key objective across Macquarie portfolio
- Positioning Forwood One as the platform designed to achieve standardisation
- Creating alignment around shared objectives without implying Macquarie issued directives
