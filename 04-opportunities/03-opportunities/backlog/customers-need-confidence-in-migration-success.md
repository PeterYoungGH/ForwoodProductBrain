# Opportunity: Customers Need Confidence in Migration Success and Platform Readiness

**Status:** Active  
**Created:** 2026-01-03  
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-03  
**Priority:** High

## Overview

Customers need confidence that Forwood One platform migrations will be successful, production-ready, and meet their operational requirements. They need assurance that the platform will be stable, performant, and ready for their critical safety operations before committing to migration.

## Customer Problem

Customers are concerned about migration risk and platform readiness, especially given:

**Platform Readiness Concerns:**
- Will the platform be production-ready with zero critical defects at go-live?
- Will performance meet requirements for safety-critical workflows (e.g., 2-second load times)?
- Will the platform be stable enough to support zero operational disruption during migration?
- Can we trust that releases will be delivered on committed dates?

**Migration Execution Concerns:**
- Will migration cause operational disruption to our safety-critical work?
- Will users be able to complete core workflows on day one?
- What happens if something goes wrong during migration?
- How do we know the platform is ready for our specific use case?

**Post-Migration Support Concerns:**
- Will support be able to resolve issues quickly without engineering involvement?
- Will ticket volume decrease after migration, or will we face more issues?
- How do we know the platform will meet our satisfaction requirements?
- What happens if we're not satisfied after migration?

**Commercial and Timeline Concerns:**
- When will our platform be ready for migration?
- How do we know the migration timeline is realistic?
- What happens if migration is delayed or doesn't meet success criteria?
- How do we ensure zero churn due to performance or migration issues?

These concerns are particularly acute for customers with:
- High contractor populations and limited face-to-face engagement
- Field users who resist technology when tools fail
- Critical operational deadlines (e.g., mid-January ramp-up periods)
- Recent negative experiences with platform stability or performance

## Insights

Key insights that led to identifying this opportunity:
- Forwood One migration success requires comprehensive, measurable criteria across technical readiness, migration execution, post-migration support, client satisfaction, and commercial milestones
- Success criteria emphasize controlled, production-ready migrations with zero operational disruption, strong client experience, and phased commercial approach
- Customers need assurance that platform will be stable, performant, and ready for critical safety operations before committing to migration
- EDP emphasized need for tool to be stable and "fully running" for mid-January ramp, indicating critical operational dependencies
- Customers are concerned about migration risk, especially given recent negative experiences with platform stability or performance

## Notes

- Success criteria provided by Meagan (Head of Solutions) represent comprehensive requirements for Forwood One platform migrations
- Criteria are treated as input, not 100% locked-in plans, allowing for adjustment based on learnings and feedback
- Criteria cover comprehensive dimensions of migration success: technical readiness, migration execution, post-migration support, client satisfaction, and commercial milestones
- Phased commercial approach recognizes that different platforms have different migration timelines and requirements
- Criteria emphasize controlled, production-ready migrations with strong client experience
