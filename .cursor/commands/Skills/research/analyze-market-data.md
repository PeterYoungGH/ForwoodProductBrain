# Analyze Market Data

**Category:** Research
**Strategic Theme:** N/A
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** 2 (Insight Extraction)

## Purpose

Analyze competitive and market data to extract insights about market position, competitive landscape, industry trends, and strategic opportunities.

## Inputs Required

- **Market Data:** Reports, competitor info, industry analysis
- **Analysis Focus:** What aspect of the market to analyze
- **Current Position:** Forwood's current market position
- **Strategic Context:** Relevant strategic themes and goals

## Process Steps

### Step 1: Categorize Market Data

Classify the data type:
- **Competitive:** Direct and indirect competitors
- **Industry:** Broader industry trends and dynamics
- **Customer:** Market segments and buyer behavior
- **Technology:** Technology trends and disruption
- **Regulatory:** Compliance and regulatory changes

### Step 2: Extract Key Data Points

For competitive analysis:
| Data Point | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Competitors | Who they are, market position |
| Offerings | Products, features, pricing |
| Strengths | Where they excel |
| Weaknesses | Where they fall short |
| Strategy | Their apparent strategic direction |
| Differentiators | What makes them unique |

For industry analysis:
| Data Point | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Market Size | TAM, SAM, SOM |
| Growth Rate | Market growth trends |
| Key Trends | Major industry movements |
| Disruptions | Emerging threats or opportunities |
| Regulations | Compliance requirements |

### Step 3: Assess Competitive Position

Evaluate Forwood's position:
- **Strengths vs. Competitors:** Where we win
- **Gaps:** Where competitors are ahead
- **Opportunities:** Unserved needs or underserved segments
- **Threats:** Competitive or market threats

### Step 4: Identify Strategic Implications

Connect findings to strategic themes:
- How does this affect Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility)?
- How does this affect Theme 2 (Data to Action)?
- How does this affect Theme 3 (Credibility & Trust)?
- How does this affect Theme 4 (Controls That Work)?
- How does this affect Theme 5 (Standardization)?

### Step 5: Generate Recommendations

Propose actions based on analysis:
- Opportunities to pursue
- Threats to mitigate
- Competitive responses
- Strategic adjustments

## Confidence Scoring

- **High:** Multiple reliable sources, recent data, clear patterns
- **Medium:** Limited sources, some dated data, general patterns
- **Low:** Single source, old data, speculative

## Example

**Input:** Safety software competitive landscape report from industry analyst.

**Output:** Structured analysis showing competitor positioning, market gaps in CRM space, and opportunities for Forwood One differentiation.
