# Analyze Safety Culture Indicators

**Category:** Safety Domain
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 3 (Credibility & Trust Through Systems)
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** 2, 4 (Insight Extraction, Opportunity Validation)

## Purpose
Measure and analyze indicators of safety culture health, particularly trust and credibility.

## Inputs Required
- Survey data
- Reporting rates
- Near-miss data
- Worker feedback

## Process Steps
1. **Assess reporting willingness** - Are people reporting?
2. **Measure trust indicators** - Do workers trust the system?
3. **Evaluate leader behaviors** - What are leaders doing?
4. **Check blame culture signals** - Is it safe to speak up?
5. **Track trend over time** - Improving or declining?

## Output Format
Culture indicator assessment with trends and improvement recommendations.
