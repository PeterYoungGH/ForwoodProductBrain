# Design Governance Visibility

**Category:** Safety Domain
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance)
**Autonomy Level:** Supervised
**Workflow Stages:** 5, 7 (Solution Exploration, Roadmap Inclusion)

## Purpose
Design board-level visibility features that enable governance and oversight of fatal risk.

## Inputs Required
- Governance requirements
- Board reporting needs
- Risk data available
- VoC from executives

## Process Steps
1. **Identify governance requirements** - What must board see?
2. **Design risk visibility** - How to show risk status
3. **Enable drill-down** - From summary to detail
4. **Support accountability** - Who owns what?
5. **Meet legal requirements** - Due diligence compliance

## Output Format
Governance visibility design with dashboard specs and reporting requirements.
