# Assess Fatal Risk Signal

**Category:** Safety Domain
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance)
**Autonomy Level:** Supervised
**Workflow Stages:** 2, 4 (Insight Extraction, Opportunity Validation)

## Purpose
Distinguish SIF (Serious Injury/Fatality) signals from routine safety data to prioritize fatal risk prevention.

## Inputs Required
- Safety data or incident reports
- Risk categorization framework
- Historical SIF data

## Process Steps
1. **Apply SIF potential criteria** - Could this result in fatality?
2. **Assess energy transfer** - What energy sources are present?
3. **Evaluate control effectiveness** - Are controls preventing harm?
4. **Check leading indicators** - What signals precede SIF?
5. **Prioritize by potential** - Not by frequency

## Output Format
SIF signal assessment with risk classification and recommended actions.
