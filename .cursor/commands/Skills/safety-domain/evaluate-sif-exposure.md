# Evaluate SIF Exposure

**Category:** Safety Domain
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility & Governance)
**Autonomy Level:** Supervised
**Workflow Stages:** 2, 4 (Insight Extraction, Opportunity Validation)

## Purpose
Assess exposure to Serious Injury/Fatality risk in work activities or scenarios.

## Inputs Required
- Work activity description
- Energy sources present
- Control measures in place
- Historical data

## Process Steps
1. **Identify energy sources** - What could cause harm?
2. **Assess exposure frequency** - How often are workers exposed?
3. **Evaluate barrier effectiveness** - Do controls actually work?
4. **Consider human factors** - What could go wrong?
5. **Rate SIF potential** - Low/Medium/High/Critical

## Output Format
SIF exposure assessment with risk rating and control recommendations.
