# Assess Control vs Audit Gap

**Category:** Safety Domain
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 4 (Controls That Actually Work)
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** 2, 4 (Insight Extraction, Opportunity Validation)

## Purpose
Find gaps between what audits show and what actually happens - the "controls on paper vs reality" problem.

## Inputs Required
- Audit results
- Field observations
- Incident data
- Worker feedback

## Process Steps
1. **Compare audit findings to incidents** - Do they align?
2. **Gather field reality** - What actually happens?
3. **Identify paper-only controls** - What exists only in documents?
4. **Assess gap severity** - How dangerous is the gap?
5. **Recommend closure** - How to close gaps

## Output Format
Control vs audit gap assessment with gap inventory and recommendations.
