# Assess Standardization Gaps

**Category:** Safety Domain
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 5 (Standardization Without Rigidity)
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** 2, 4 (Insight Extraction, Opportunity Validation)

## Purpose
Identify inconsistency across sites or teams that creates risk or inefficiency.

## Inputs Required
- Site/team configurations
- Process documentation
- Terminology in use
- Incident data by location

## Process Steps
1. **Inventory variations** - What differs across sites?
2. **Assess risk of variation** - Does it create danger?
3. **Identify efficiency loss** - Does it slow things down?
4. **Prioritize gaps** - Which matter most?
5. **Recommend standardization** - What should be consistent?

## Output Format
Standardization gap assessment with prioritized gaps and recommendations.
