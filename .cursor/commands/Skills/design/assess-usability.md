# Assess Usability

**Category:** Design
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 3 (Credibility & Trust)
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** 5, 6 (Solution Exploration, Experimentation)

## Purpose
Evaluate designs or solutions against usability heuristics and best practices.

## Inputs Required
- Design or solution to evaluate
- Usability heuristics to apply
- User context and needs

## Process Steps
1. **Apply Nielsen's heuristics** - 10 usability heuristics
2. **Check accessibility** - WCAG compliance
3. **Evaluate learnability** - How easy to learn?
4. **Assess efficiency** - How fast for experienced users?
5. **Rate error prevention** - How well does it prevent mistakes?

## Output Format
Usability assessment with heuristic scores, issues, and recommendations.
