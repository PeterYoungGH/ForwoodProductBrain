# Create Decision Brief

**Category:** Communication
**Strategic Theme:** N/A
**Autonomy Level:** Supervised
**Workflow Stages:** 4, 5, 7 (Decision points)

## Purpose
Prepare decision documents that present options with trade-offs for stakeholder decisions.

## Inputs Required
- Decision to be made
- Options considered
- Analysis and trade-offs
- Recommendation

## Process Steps
1. **Frame the decision** - What exactly needs to be decided?
2. **Present options** - 2-4 viable options
3. **Analyze trade-offs** - Pros/cons of each
4. **Make recommendation** - What you suggest and why
5. **Define next steps** - What happens after decision

## Output Format
Decision brief with options, trade-offs, recommendation, and decision request.
