# Translate Metrics for Executives

**Category:** Communication
**Strategic Theme:** N/A
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** All

## Purpose
Make technical or detailed metrics accessible and meaningful for executive audiences.

## Inputs Required
- Technical metrics to translate
- Executive audience context
- Business outcomes the metrics relate to

## Process Steps
1. **Connect to business outcomes** - Why does this metric matter?
2. **Use comparisons** - Industry benchmarks, historical trends
3. **Add context** - What's good, bad, expected
4. **Simplify units** - Avoid jargon, use intuitive measures
5. **Show impact** - What does this mean for the business?

## Output Format
Translated metrics with context and business implications.
