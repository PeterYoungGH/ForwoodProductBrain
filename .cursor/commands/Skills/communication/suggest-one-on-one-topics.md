# Suggest One-on-One Topics

**Category:** Communication
**Strategic Theme:** N/A
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** All

## Purpose
Generate relevant topics for 1:1 meetings based on current work, blockers, and stakeholder context.

## Inputs Required
- Stakeholder profile and role
- Current projects and status
- Recent decisions and outcomes
- Known concerns or interests

## Process Steps
1. **Review recent activity** - What's happened since last meeting
2. **Identify blockers** - What needs their help
3. **Surface decisions** - What needs their input
4. **Consider their priorities** - What do they care about
5. **Prepare questions** - What to ask them

## Output Format
1:1 agenda with topics, talking points, and questions.
