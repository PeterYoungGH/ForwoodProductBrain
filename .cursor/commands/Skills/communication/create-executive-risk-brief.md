# Create Executive Risk Brief

**Category:** Communication
**Strategic Theme:** Theme 1 (Fatal Risk Visibility)
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** All

## Purpose
Prepare board-level risk summaries that convey safety status and recommendations for governance oversight.

## Inputs Required
- Risk data and trends
- Key incidents or near-misses
- Recommendations and actions

## Process Steps
1. **Lead with status** - Green/Amber/Red overall assessment
2. **Highlight critical risks** - Top 3-5 risk areas
3. **Show trends** - Improving/stable/deteriorating
4. **Recommend actions** - What should board know/approve
5. **Format for scanning** - Tables, bullets, visual indicators

## Output Format
Executive risk brief suitable for board presentation.
