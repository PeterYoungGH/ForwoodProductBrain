# Articulate Vision

**Category:** Strategy
**Strategic Theme:** N/A
**Autonomy Level:** Supervised
**Workflow Stages:** 7 (Roadmap Inclusion)

## Purpose
Frame product vision statements that align stakeholders around a shared future state.

## Inputs Required
- Strategic context and themes
- Customer outcomes and problems
- Market position and aspirations

## Process Steps
1. **Define the future state** - What does success look like in 2-3 years?
2. **Anchor in customer outcomes** - What will customers achieve?
3. **Differentiate from alternatives** - Why us?
4. **Make it inspiring and memorable** - Can people rally around it?
5. **Test for clarity** - Can anyone explain it?

## Output Format
Vision statement with supporting rationale and alignment to strategic themes.
