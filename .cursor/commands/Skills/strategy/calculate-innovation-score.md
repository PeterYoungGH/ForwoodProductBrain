# Calculate Innovation Score

**Category:** Strategy
**Strategic Theme:** N/A
**Autonomy Level:** Full
**Workflow Stages:** 7 (Roadmap Inclusion)

## Purpose
Score features for innovation potential using the Innovation Decision Framework.

## Inputs Required
- Solution details and validation results
- Market context and competitive position
- Strategic alignment assessment

## Process Steps
1. **Score Revenue Potential (1-5)** - Impact on business metrics
2. **Score Reach & Impact (1-5)** - How many users affected, how significantly
3. **Score Buy-In & Feasibility (1-5)** - Stakeholder support, technical feasibility
4. **Score Execution Difficulty (1-5)** - Complexity, risk, effort
5. **Calculate composite** - Weighted combination
6. **Classify** - Moonshot, Big Bet, Small Bet, Quick Win

## Output Format
Innovation score with breakdown and classification for roadmap prioritization.
