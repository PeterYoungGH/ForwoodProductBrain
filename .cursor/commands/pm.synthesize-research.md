# pm.synthesize-research

**Step 3** (Optional) - Command to synthesize market research, competitive analysis, industry reports, or multiple market intelligence sources.

## Command Purpose

This command synthesizes market intelligence from multiple sources to create a comprehensive understanding of market trends, competitive positioning, and opportunities.

## Execution Steps

### Step 1: Read Required Files

1. **Read the Market Synthesis Prompt**
   - File: `07-reference/prompts/research-analysis/market-synthesis-prompt.md`
   - Purpose: Understand how to synthesize market research
   - Action: Read and understand the prompt structure, parameters, and expected output

2. **Read Source Materials**
   - Files: Paths to market research documents, competitive analyses, or industry reports
   - Purpose: Materials to synthesize
   - Action: Read and understand all source materials

3. **Read Related Documents (Optional)**
   - If synthesizing research that relates to existing documents, read those documents
   - Purpose: Understand context and identify relationships

### Step 2: Apply Market Synthesis Prompt

1. **Apply the Prompt**
   - Use the market synthesis prompt to analyze the source materials
   - Synthesize market overview (size, growth, drivers, segments)
   - Analyze competitive landscape
   - Identify customer needs and pain points
   - Synthesize technology and innovation trends
   - Identify market opportunities
   - Determine implications for product strategy

2. **Structure Synthesis**
   - Organize by synthesis categories
   - Resolve contradictions or conflicts
   - Create comprehensive understanding
   - Generate unified insights

### Step 3: Generate Research Document

1. **Use Research Document Generation Prompt**
   - File: `07-reference/prompts/document-generation/research-document-prompt.md`
   - Purpose: Generate structured research document
   - Action: Apply the prompt to create the document

2. **Create Document**
   - Generate research document following the template
   - Include market overview, competitive analysis, customer needs, trends, opportunities, and implications
   - Link to source materials and related documents
   - Include source references and citations

### Step 4: Save Document

1. **Determine Location**
   - Default: `03-discovery/research/market-research/`
   - Use descriptive filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-[topic].md`

2. **Save and Link**
   - Save the research document
   - Link from source materials (if applicable)
   - Link to related opportunities or outcomes
