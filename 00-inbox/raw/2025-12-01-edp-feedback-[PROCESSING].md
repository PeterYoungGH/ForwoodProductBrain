# EDP Feedback - Processing

Feedback from EDP

Items they believe are still needed to meet our contractual requirements - (Note that Forwood doesn't believe these are contractual) 

(should have been closed by end of October):

 

Enhanced Self-Service and Configuration Capability: Ability to include or modify questions or questionnaires.

Application Loading on Other Devices: Loading issues on certain devices (various Android versions and terminal models).

Viewable User Comments: Comments from risk checks cannot be viewed or exported together; each check must be accessed individually.

Configurable Action Plans and Evidence: No direct warning when 'No' is answered in checks, which could lead to critical risks materializing as accidents.

Safety+App Dashboard and Predictive Analytics: Dashboard is overly simple; The main differentiating element of this application (the predictive capacity for accidents) has not been shown in any model or example.

Dashboards should be more action-oriented, easier to interpret, and enable immediate action based on results.

Import/Export Data Functionality: Ability to import/export data between the app and other EDP systems has not been demonstrated. In relation to it, EDP does not currently have the capacity to import/export data in an agile and manageable way. EDP also does not have the capacity to verify the information included in the application.

Date Filtering in Map View: Risk assessments cannot be filtered by completion date on the map view. This is partially done, but it is not available for all users (as it should be)

Capturing the description of the works referred to in the verifications: It is unclear what work each verification refers to.

 

 

Other Detected Issues

 

Stability problems reported by end users, discouraging use and risking job performance.

Ticket management:

Difficulty replicating reported problems

Slow resolution of operational incidents

False closure of unresolved tickets

Establish minimum information that must be provided when opening a new ticket, so that the person who opens a ticket is not requested to facilitate new information constantly

Quicksight (Questions Tab): Questions and questionnaires appear in English; they should be in Spanish.

Remove Forwood's test verifications and those from EDP labeled "test" or "prueba".

Open/pending tickets:

 

A white page with black text

AI-generated content may be incorrect.

Corrective and Minor Evolutionary Service Requests

 

Improve usability based on our suggestions.

Propose methods to verify accuracy of user-entered information (including photographic evidence where applicable, under the responsibility of the contractors for verification, with EDP being exempt from this). The accuracy of the information entered is the greatest risk of the tool, and Forwood must therefore include mechanisms to control it.

Provide greater traceability and status updates for tickets, including expected resolution dates.

Make the dashboards more action-oriented based on the information they contain; make them easier to interpret and make it more immediate to take action based on the results (applies to the three types we currently have).

Prepare comprehensive user manuals and quick reference guides for interpreting reports and suggested actions.

Create a simple operational manual for bulk loading companies and contracts.

Enable admin role to delete records (e.g., tests).

Grant contractors admin roles to download verifications from Safety Plus (in a "manageable file," such as Excel) and create DACs for their personnel.

Adjust questionnaire options:

Action Type: Only "Implemented in field" or "Stop work"

Action Plan Type: Only "Procedure" and "Installation/equipment/weather"

Adapt organizational structure and visibility to EDP requirements: Contractors should be able to view the verifications of their subcontractors within the contracts they share. EDP staff who are contract managers should be able to view the verifications carried out within the contracts for which they are responsible.

Include audio recording for pre-task talks during questionnaire completion.

When a new version is available for download from the Play Store or Apple Store, create a pop-up for the user that prompts them to update it
