# Forwood Product User Personas with Jobs-to-Be-Done Overlay (Working Draft)

Each persona below includes both **functional jobs** (what they need to get done), **emotional jobs** (how they want to feel while doing it), and **social jobs** (how they want to be perceived by others). It also retains the **role details, goals, frustrations, and design implications** from the previous version to provide richer context for product and design decisions.

---

## 1. Operator (Field Worker)

**Profile:**

Performs physical work on site and completes field-level verifications, audits, and safety observations using the Forwood mobile app.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Complete verifications, inspections, and safety forms quickly and accurately
- Report hazards, incidents, or near misses using mobile tools
- Stay compliant while performing physical work in variable field conditions

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident that their work is captured correctly and valued
- Avoid frustration from slow or confusing mobile experiences
- Feel safe and supported while working in challenging environments

**Social Jobs:**

- Be seen as reliable and safety-conscious by peers and supervisors
- Demonstrate responsibility and professionalism in their reporting

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Limited or unreliable connectivity in field conditions
- Small screen usability issues, glare, or difficulty reading content
- Too many steps or unclear workflows for simple tasks

**Platform Use:**

- Exclusively mobile (Android/iOS)
- Often in offline or low-connectivity environments

**Design Implications:**

- Must function reliably offline with auto-sync on reconnect
- Use large touch targets, minimal typing, and clear visual feedback
- Consider environmental conditions (sunlight, gloves, noise)
- Support short, guided workflows and local language options

## 2. Supervisor (Frontline Operations Leader)

**Profile:**

Supervises daily site activities, ensuring work is performed safely and that operators engage with verification and reporting processes.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Oversee operator activities and ensure verification completion
- Coach teams and reinforce correct safety behaviors
- Track and report on daily performance and outstanding actions

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel in control of team safety outcomes
- Gain satisfaction from smooth, compliant operations
- Avoid stress caused by poor visibility or delays in data

**Social Jobs:**

- Be perceived as a capable, proactive leader who ensures team compliance
- Build trust with both operators and management

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Time pressure and competing operational demands
- Poor usability in mobile workflows or slow app performance on site
- Difficulty tracking completion or follow-up on actions

**Platform Use:**

- Both desktop (for planning, reporting) and mobile (for field verification)

**Design Implications:**

- Needs fast, intuitive mobile workflows and clear offline handling
- Clear progress tracking and visual indicators for outstanding tasks
- Quick access to history, reports, and team progress

---

## 3. HSE Manager (Safety and Risk Manager)

**Profile:**

Responsible for overseeing the company's safety programs, reporting on performance, and ensuring continuous improvement in line with corporate and regulatory expectations.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Conduct and review verifications and incident reports
- Analyze trends and identify systemic risks
- Generate reports for executives and teams

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident that data is accurate and complete
- Experience trust in the system's ability to surface meaningful insights
- Feel effective in driving organizational safety improvement

**Social Jobs:**

- Be seen as a data-driven, strategic contributor to safety outcomes
- Demonstrate leadership in improving safety culture

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Difficult or time-consuming reporting workflows
- Data inconsistency between sites
- Limited visibility into action completion or overdue verifications

**Platform Use:**

- Primarily desktop/web
- Occasionally mobile when visiting site

**Design Implications:**

- Flexible reporting tools, trend dashboards, and automated alerts
- Intuitive filters and visual insights for communicating results
- Needs workflows that enable easy escalation and delegation of actions

## 4. System Administrator (Corporate-Level ICT Admin)

**Profile:**

A technically proficient user responsible for maintaining the integrity and performance of the Forwood Safety+ platform across the organization.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Manage users, permissions, and integrations
- Maintain stable system performance and clean data
- Oversee subscription plans, billing, and license allocation

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident that the system is reliable and under control
- Avoid stress from outages, sync errors, or billing confusion
- Take pride in maintaining smooth system operations

**Social Jobs:**

- Be viewed as the technical expert who keeps things running efficiently
- Be trusted by leadership to manage system compliance and cost

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Integration failures or sync errors between systems
- Inconsistent user role management and access requests
- Lack of visibility into backend data or billing issues

**Platform Use:**

- Primarily desktop/web application
- Occasionally mobile (future use expected to grow with admin app capabilities)

**Design Implications:**

- Provide health dashboards, clear billing overviews, and automation options
- Offer precise audit trails and role management tools
- Include clear UI for managing hierarchies, permissions, and data syncs

## 5. Corporate Executive (CSO/VP HSE)

**Profile:**

Senior leaders who oversee enterprise-wide safety performance, governance, and investment in the Forwood Safety+ platform.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Monitor organization-wide safety performance and assurance coverage
- Receive alerts for critical events and overdue verifications
- Evaluate ROI, adoption, and subscription utilization

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident that risk is managed and governance is sound
- Experience assurance that their investment is delivering results
- Avoid anxiety from missing or delayed high-priority information

**Social Jobs:**

- Be recognized as a strategic leader driving zero-harm culture
- Communicate safety excellence to boards and investors

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Limited transparency into assurance activity completion or coverage
- Too much granular data without clear insights or summaries
- Delay in receiving critical alerts or performance updates

**Platform Use:**

- Primarily desktop/web dashboards and reports
- Mobile access for alerts, summaries, and quick overviews while traveling

**Design Implications:**

- Executive dashboard with configurable KPIs and filters (site, business unit, risk type)
- Smart notifications for key incidents or overdue assurance activities
- ROI and usage analytics to support budgeting and performance reviews

## 6. Incident Reporter (Any Role)

**Profile:**

Any user (operator, supervisor, contractor, or visitor) who witnesses or is involved in an incident or near miss and initiates an event report in the system.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Report incidents or near misses quickly and accurately
- Capture key details and attach evidence while on site

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel supported and unafraid to report issues
- Experience relief knowing the right people are alerted instantly

**Social Jobs:**

- Be seen as responsible and proactive in maintaining safety

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Complex or time-consuming reporting forms
- Poor mobile performance in stressful or low-connectivity environments
- Uncertainty about who gets notified or what happens next

**Platform Use:**

- Predominantly mobile (on-site or in the field)
- Occasionally desktop for detailed follow-up or confirmation

**Design Implications:**

- Simplify the process to minimize stress and cognitive load
- Auto-confirm submission and provide clear follow-up expectations

---

## 7. Responder (Immediate Response Role)

**Profile:**

Supervisors, managers, or safety officers who receive initial notifications of incidents and are responsible for immediate response and escalation.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Coordinate immediate emergency responses and notifications
- Confirm incident severity and trigger appropriate escalation

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident, calm, and supported during urgent situations
- Avoid panic caused by unclear or delayed information

**Social Jobs:**

- Be trusted to handle emergencies decisively and effectively

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Delay in receiving notifications or incomplete incident details
- Poor visibility of real-time updates from the field
- Confusing escalation workflows

**Platform Use:**

- Mobile for initial alerts and confirmations
- Desktop for ongoing response tracking or coordination

**Design Implications:**

- Real-time notifications and streamlined escalation workflows
- Clear acknowledgement and response tracking tools

---

## 8. Investigator (Post-Incident Analysis Role)

**Profile:**

Experienced safety professionals, supervisors, or managers responsible for root cause analysis and follow-up investigations after incidents or near misses.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Conduct root cause investigations and document findings
- Use frameworks (ICAM, Five Whys, Bowtie) to analyze data

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel thorough, methodical, and confident in uncovering causes
- Avoid frustration from missing evidence or fragmented systems

**Social Jobs:**

- Be perceived as objective, competent, and reliable in investigations

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Difficult evidence collection or data upload processes
- Limited collaboration tools for multi-person investigations
- Inflexible or inconsistent investigation templates

**Platform Use:**

- Primarily desktop (analysis, documentation, follow-up)
- Mobile for field evidence collection and note-taking

**Design Implications:**

- Provide configurable investigation templates and offline evidence capture
- Ensure collaboration features for multi-user investigations

---

## 9. Action Reporter (Actions Workflow Context)

**Profile:**

Any user (operator, supervisor, or manager) who identifies a need for corrective or preventive action and creates it in the system.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Create new actions linked to incidents, inspections, or verifications
- Assign responsible people and ensure traceability

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident that the issue will be addressed
- Avoid frustration from unclear ownership or tracking

**Social Jobs:**

- Be viewed as proactive and committed to improvement

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Action creation feels cumbersome or disconnected from the workflow
- Lack of visibility on whether the action was acknowledged or completed
- Unclear relationships between actions and originating issues

**Platform Use:**

- Mobile for immediate creation during field work
- Desktop for linking to reports or audits

**Design Implications:**

- Embed action creation in workflows with auto-linking and progress visibility

---

## 10. Responsible Person (Actions Workflow Context)

**Profile:**

The person assigned to complete an action or ensure its resolution, often a supervisor, technician, or subject matter expert.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Complete assigned actions and provide proof of completion
- Plan, prioritize, and manage workload effectively

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel organized, accountable, and supported in their responsibilities
- Avoid confusion about priorities or expectations

**Social Jobs:**

- Be seen as dependable and solution-oriented by peers and management

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Unclear expectations or missing context
- Difficulty updating progress or uploading completion evidence
- Poor visibility of deadlines or dependencies

**Platform Use:**

- Mobile for field execution and updates
- Desktop for planning and tracking multiple actions

**Design Implications:**

- Clear deadlines, visual progress indicators, and contextual details
- Offline functionality for completion updates
- Calendar and prioritization tools on desktop

---

## 11. Reviewer (Actions Workflow Context)

**Profile:**

A supervisor, manager, or HSE representative responsible for verifying that completed actions meet required standards and are appropriately closed out.

**Functional Jobs:**

- Review completed actions for adequacy and compliance
- Approve or reject closure requests with comments

**Emotional Jobs:**

- Feel confident that standards are upheld
- Avoid frustration from incomplete evidence or context

**Social Jobs:**

- Be recognized as a diligent, fair authority ensuring follow-through

**Frustrations / Pain Points:**

- Reviewing on mobile may be difficult without full context
- Missing or unclear evidence of completion
- High volume of actions with no prioritization view

**Platform Use:**

- Desktop for full context and attachments
- Mobile for quick field-level approvals or rejections

**Design Implications:**

- Approve/reject workflows with comments and quick access to linked content
- Visual differentiation between pending and verified actions
- Easy access to linked reports, photos, and history

## Integration with JTBD Framework

These personas are enhanced with Jobs-to-Be-Done thinking:

- **Functional Jobs** → What tasks must they accomplish?
- **Emotional Jobs** → How do they want to feel?
- **Social Jobs** → How do they want to be perceived?

Use this overlay when designing features to ensure you're addressing not just *what* users do, but *how* they feel and *who* they want to be while doing it.

Reference these personas during discovery and specification to:
- Validate that features address real functional, emotional, and social jobs
- Design interactions that reduce friction and create delight
- Ensure platform capabilities serve all organizational roles and workflows
