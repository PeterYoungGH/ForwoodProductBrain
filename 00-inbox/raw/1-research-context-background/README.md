# Research Context & Background

## Purpose

This stage establishes the foundational context required for all subsequent prototyping work. Before creating specifications, plans, or agent instructions, the system must understand:

1. **Business Context** — How Forwood operates, our strategic position, and organizational priorities
2. **Market Landscape** — Market dynamics, opportunities, and competitive positioning
3. **Competitive Intelligence** — Competitor offerings, differentiation opportunities, and market gaps
4. **Domain Expertise** — Subject matter knowledge and industry-specific considerations
5. **Platform Capabilities** — Our technical infrastructure and what's possible to build
6. **Feature-Specific Requirements** — Detailed scope and objectives for the feature being prototyped

## Input Files

- **forwood-background.md** — Company context, mission, values, and strategic positioning
- **market-background.md** — Market size, trends, customer segments, and opportunities
- **competitor-background.md** — Competitor analysis, feature benchmarks, and differentiation points
- **domain-SME-background.md** — Subject matter expertise, domain terminology, and best practices
- **platform-background.md** — Technical architecture, capabilities, constraints, and tech stack
- **feature-specific-background.md** — Objectives, requirements, success metrics for the specific feature

## How to Use

1. **Review all files** in this folder before advancing to specification or planning stages
2. **Reference this context** when responding to feature briefs or requirements
3. **Ground agent reasoning** in this background to ensure recommendations align with business goals, market realities, and technical feasibility
4. **Update files** as new market intelligence or platform capabilities emerge

## Next Stage

Once this context is established, proceed to [Stage 2: Create a Spec](/docs/Prototyping%20prompts/2-create-a-spec/) to transform these insights into detailed specifications.
