## Original Content

Product – One Page Strategy (FY 25-26) 

Peter Young (Snr Product Manager) 

Objective: Advance our product vision through the delivery of the Forwood One platform, achieving commercial traction, and defining a clear upgrade pathway for current customers. 

 

Key Results: 

Launch Forwood One and win 5 new deals (new logos). 

Build and execute Safety+ to Forwood One upgrade plan in concert with Head of Solutions and other departments; transition all customers in FY26. 

Achieve an Forwood One product NPS score of 20 from initial customers. 

Build CRM+ and Risk+ to Forwood One upgrade plans to support unified product architecture. 

Establish a new AI-driven product discovery and design workflow to improve efficiency and achieve commercial goals 

Build a strategic integration plan across product pillars of CRM, Risk, and Compliance. 

Achieve ≥90% task success rate in usability testing across critical user flows (e.g., verification checklist management, form building, action management). 

Secure evidence from ≥3 customers that design and usability were cited as key deciding factors in pursuing an Forwood One upgrade. 

Ensure a positive (via survey) implementation, onboarding, training, and support experience for Forwood One. 

Deliver a new design system for Forwood One that enables a cohesive, scalable, and accessible user experience across all product interfaces. 

 

The Challenge: Bringing Forwood One to market within 12 months will require balancing rapid customer acquisition with the complex migration of existing clients. We must accelerate discovery and delivery quality while empowering the team to achieve outsized impact through collaboration, culture, upskilling and AI‑enabled tools. Embedding a culture of product discovery focused on deeply understanding industry problems, not just requested solutions, will be key to success. 

FY26 Key Strategic Principles / Pillars 

 

Focus: Drive prioritisation that aligns effort to key results and governs scope, requirements, and commercial agreements. Advocate for the importance of delivery velocity, quality and impact of agreed outcomes. 

Team Capability: Develop depth of skill and product principles within the team while identifying and resourcing key gaps to ensure sustainable delivery and innovation capacity. 

Customer Connection: Build close, insight-driven relationships with clients to inform product direction, create growth loops, and ensure every release drives measurable value. 

Training and Enablement: Strengthen internal capability confidence through structured training, communication, and enablement programs. 

Innovation and Experimentation: Nurture innovation streams that test emerging technologies, such as AI and data-driven experiments, to inform our 12–24 month horizon and maintain an outcome-focused mindset. 

FY26 Key Projects / Milestones / Roadmap 

 

Q1 (July-Sep 25) 

Define and embed agentic product processes 

Launch Forwood One MVP, UAT and train wider team 

Q2 (Oct-Dec 25) 

Onboard and embed Product Designer 

Dec: Sales have >15 deals in Forwood One pipeline – Actively conducting demonstrations 

Launch Forwood One V1, UAT and train wider team 

Launch Forwood One V2, UAT and ready migration 

Validate Risk+ iframe in Forwood One with UAT and possible migration 

Implement branding and new platform name 

Q3 (Jan-Mar 26) 

Begin migration of Safety+ clients to Forwood One 

Feb/Mar: First deals signed for Forwood One 

Deeply integrate new design principles and systems 

Launch MVP Forwood One Native App 

Develop and Present State of the Product (Int/Ext) 

Q4 (Apr-Jun 26) 

April/May: Onboarding new Forwood One clients 

Launch Forwood One V3, UAT and ready migration for CRM clients 

Conduct NPS (That includes Forwood One Clients) 

Launch AI features from innovation stream 

What Else We Need To Succeed: 

Tight alignment of scope, priorities, and commercial agreements to our OKRs. 

Strong collaboration across Product, Engineering, Commercial, and Operations. 

Focused training and enablement to build internal and customer confidence. 

Savvy approach to resourcing and capability growth to reduce the impact of emerging bottlenecks. 

Continued investment in improving engineering velocity and quality to scale efficiently. 

Effective change management for smooth client migration and adoption.
