# Delight Excellence Evaluation Framework - Processing

## Preamble: Using the Checklist During Planning and Specification Creation

When defining or prioritizing product features, the objective is to ensure that each planned capability not only delivers functional value but also creates emotional resonance — what Dr Nesrine Changuel calls **delight**.

This framework is to be used during the **planning and specification stages** of feature development to guide decision-making, validate design direction, and ensure the resulting product achieves both user and business outcomes.

## How AI Agents Should Use This Checklist

The AI agent (or product planning assistant) should use this checklist to:

- **Evaluate proposed features or enhancements** before they progress to specification
- **Identify potential gaps** in user, emotional, or business impact
- **Recommend refinements** to strengthen features that lack balance across function, emotion, and feasibility
- **Ensure continuity and measurability** — confirming that delightful experiences can be sustained and tracked over time

## Application Process

For each feature concept, review all checklist items below:

- **A Yes response** indicates readiness to advance to specification
- **A No response** indicates that further clarification, validation, or redesign is required before proceeding

**The AI agent should document reasoning for each decision and propose corrective next steps where gaps exist.**

## Delight Excellence Checklist

### User Impact

**Does the solution effectively help users achieve their goals?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Validate that the feature solves a real user problem and delivers measurable value to the target audience.

---

### Business Impact

**Does the solution effectively help to achieve business goals?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Ensure alignment with Forwood's strategic objectives, revenue targets, and market positioning.

---

### Data and Insights

**Did you use customer insights or data to validate emotional needs?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Ground the feature in real user research, not assumptions. Use qualitative and quantitative data to understand how users _feel_ about the problem and proposed solution.

---

### Familiarity

**Does the solution balance being familiar and being innovative well enough to capture interest?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** The feature should feel intuitive to users (leveraging familiar patterns) while offering something new or delightful that captures attention and creates positive emotion.

---

### Feasibility

**Can the solution be developed within a reasonable timeframe and with the available skills and technologies?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Assess technical complexity, resource availability, and timeline. A delightful feature that cannot be built is not viable.

---

### Inclusiveness

**Could the product potentially be harmful in some cases?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Consider accessibility, safety, bias, and unintended consequences. Delight should not come at the cost of excluding or harming any user group.

---

### Distraction

**Does the solution enhance the primary experience without distraction?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** The feature should complement the core user journey without creating friction, confusion, or cognitive overload. Delight should feel natural, not forced.

---

### Continuity

**Is there a plan to maintain this delightful solution continuously?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Delight degrades over time without maintenance. Ensure there are mechanisms (monitoring, updates, improvements) to sustain the feature's positive impact.

---

### Measurability

**Are there metrics to track the desired delight?**

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No

**Guidance:** Define clear KPIs (engagement, satisfaction, adoption, retention, NPS, etc.) to validate that the feature delivers the expected delight and business outcomes.

---

## Summary

All checklist items should be evaluated as **Yes** before advancing a feature to specification. Any **No** responses require documented clarification, design refinement, or validation before progression. The AI agent is responsible for ensuring this rigor and documenting the reasoning for all decisions.
