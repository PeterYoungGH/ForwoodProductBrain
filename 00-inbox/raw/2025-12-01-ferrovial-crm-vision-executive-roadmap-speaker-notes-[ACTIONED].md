Our Mission 

To be the global gold standard in fatality prevention by delivering the most trusted, effective, and widely adopted critical risk management methodology and software solutions. 

 

Our Vision for Oculus 

Project: Oculus is the codename for Forwood's most ambitious product evolution — a complete redesign, rebuild, and reimagining of our platform to achieve this mission. 

Our goal with Oculus is to take our suite of products — e.g. CRM+, Risk+, Safety+, Compliance+ — and combine them into a single, unified system. We will leverage the latest technologies and strategies, including AI-enhanced product development workflows, to deliver a solution that is even more effective for our customers, while enabling us to ship the updates they want and need, faster. 

 

Our Strategic Pillars for the Next Two Years 

We spoke to our customers and are focusing on four key areas in our roadmap for the next two years: 

Ease of Use – Deliver an intuitive, decluttered experience that makes Forwood effortless for every user at every level — operators, supervisors, and managers. 

SIF Prevention – Double down on CRM+; make our methodology and tools even more effective, useful, and powerful; sharpen our tools, workflows, and insights to directly reduce Serious Injuries and Fatalities. 

Adoption & Engagement – Accelerate user uptake through role-based design, gamification, smart onboarding, and in-product coaching. 

Data Insights – Embed intelligence and analytics to turn data into proactive action, predict incidents before they happen, and measure and benchmark your maturity model. 

 

Core Pillars of CRM (Optional to say) 

At its core, Critical Risk Management can be broken down into a few key pillars: 

Focus on Fatal Risks – Identify only the risks that can cause serious injury or death. 

Define Critical Controls – Establish non-negotiable controls that must never fail. 

Verify in the Field – Conduct real-time control checks with frontline engagement. 

Ensure Data Transparency – Make performance visible to drive accountability. 

Learn and Prevent – Use failures and trends to drive proactive learning and prevent SIFs. 

With Oculus, our aim is to double down on these and make them even more effective.
