# 2-Year Strategy & Roadmap Overview - Processing

## 2-Year Strategy

### Mission
To be the gold standard in Serious Injury and Fatality prevention in high-risk industries.

### Vision
**Project: Oculus**  
Unify CRM+, Risk+, Safety+, Compliance+ into one intelligent system and enable faster, AI-assisted delivery.

### Delivery Goal
Make our products 10x more effective for customers & ship high-value updates faster.

### Strategic Pillars

**Ease of use**  
Intuitive, decluttered, effortless for all roles.

**SIF Prevention**  
Double-down on CRM+, sharpen tools & workflows, reduce SIFs.

**Adoption & Engagement**  
Role-based design, smart onboarding & coaching, gamification.

**Data & Insights**  
Proactive analytics, predict incidents, benchmark maturity.

### CRM Pillars

**Focus on Fatal Risks**  
Identify only the risks that can cause serious injury or death.

**Define Critical Controls**  
Establish non-negotiable controls that must never fail.

**Verify in the Field**  
Conduct real-time checks with frontline engagement.

**Ensure Data Transparency**  
Make performance visible to drive accountability.

**Learn and Prevent**  
Use trends and lessons to drive proactive improvement.
