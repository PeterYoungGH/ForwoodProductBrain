# Forwood One Success Criteria - Inbox

From Meagan - Head of Solutions

Treat this as input and not 100% our locked in plans

FORWOOD ONE SUCCESS CRITERIA 

Platform Readiness

• V1, V2, and V3 released on committed dates.

• No P1 defects at client cut-over; maximum three P2 defects outstanding.  Each P2 must have:

– a documented workaround

– a committed fix date

– a communication line for the client

• Critical workflow UAT pass rate above 95 percent.

• Average load time under 2 seconds for safety-critical workflows.

Migration Outcomes

• Zero operational disruption during client migrations.

• All Safety+ and Safety+ legacy clients migrated in agreed sequence without rollback.

• Ninety percent of users able to complete core workflows on day one.

Support and Stability

• Fifty percent reduction in P1–P3 tickets within 60 days post-migration.

• Eighty percent of tickets resolved by CS or support without engineering by month two.

• No bespoke development delivered outside approved change control.

Scope and Governance

• All change requests managed through the approved framework.

• Fewer than five unauthorised scope deviations annually.  (An "unauthorised scope deviation" is any change to the agreed roadmap, migration scope, delivery timeline, or feature set)

• Engineering velocity unaffected by more than ten percent from client demands.

Client Satisfaction

• Client satisfaction score of 4.2/5 or higher within 60 days.

• Zero executive-level escalations relating to performance or delivery.

• Three or more clients provide testimonials supporting the public launch.

Internal Readiness

• Sales, CS, Support, and Academy fully educated before first migration.

• Onboarding playbook used consistently for all clients.

• Tenant creation and onboarding commenced within three business days for new clients.

Data and Reporting

• Continuous reporting without history loss or data gaps.

• QuickSight or embedded reporting operating from a unified data model.

• No manual reporting workarounds beyond two weeks of hypercare.

Commercial Position (Phased Migration)

• Safety+ phase: eighty percent of Safety+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2026.

• CRM+ phase: eighty percent of CRM+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q4 2027.

• Risk+ and Compliance+ phase: eighty percent of Risk+ and Compliance+ revenue assigned to migration timelines by Q2 2027.

• ForwoodOne becomes the default platform for all new sales by mid-2026.

• Zero churn of Safety+ and CRM+ clients due to performance or migration issues.

Organisational Alignment

• All four communication streams delivered on time with consistent messaging.

• SC gateway approvals achieved without rework.

• Governance and change control adopted across all delivery teams.

Post-Migration Performance

• Ninety-five percent of client operational activity occurring in ForwoodOne by end-2027.

• Clients exit Hypercare within eight weeks and transition to BAU support.
