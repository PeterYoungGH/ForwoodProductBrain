### Mission Zero

Hello and welcome to Forwood. Like you, we believe that life is fragile and precious. The loss of just one worker diminishes us all. Our Mission Zero is to discover and eliminate serious injury and fatality risks (SIF) in high risk industries.

### Already the World Leader

Because we understand that many risks are dynamic, we are the world leader in fatality prevention and critical risk management. At the heart of this success is a simple but extremely powerful tool called Forwood CRM. It helps people identify and control risks before they kill.

### We're Just Beginning

In 2023, the ILO reported nearly three million workers die annually from work-related accidents and diseases. With the rise in high-risk industries worldwide, Forwood CRM is expanding rapidly across utilities, infrastructure and other sectors.
