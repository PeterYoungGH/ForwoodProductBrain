# Research

This folder is structured to capture, organize, and synthesize research that informs product strategy and decision-making.

## Structure

### `sources/`
Raw research inputs from various sources:
- Articles and blog posts
- Research papers and studies
- Industry reports
- Conference presentations
- Webinars and podcasts (transcripts/notes)
- Competitive analysis
- Market research

**Organization:** Files can be organized by source type, date, or topic as needed.

### `synthesis/`
Synthesized research outputs:
- Research summaries
- Comparative analyses
- Trend reports
- Literature reviews
- Research briefs

**Purpose:** Transform raw research into actionable insights.

### `topics/`
Research organized by topic or theme:
- Product-specific research
- Market research
- Technology trends
- User research
- Competitive intelligence
- Industry analysis

**Organization:** Each topic can have its own subfolder with sources, synthesis, and insights.

### `insights/`
Key insights and learnings extracted from research:
- Strategic insights
- Product implications
- Market opportunities
- Risk assessments
- Recommendations

**Purpose:** High-level takeaways that directly inform strategy and decision-making.

### `templates/`
Templates for capturing and documenting research:
- Research capture template
- Synthesis template
- Insight extraction template
- Literature review template

**Purpose:** Standardize how research is captured and documented.

## Workflow

1. **Capture:** Add raw research materials to `sources/` or relevant `topics/` subfolder
2. **Synthesize:** Create summaries and analyses in `synthesis/` or topic-specific folders
3. **Extract Insights:** Document key insights in `insights/` or topic-specific folders
4. **Apply:** Use insights to inform strategy, roadmap, and decision-making

## Best Practices

- **Date your research:** Include dates in filenames or metadata
- **Cite sources:** Always include source information and links
- **Tag and categorize:** Use consistent naming and organization
- **Regular synthesis:** Periodically synthesize related research
- **Update insights:** Keep insights current as new research emerges
