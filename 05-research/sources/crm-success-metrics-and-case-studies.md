## Summary

This research captures key success metrics, customer case studies, and market statistics from Forwood CRM's track record. The data demonstrates CRM's effectiveness in fatality prevention with a 99.6% success rate, deployment at 1000+ sites globally, and compelling industry-wide comparisons showing zero fatalities among companies using Forwood CRM versus 791 fatalities among companies without it.

---

## Key Points

- Forwood CRM has achieved a 99.6% success rate after full implementation
- CRM is trusted at more than 1000 sites across the world
- ICMM Case Study (2012-2020): Companies without Forwood CRM had 791 fatalities; companies with Forwood CRM had 0 fatalities
- Global workplace fatality problem: 330,000 die annually from workplace accidents; 2.6 million die from work-related causes
- ILO 2023 report: Nearly three million workers die annually from work-related accidents and diseases

---

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Exceptional Success Rate
Forwood CRM has achieved an incredible 99.6% success rate after full implementation. This demonstrates the effectiveness of the CRM system in preventing fatalities and serious incidents.

### Finding 2: Global Scale and Trust
Forwood CRM is trusted at more than 1000 sites across the world, indicating widespread adoption and confidence in the system across multiple industries and regions.

### Finding 3: Industry-Wide Impact (ICMM Case Study 2012-2020)
A significant industry-wide contrast was observed:
- **Companies without Forwood CRM:** 791 fatalities
- **Companies with Forwood CRM:** 0 fatalities

This demonstrates the transformative impact of CRM implementation on fatality prevention.

### Finding 4: Customer Success Stories

**Rio Tinto:**
- Six consecutive years fatality free globally
- 2019 marked their first fatality-free year in 147 years

**Newcrest:**
- Seven consecutive years fatality free to deployed sites

**HEDNO (Greece):**
- Full implementation led to fewer disruptions, zero fatalities, and a stronger safety-first culture
- A nationwide cultural and safety transformation

### Finding 5: Global Workplace Fatality Problem
- 330,000 die annually from workplace accidents
- 2.6 million die from work-related causes
- ILO 2023 report: Nearly three million workers die annually from work-related accidents and diseases
- A system like CRM is required to prevent SIF incidents
