## Executive Summary

This meeting reviewed Gold Fields' CRM implementation performance from January to November 2025, covering verification volumes across managers, supervisors, and operators. Key findings include strong supervisor commitment and verification cadence, but concerning compliance rates near 100% (indicating potential superficial verifications) and low manager participation, especially in November. A key recommendation is to conduct on-field support with partners to improve verification quality and leadership engagement.

## Key Performance Metrics (Jan 2025 - Nov 2025)

### Overall Verification Performance

Performance data reviewed for:
- **Managers** - From Jan 2025 to Nov 2025
- **Supervisors** - From Jan 2025 to Nov 2025
- **Operators** - From Jan 2025 to Nov 2025

### Positive Aspects ✅

1. **Strong commitment from supervisors**
   - Significant volume of verifications completed by supervisors
   - Demonstrates operational discipline and consistent commitment to CRM process

2. **Good verification cadence from supervisors throughout the year**
   - Clear and continuous routine from supervisor group in applying CRM
   - Ensures regularity in verification process
   - **However, this consistency is not reflected at the management level**

### Critical Observation Points ⚠️

1. **Compliance rates close to 100% — red flag**
   - Such high percentages may indicate:
     - Superficial verifications
     - Limited depth in risk identification
     - Incomplete understanding of what constitutes a non-conformance
   - **This reduces the real value of verifications for risk management**

2. **Low participation from managers, especially in November**
   - Manager engagement has declined, particularly in the most recent month
   - This contrasts with strong supervisor performance

**Strategic Implication:** The near-perfect compliance rate is a critical concern. Similar to CoMinVi's situation, this suggests the verification process may not be identifying real risks effectively. The low manager participation compounds this issue, as manager engagement is crucial for CRM success.

---

## Key Recommendation

### 🔧 On-Field Support with Partners

**Recommendation:** Conduct hands-on field support alongside partners, involving leadership directly in a high-quality verification session.

**During this activity, partners can:**
- Observe real verifications together with supervisors and managers
- Provide immediate guidance on what constitutes quality, depth, and legitimate non-conformities
- Align expectations with leadership regarding their role in the CRM routine
- Help assess whether the quantity and focus of verifications correspond to the actual risk exposure of the activities

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Improve verification quality
- Increase leadership engagement
- Ensure process aligns with site's critical risk exposures

**Strategic Implication:** This recommendation addresses both the high compliance rate issue and low manager participation. On-field support with partners can provide the hands-on coaching needed to improve verification quality and leadership engagement.

### 4. New Action Plan Review Field (FYI)

**What's happening:**
- New Action Plan Review field added for task actions
- Supports post-completion effectiveness checks

**Key details:**
- New "Review required" checkbox when creating or updating task action
- If selected, users can nominate a reviewer
- Reviewer assesses action plan once it's completed
- Reviewer receives notification when task is ready for review
- Reviewers can approve or reject based on effectiveness

**Why it matters:**
- Strengthens accountability and continuous improvement
- Embeds formal review process into action management
- Ensures corrective actions are not only completed, but validated for impact

**Strategic Implication:** This feature directly addresses action plan effectiveness, which is important for CRM success. The review process ensures actions actually solve problems, not just get completed.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: High Compliance Rates Are a Critical Concern
Similar to CoMinVi, Gold Fields has compliance rates close to 100%, which is a red flag indicating:
- Superficial verifications
- Limited depth in risk identification
- Incomplete understanding of what constitutes a non-conformance
- Reduced real value of verifications for risk management

**Strategic Implication:** This is a fundamental issue that needs immediate attention. The recommendation for on-field support with partners directly addresses this concern.

### Finding 2: Strong Supervisor Performance but Manager Engagement Declining
Supervisors show strong commitment and good verification cadence throughout the year, but:
- This consistency is not reflected at management level
- Low manager participation, especially in November
- Manager engagement is crucial for CRM success

**Strategic Implication:** The on-field support recommendation should specifically target manager engagement to address this gap.

### Finding 3: Structured Champion Program
Gold Fields has 17 champions, each responsible for one critical risk, with monthly meetings to:
- Review action plans for specific challenges
- Understand how verifications can achieve higher quality

**Strategic Implication:** This is a best practice that could be shared with other clients. The champion model provides clear ownership and accountability.

### Finding 4: Active Learning and Engagement
Gold Fields is actively seeking to improve:
- Requested support materials on non-conformities and high-quality verifications
- Wants to learn more about platform capabilities
- Agreed to biweekly meetings for more frequent engagement
- Requested context meeting to discuss goals

**Strategic Implication:** Gold Fields is engaged and wants to improve. This presents opportunity for expanded training, enablement, and strategic account management.

### Finding 5: Feature Requests Show Maturity
Requests for filtering by area and default area linking show:
- Thinking about reporting and process efficiency
- Understanding of how to improve workflows
- Maturity in CRM usage

**Strategic Implication:** These are reasonable feature requests that could benefit other clients as well.

### Finding 6: Phased Adoption Approach
Gold Fields is focused on Manager and Supervisor verifications, with Operator verifications planned for future. This suggests:
- Phased approach to CRM adoption
- Focus on getting core workflows right before expanding
- Opportunity to plan Operator verification rollout
