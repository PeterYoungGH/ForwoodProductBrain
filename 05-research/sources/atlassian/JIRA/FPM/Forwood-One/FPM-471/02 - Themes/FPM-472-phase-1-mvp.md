---

## Description

The goals of this phase are:

* **Value:** The primary goal of the MVP stage is to set the tone for a unified platform that can be grown and matured over time. 

    * Validate modular approach (Features and functions are designed and built to be used across a range of industries and applications)
    * Validate agentic workflows for Product Mgmt, Product Design and Product Engineering.
    * Re-align our platform offering around the core goal of Fatality Preventions (SiFs)
    
* **Usability and Feasability:** Core modular systems are designed and built:

    * Content Mgmt: Checklist and Questions builder
    * End user delivery: Form creation, user submission
    * Real world impact: Action creation, simple dashboards
    
* **Commercial:** No explicit commercial goals, however this is an important first step in consolidating our pipeline into a unified product and early input on conceptual packaging would be valuable
