## Summary

Oculus V2 - Safety+ Upgrade Pathway and Expanded ICP

---

## Description

The goals of this phase are:

* **Commercial/Business:** 

    * Enable modules currently sold under the Safety+ banner to be sold and packaged under the Oculus platform. 
    
        * Critical Control Verifications (Using CRM methodology)
        * Inspections
        * Safety Interactions (Safety Conversations, Safety Meetings)
        * Event Management (Incident and Near Miss management)
        
    * Create a viable upgrade pathway with the aim of shifting all current Safety+ clients on to the Oculus platform.
    * Ultimately decommision Safety+ (Likely achievable post this phase)
    
* **Usability:**

    * Clients currently on Safety+ are able to easily pilot, onboard and transition their teams into the Oculus platform
    * All base outcomes of the supported modules in Safety+ are supported in Oculus. Clients are able to conduct the safety practices associated with the modules with either the same or alternate functionality
    
* **Feasibility:** 

    * A feasible method is established to port across all relevant client data from Safety+ to Oculus
    * All minimal functionality needed to support the Safety+ modules and an effective upgrade/migration of these clients is completed, robust and commisioned
    * Both web and app access points are available 
    
* **Value:** 

    * Existing Safety+ clients are able to upgrade to the Oculus platform and experience an improved experience (NPS score)
    * Clients in the pipeline such as Barrick are able to use the Inspections module
