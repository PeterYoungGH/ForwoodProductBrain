## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Comprehensive Solution Ecosystem
Forwood offers a comprehensive ecosystem of solutions:
- **CRM+:** Proven fatality elimination, field-tested verifications (10+ years), patented 3-tier methodology
- **RISK+:** Enterprise Risk Register, bow-tie visualization, links between risks, controls, and operational data
- **Tailings+:** Accelerates tailings facility audits for global compliance
- **Safety+:** Manages critical control verifications, incident reporting, safety conversations
- **Compliance+:** Tracks multi-standard audit compliance, simplifies governance reporting
- **Vision+:** AI-powered behavioral monitoring, identifies unsafe acts, links to critical controls
- **ESG+:** Monitors adherence to sustainability and ESG frameworks globally

This ecosystem approach positions Forwood as a comprehensive platform provider, not just a point solution.

### Finding 2: Proven Impact and Scale
Forwood highlights outcomes described as "unheard of" in the critical risk management industry, with:
- **1,000+ Operating Sites**
- **60 Countries**
- **Over 200 million critical control data points**
- **Available in multiple languages**
- **15+ years** of experience

This scale provides significant credibility and demonstrates proven effectiveness.

### Finding 3: Strong Customer Testimonials
The website includes powerful customer testimonials from:
- **Senior Safety Advisor, Metso:** "The most important reason we use Forwood's CRM solution is that it gives us a fatality prevention program. We don't want fatalities."
- **Superintendent, Global Mining Company:** "It's incredibly user-friendly and straightforward to teach."
- **COO, Tier 1 US Mining Company:** "Forwood CRM+ is the most important system we have across our entire company. Great companies don't kill people."
- **VP EHS & Risk, Tier 1 Mining Company:** "When everyone is doing the same thing, the fatality prevention program becomes far more effective."
- **Brazilian Mine Operator:** "CRM+ eliminates unnecessary paper and focuses directly on what poses a risk. You focus on the problem and the resolution is immediate."

These testimonials provide strong social proof and highlight different value propositions (fatality prevention, usability, importance, effectiveness, efficiency).

### Finding 4: Thought Leadership Content
The website includes thought leadership content:
- **Real Safety Leadership:** 10 ways executives can drive safety in the field
- **What Is Critical Risk Management?** Explains CRM as proven, systematic method
- **Access Key Data with Safety+ 2.0:** Product evolution driven by client feedback
- **Research Spotlight – Unplanned Work and SIF Risk:** Shows unplanned work carries significantly higher risk

This content positions Forwood as a thought leader and provides valuable resources for customers.

### Finding 5: Brand Promise and Market Position
Forwood emphasizes a balance of **innovation, simplicity, and practicality**. Its systems are presented as both technological and methodological—blending analytics, user design, and behavioral insights to operationalize safety leadership.

**Key Differentiators:**
- **Data-driven prevention** — Over 200M+ verification records
- **Standardized yet configurable** — Approach across multiple languages and industries
- **Embedded leadership visibility and accountability** — Visible at all organizational levels
- **Integrated ecosystem** — Safety, risk, compliance, ESG, and AI-driven behavioral monitoring

### Finding 6: Unifying Message
**"Great companies don't kill people."**

This powerful message unifies Forwood's technology, method, and leadership culture, making the principle of fatality prevention a measurable reality.

### Finding 7: Research Finding on Unplanned Work
The website highlights research showing that **unplanned work carries a significantly higher risk of serious injury or fatality**. This finding draws on hundreds of sites globally and highlights actionable factors for improving planned work safety. This research-based insight provides additional credibility and value.
