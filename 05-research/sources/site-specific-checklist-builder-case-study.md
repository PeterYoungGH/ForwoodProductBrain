## Summary

This research captures a case study of working with a major Tier 1 customer to develop advanced CRM+ methodology and software capabilities. The challenge addressed was scaling critical control assurance across diverse global sites without losing consistency or local relevance. The solution enabled corporate to define a master checklist once, while site teams digitally tailor and justify changes within a governed framework. All master and site-specific checklists are stored in a central content library for comparison, audit, and continuous improvement. A built-in smart scheduling engine ensures the right verifications occur at the right sites and times. The impact: what once took weeks of workshops and manual coordination can now be completed in a single day — with full oversight, speed, and assurance.

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Balancing Global Consistency with Local Relevance

The challenge was scaling critical control assurance across diverse global sites without losing consistency or local relevance. This represents a common problem for large, multi-site organizations:
- **Global consistency:** Corporate needs to ensure all sites follow the same critical risk management standards
- **Local relevance:** Site teams need flexibility to address site-specific conditions and requirements
- **Governance:** Changes must be justified and tracked within a governed framework

The solution enables both: corporate defines master checklists that ensure consistency, while site teams can tailor them to local needs within a governed framework.

### Finding 2: Digital Tailoring with Governance

Site teams can digitally tailor master checklists and justify changes within a governed framework. This approach:
- **Maintains consistency:** Master checklist ensures baseline standards are met
- **Enables customization:** Site teams can adapt to local conditions
- **Provides governance:** All changes are tracked, justified, and auditable
- **Supports learning:** Changes can be reviewed and potentially rolled up to master if valuable

This represents a shift from rigid, one-size-fits-all approaches to flexible, governed customization.

### Finding 3: Central Content Library for Continuous Improvement

All master and site-specific checklists are stored in a central content library for:
- **Comparison:** Compare master vs. site-specific versions to understand variations
- **Audit:** Track all changes and justifications for compliance and learning
- **Continuous improvement:** Identify best practices from site-specific adaptations that could improve the master checklist

This creates a learning organization where local innovations can inform global standards.

### Finding 4: Smart Scheduling Engine

A built-in smart scheduling engine ensures:
- **Right verifications:** The appropriate verifications occur at each site
- **Right times:** Verifications are scheduled at optimal times
- **Right sites:** Site-specific requirements are automatically considered
- **Automation:** Reduces manual coordination and scheduling overhead

This automation is critical for scaling across many sites without proportional increases in coordination effort.

### Finding 5: Dramatic Time Reduction

The impact demonstrates significant efficiency gains:
- **Before:** Weeks of workshops and manual coordination
- **After:** Single day with full oversight, speed, and assurance
- **Value:** Enables rapid scaling and reduces administrative burden

This time reduction is critical for large organizations managing hundreds of sites globally.
